Questions

Good to know

Do learners pay per seat?

No. Aura Learn is built for unlimited learners. You run as many students, trainees or cohorts as you like without per-seat pricing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Course video and material can be accessed offline in the learner app, so field staff and students in low-connectivity areas can keep learning and sync progress when back online.

Can it issue certificates?

Yes. Quizzes and mock tests are auto-graded and certificates are issued on completion, including compliance tracking for regulated training such as certifying field engineers on new equipment.

Is this proven, or a first attempt?

Proven. I have already built and shipped my own learning platform end to end, a government-exam prep Android app on the Play Store with Telegram practice bots and content automation.

Who supports us?

The person who built it. Direct on WhatsApp, and in person in Chennai. No ticket queue, no account-manager layers.
