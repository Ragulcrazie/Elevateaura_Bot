Common questions

Healthcare apps, answered.

What kind of healthcare apps do you build?

Patient apps, clinical and doctor apps, and field-staff apps such as home-care or sample-collection, all in React Native with a Supabase backend.

Do you handle Android and iOS?

Yes. One React Native codebase for both, and we already have an app live on the Play Store.

Can it integrate WhatsApp and ABHA?

Yes. We build WhatsApp Business messaging into apps and design records to support ABHA linking and ABDM alignment.

Do you build the backend too?

Yes. The backend runs on Supabase with Postgres, auth, realtime and storage, so the app and data are one delivery.

How fast can you ship?

We ship focused apps in weeks, founder-led and AI-assisted, with a working build you can test early. See also custom healthcare software.
