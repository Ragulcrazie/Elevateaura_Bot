What healthcare IoT software development means here

Healthcare IoT software development is the set of software layers that take a reading from a device and put it in front of a person who needs it: firmware on the device, a link to a phone or gateway, a cloud pipeline that stores and processes the telemetry, and an app or dashboard that shows it. Elevate Aura writes all four layers, or the ones you are missing.

The device firmware is written in C/C++ or MicroPython to your hardware spec. The link is BLE, Wi-Fi, serial or a wired protocol as the hardware allows. The telemetry pipeline runs on Supabase or Azure. The companion app is built in React Native and the dashboard on the web. Hardware design, manufacturing and regulatory certification stay with your team.

In practice

What a healthcare IoT build looks like.

A home monitoring device reporting to a clinic

Workflow: The device streams readings over BLE to a patient app, which syncs them to the cloud; a clinic dashboard shows each patient's trend.

Result: The clinic sees the reading without the patient visiting.

Hospital equipment reporting its own status

Workflow: Firmware sends usage and fault codes over Wi-Fi to the pipeline; the service dashboard flags devices that need attention.

Result: A fault is known before the ward calls.

An instrument on a wired network

Workflow: The analyser speaks Modbus to a gateway, which forwards telemetry to the cloud and a lab dashboard.

Result: Results and instrument health in one view.

Questions

Healthcare IoT software, answered.

Which layers can you take on?

All four: device firmware, the communication link, the cloud telemetry pipeline and the app or dashboard. If your team already has one layer, we write the others to fit it. See IoT product development for the general service.

Where does the data live?

In a Supabase or Azure project that belongs to you. The pipeline, database and any processing are built in your account, so you keep control of the data and can move or extend it without depending on us.

Do you build the device hardware?

No. Elevate Aura is software-only. The device, sensors, PCB, enclosure and manufacturing are your hardware team's or your contract manufacturer's. We write the firmware to their specification and test it on the boards they provide.

What about regulatory approval of the device?

That remains with your team. We provide design and interface documentation for the software we write, which your team can use in its own submissions, but we do not claim compliance of the device or the software with any regulation.

Can the device work when the connection drops?

Yes. Firmware buffers readings locally and the link layer retries and resyncs when the connection returns, so the record on the cloud has no gaps from a dropped connection. See device-to-cloud telemetry.

How is the engagement scoped?

Fixed scope, agreed on a call about the device, the link, the volume of data and who reads it. You get a plan with the layers, the timeline and the handover, and you own all the code at the end.
