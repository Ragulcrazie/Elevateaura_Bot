Common questions

Device software, answered.

Do you design or build the hardware?

No. This is software-only. We write firmware, device communication and the companion app to your hardware team's specification. We do not design PCBs, manufacture, or do enclosure or mechanical work.

What does medical device software cover?

The embedded firmware on the device, the communication layer to a phone or gateway, the companion mobile or web app, and a cloud telemetry pipeline, tested on your hardware.

Which controllers and languages do you use?

Embedded firmware in C/C++ or MicroPython depending on your controller, with the app in React Native and the cloud on Supabase or Azure.

Do we own the code?

Yes. Code is delivered ready to flash, with documentation your hardware team can maintain.

Is this suitable for regulated devices?

We write clean, documented, testable software and work to your quality and documentation requirements. Regulatory certification remains with the device manufacturer.
