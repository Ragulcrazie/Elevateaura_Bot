Common questions

Embedded software, answered.

What is embedded software development here?

Writing the firmware that runs on your device's controller: core logic, state machines, sensor and peripheral drivers, and power management, to your hardware spec.

Which languages and controllers?

C/C++ or MicroPython depending on your microcontroller. We work to your chosen chip and toolchain rather than forcing ours.

Do you design the hardware?

No. Software-only. We write firmware to your hardware team's specification and do not design PCBs or manufacture.

How is the firmware delivered?

Delivered ready to flash to your chip, tested on your hardware, with documentation your team can maintain.

Can you integrate sensors and peripherals?

Yes. We write drivers and integration for the sensors, actuators and peripherals your device uses. See also firmware development.
