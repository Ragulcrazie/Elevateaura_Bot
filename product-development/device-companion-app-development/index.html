Common questions

Companion apps, answered.

What is a companion app?

The mobile app that a device pairs with. It handles setup and pairing, shows status and readings, controls settings, and can trigger firmware updates.

How does pairing work?

Over BLE or Wi-Fi, with a reliable pairing and reconnect flow matched to your firmware's communication scheme.

Can it trigger OTA updates?

Yes. The app can start over-the-air firmware updates, working with the update mechanism your firmware provides.

Is it one app for many devices?

Yes. The app can manage multiple units, useful for products where a user or technician handles several devices.

Do you build the firmware too?

We can. We build firmware and the app together so they match, or build the app to your existing firmware spec.
