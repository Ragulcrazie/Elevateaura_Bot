Common questions

Medtech product development, answered.

What does medtech product development cover here?

The full software of a medical technology product: firmware, the companion app, the cloud pipeline and any web platform, taken from prototype to a shipped, maintainable product.

Do you build the hardware?

No. Software-only. We work to your hardware team's specification and do not design PCBs, manufacture or do mechanical work.

Can you take us from prototype to product?

Yes. We turn a rough prototype into clean, documented software that ships and can be maintained by your team.

Are you in Chennai?

Yes. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai, so discovery and reviews can happen in person, founder-led.

Who owns the IP?

You do. Code and documentation are delivered to you, and the hardware and certification remain yours. See the full device software service.
