What a UK engagement looks like from here

Elevate Aura is a founder-led studio based in Chennai, India. UK device companies use us for embedded firmware in C/C++ or MicroPython, communication over BLE, Wi-Fi, serial, Modbus or CAN, a React Native companion app, and telemetry on Supabase or Azure.

There is no United Kingdom entity, office or staff behind this, and nothing on this page should be read as implying one. Delivery is remote. The practical consequence is better than it sounds for a UK buyer: Indian working hours sit only a few hours ahead of British time, so a normal UK morning and afternoon are both live for us rather than being a narrow window at the edge of a day.

The engagement is software only. We do not design printed circuit boards, source parts, manufacture, do mechanical or enclosure work, or take on hardware regulatory certification. What we deliver is firmware and software, source, build instructions and documentation your team can use in its own submissions, all owned by you.

Questions

Working with a UK company, answered.

Are you a UK company?

No. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai, India, with no entity, office or staff in the United Kingdom. Engagements with UK clients are delivered remotely, and we state that up front rather than leaving it to be assumed.

How does the time difference work in practice?

Indian hours run a few hours ahead of British time, so our afternoon and evening cover your morning and much of your afternoon. In practice a UK team can raise something at their desk and have an answer the same day rather than the next.

Will you work under our NDA and in our repository?

Yes. Your NDA is signed before any specification or schematic is shared, code can be delivered into the repository you nominate, and any cloud resources can sit in your own account, so access stays yours to grant and withdraw.

Who owns what you write?

Your company does. Source, build and flashing instructions, test notes and documentation are delivered to you, and nothing in the handover requires us to stay involved for your team to keep working on it.

Do you do hardware, CE marking or certification?

No. The engagement is software only: no PCB design, no sourcing or manufacturing, no mechanical or enclosure work, and no hardware regulatory certification of any kind. Those remain with your team, and we supply documentation your team can use in its own submissions.
