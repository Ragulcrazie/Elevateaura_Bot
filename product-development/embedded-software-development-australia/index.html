Why the hours suit an Australian team

Elevate Aura is a founder-led studio in Chennai, India, writing embedded firmware in C/C++ or MicroPython, communication layers over BLE, Wi-Fi, serial, Modbus or CAN, React Native companion apps and telemetry pipelines on Supabase or Azure.

Of the markets we serve remotely, Australia has the easiest rhythm. Indian hours sit a few hours behind eastern Australian time, so your afternoon is our late morning and there is no day lost waiting for a reply. A question raised after your stand-up is usually answered before you finish for the day.

There is no Australian entity, office or staff, and we do not suggest otherwise. Everything is delivered remotely, and the scope is software only: no PCB design, no component sourcing, no manufacturing, no mechanical or enclosure work and no hardware regulatory certification. You receive firmware, source, build instructions and documentation your team can use in its own submissions, and you own all of it.

Questions

Working with an Australian company, answered.

Do you have anyone in Australia?

No. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai, India, with no Australian entity, office or staff, and we would rather say so on the page than have it come up later. Work for Australian clients is delivered remotely.

How many hours of the day do we share?

Indian hours sit a few hours behind eastern Australian time, so a normal Chennai working day covers most of yours. A standing call in your afternoon lands comfortably in our morning, and questions are usually answered the same day.

How do you test firmware on our hardware?

How testing happens on your hardware is agreed when we scope: an evaluation unit sent to Chennai, or a rig in your lab we can reach. Either way the firmware is tested on the real thing before handover, with logs and test notes. Where equipment only exists in your lab, your team runs the measurement and we work to what it reports.

What about our intellectual property?

Your NDA is signed before any specification or schematic is shared. Code can be delivered straight into the repository you nominate, and any cloud resources can sit in your own account, so access stays yours to grant and withdraw. No client is named publicly without written permission.

Do you do hardware or regulatory certification?

No. The engagement is software only. PCB design, component sourcing, manufacturing, mechanical and enclosure work and hardware regulatory certification all stay with your team, and we supply documentation your team can use in its own submissions.
