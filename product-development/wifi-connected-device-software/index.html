What Wi-Fi connected device software has to do

Wi-Fi connected device software is the part of the firmware that gets a product onto a network it has never seen, keeps it there, and carries its data to the cloud. Elevate Aura writes that layer to your hardware spec, together with the companion app screens that walk the owner through the first connection.

Most of the work is in the unglamorous half. Credentials have to be captured and stored without being typed on a device with no keyboard. The radio has to come back after a router reboot, a moved access point or an hour with no internet, without a person power cycling anything. Readings taken while offline have to reach the cloud once the link returns, in order and without duplicates.

We write the provisioning flow, the connection and retry logic, the buffering and sync, and the pairing path in the app. The module, antenna, board and enclosure are your hardware team's work. This is a software-only engagement, and we say so before scoping.

The connectivity problem

It worked on the bench Wi-Fi.

Connectivity complaints are rarely about the radio. They are about what the software does in the five minutes after the radio fails.

Onboarding people give up on

The first connection needs a hotspot, an app screen and clear failure messages, not a manual with an IP address in it.

No reconnect strategy

The device retries in a tight loop, drains the battery or hammers the router, and never backs off.

Data lost while offline

Nothing is buffered, so an outage becomes a hole in the record that no one can explain later.

Credentials handled carelessly

Network details are stored in plain flash and no one has thought about how the device is handed to a new owner.

In practice

Where the connectivity layer earns its place.

A device onboarded by a non-technical owner

Workflow: The app finds the device over its own hotspot, sends the network credentials and confirms the link before the screen moves on.

Result: Setup finishes without a support call.

Equipment on a clinic network

Workflow: The device tolerates a nightly router restart, reconnects on its own and uploads what it buffered in between.

Result: The record has no gaps and nobody visits the site.

A product moving to a new owner

Workflow: A defined reset clears stored credentials and identity, and the device returns to its onboarding state.

Result: Resale or redeployment without a trip back to the factory.

Questions

Wi-Fi device software, answered.

How does a device with no screen join a network?

Usually the device raises its own temporary hotspot and the companion app hands over the network credentials, or the app sends them over BLE. We build whichever fits your hardware, including the app screens, the confirmation step and readable failure messages.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The device keeps working locally, buffers readings in its own storage and retries the link with a backoff so it does not drain power or flood the router. When the link returns the buffered data uploads in order and duplicates are rejected on the cloud side.

Can you build the companion app as well?

Yes. The app is built in React Native for Android and iOS on the same engagement, so pairing, status and control are written to match the firmware. See device companion app development.

Do you choose the Wi-Fi module or antenna?

No. Elevate Aura is software-only. The module, antenna, board layout, certification of the radio and the enclosure are all your hardware team's decisions. We write and test the software on the hardware you provide.

Can the device be updated in the field afterwards?

Yes, and it should be. Once a product is on customer networks, an update path is how you fix a connectivity bug without a site visit. See OTA firmware update development.
