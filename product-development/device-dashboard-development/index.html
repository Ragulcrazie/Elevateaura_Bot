What a device monitoring dashboard is for

A device monitoring dashboard is the web application your team opens to see a fleet of devices at once: which units are reporting, which have gone quiet, what they are measuring and which ones need somebody to act. Elevate Aura builds it on the same engagement as the firmware and the telemetry pipeline, or on top of a backend you already have.

It is built around questions rather than charts. Which devices have not been heard from since yesterday, which ones are outside their normal band, which firmware version is where, and what has this particular unit been doing for the last week. Each of those becomes a view, and the views are what people use.

Roles decide who sees what, because a support engineer, an internal manager and a customer looking at their own units need different things. Alerting is described generically and configured to your thresholds rather than ours. The devices and their hardware stay with your team. This is a software-only engagement.

Silence goes unnoticed

No alerting, or far too much

Either nothing fires, or every minor wobble sends an email and people stop reading them.

Questions

Device dashboards, answered.

Where does the data live?

In your own Supabase or Azure account. The dashboard is deployed there, the source is handed over and your team can maintain or move it. Nothing depends on an Elevate Aura account remaining open.

Can our customers log in and see their own devices?

Yes. Roles scope a customer to their own sites and units with a branded login, while your support and management roles see the whole fleet. Who changed what is recorded.

How do alerts work?

Thresholds are set per reading, and a device that stops reporting for longer than its expected interval raises an alert of its own. Notification goes by email or WhatsApp, and an alert can be acknowledged with a note so two people do not chase the same thing.

Do you supply the devices or any hardware?

No. Elevate Aura is software-only. We do not design PCBs, manufacture, source components, do mechanical work or handle hardware regulatory certification. The devices are yours, and we build the software that reads and presents what they send.

Can it show firmware versions across the fleet?

Yes, and that view is usually one of the most used. It pairs with the update path, so a staged rollout can be watched before a build is released widely. See OTA firmware update development.
