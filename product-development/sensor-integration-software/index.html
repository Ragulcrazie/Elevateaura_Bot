What sensor integration software actually does

Sensor integration software is the layer between a component on your board and a number your product can rely on. Elevate Aura writes the drivers that talk to each sensor, the sampling that decides when readings are taken, the filtering that removes what is not signal, and the logic that acts on the result by driving an actuator or raising a fault.

Calibration is treated as part of the software rather than a note in a spreadsheet. Offsets and scaling factors are stored on the device, applied on every reading, versioned so you know which values a unit is using, and updatable during production or service without a rebuild of the firmware.

The same engagement carries the reading onward: buffered locally, sent over BLE, Wi-Fi, serial, Modbus or CAN, and landed in a pipeline or a dashboard. The sensors themselves, their placement, the board and any hardware certification are your team's work. This is a software-only engagement.

The measurement problem

The sensor is fine. The numbers are not.

Most readings that look wrong are not a component fault. They are a software decision that was never made deliberately.

Sampled in the main loop

Readings are taken between other work, so the interval drifts whenever the device is busy.

Calibration in a spreadsheet

Offsets live outside the device, and nobody can say which values a particular unit was given.

No filtering, or far too much

Either every bit of noise reaches the logic, or the smoothing hides the event you needed to see.

A failed sensor reads zero

A disconnected part is treated as a genuine measurement and the device acts on it.

In practice

Where the sensor layer decides the product.

A device that must hold a value steady

Workflow: Readings arrive on a fixed interval, are filtered for the signal and drive the actuator with defined limits and a safe state.

Result: Control behaviour that is the same on every unit.

Units calibrated at the end of the line

Workflow: A calibration mode writes offsets and scaling to the device, and the values are recorded against the serial number.

Result: Production calibration without a firmware rebuild per unit.

Readings a clinician or engineer will act on

Workflow: Implausible and disconnected readings are flagged rather than forwarded, and the fault is shown as a fault.

Result: Nobody makes a decision on a number the device did not believe.

Questions

Sensor integration software, answered.

Do you choose the sensors?

No. Sensor selection, placement, analogue front end and board design are your hardware team's work. We write the software for the parts you have chosen, and we will tell you plainly if a part's timing or interface makes the software requirement difficult.

How is calibration handled?

Offsets and scaling factors are stored on the device and applied to every reading, with a calibration mode that lets production or service write new values without rebuilding the firmware. Values are versioned so you know what each unit is using.

What happens if a sensor fails?

The driver checks range and plausibility and detects a disconnected part rather than passing a zero along as a measurement. The device moves to a defined safe state, records the fault and reports it, rather than acting on a reading it cannot trust.

Can the readings reach a phone or a dashboard?

Yes. Readings are buffered locally and sent over BLE, Wi-Fi, serial, Modbus or CAN into a pipeline on Supabase or Azure. See device-to-cloud telemetry software.

Do you do any hardware or certification work?

No. Elevate Aura is software-only: no PCB design, no manufacturing or sourcing, no mechanical or enclosure work and no hardware regulatory certification. We deliver firmware with documentation your team can use in its own submissions.
