Common questions

Device apps, answered.

What does a medical device app do?

It pairs with your device over BLE or Wi-Fi, shows live readings, lets the user control settings, stores history and syncs to the cloud or your platform.

Which framework do you build in?

React Native, so one codebase runs on Android and iOS, with native modules where the device link needs them.

Do you handle the device connection?

Yes. We build the BLE or Wi-Fi pairing and the communication layer, matched to your firmware and hardware spec.

Can it publish to the app stores?

Yes. We build for Play Store and App Store release, and our own app is already live on the Play Store.

Do you build the hardware?

No. Software-only. The app is built to your hardware team's specification. See also companion app development.
