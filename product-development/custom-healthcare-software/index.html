Common questions

Custom software, answered.

What is custom healthcare software?

Software built to your exact workflow rather than a fixed product: a web platform, dashboard, portal or automation that fits how your team actually works.

What stack do you use?

React and Next.js on the front, Supabase with Postgres, auth, realtime and storage on the back, with WhatsApp and ABHA-ready integrations.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We build APIs and integrations to connect with your current tools, devices or data where they expose an interface.

Is it only for hospitals?

No. We build for hospitals, clinics, diagnostics, distributors and health startups, whoever needs software shaped to their process.

How fast can you deliver?

Focused platforms ship in weeks, founder-led and AI-assisted, with a working build you can test early. See also healthcare apps.
