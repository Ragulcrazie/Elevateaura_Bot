How a US engagement actually works

Elevate Aura is a founder-led software studio in Chennai, India. For device companies in the United States we write embedded firmware in C/C++ or MicroPython, the communication layer over BLE, Wi-Fi, serial, Modbus or CAN, a companion app, and a cloud telemetry pipeline on Supabase or Azure.

We have no entity, office or staff in the United States and do not claim any. Everything is delivered remotely: calls in an agreed window, work in your repository, and testing on your hardware by whichever route we agree when scoping. Where travel is genuinely needed, it is your hardware team who has the lab, and we work to what they measure and report.

The scope is software only. PCB design, component sourcing, manufacturing, mechanical and enclosure work and hardware regulatory certification are not offered and are not implied. We deliver firmware, source, build instructions and documentation your team can use in its own regulatory submissions, and you own all of it.

What usually goes wrong

Remote engineering fails for boring reasons.

The problems in an offshore engagement are almost never technical, which is why they are worth designing around first.

No overlap, so no conversation

Questions wait a full day for an answer, and a week of work turns out to have been based on a guess.

Scope that drifts quietly

Nobody wrote down what was included, so every disagreement becomes a negotiation at the end.

Ownership left vague

The contract never says plainly who owns the source, and that only matters when you want to leave.

Nobody can test on the hardware

Firmware is written against a simulator and meets your board for the first time after handover.

Questions

Working with a US company, answered.

Do you have a US office or US-based engineers?

No. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai, India, and has no entity, office or staff in the United States. Every engagement with a US company is remote, and we would rather say that plainly than let anyone assume otherwise.

How much of your day overlaps with ours?

India is ahead of every United States time zone, so our late afternoon and evening meet your morning. We agree a fixed live window for each project, usually your morning, and keep a written thread for anything that arrives outside it.

Will you sign our NDA?

Yes, before any specification, schematic or datasheet is shared. Work can sit in the repository and cloud account you nominate, so credentials stay yours to issue and withdraw, and we name no customer publicly without written permission.

Who owns the code?

You do. Source, build and flashing instructions, test notes and documentation are delivered into your repository, and your team or another engineer can maintain the work without depending on us.

Do you handle hardware or FDA work?

No. This is software only: no PCB design, no manufacturing or sourcing, no mechanical or enclosure work, and no hardware regulatory certification. Regulatory strategy and submissions stay with your team, and we provide documentation your team can use in its own submissions.
