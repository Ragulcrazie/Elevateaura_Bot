What an OTA update system has to get right

An over-the-air update system is how firmware reaches a device that is already in a customer's hands. Elevate Aura builds that path end to end: publishing a build, delivering it over Wi-Fi, BLE or a gateway, verifying it before it is trusted, installing it, and recording which version each unit is now running.

The hard part is failure. An update that arrives half way through a power cut, or a build that starts and then stops responding, must leave a working device behind. We design that behaviour generically around your controller and its storage: the running image is not discarded until a new one has proved it boots, and a device that cannot confirm a healthy start returns to what it had.

We also build the human side: the version view across your fleet, staged rollout to a small group first, and an update the owner can trigger from the companion app. Hardware, storage layout capability and the bootloader support on the part are your hardware team's territory. This is a software-only engagement.

Questions

OTA firmware updates, answered.

What stops a failed update from bricking a device?

The new image is verified before it is trusted and the previous working image is kept until the new one has confirmed a healthy start. A device that cannot confirm that start returns to the build it was running. The exact arrangement depends on the storage and bootloader support your controller offers.

How does an update reach the device?

Over whatever link the product already has: Wi-Fi to your backend, BLE from the companion app, or a gateway on the machine's own bus. Downloads resume after an interruption rather than starting again from the beginning.

How do we know which version each unit runs?

Every device reports its version, and that is recorded against the unit with its update history and any failure. The fleet view sits on a dashboard your support team uses. See device dashboard development.

Can an owner start the update themselves?

Yes. The companion app shows that a newer build is available, starts the update, shows progress and reports failures in plain language rather than an error code. See device companion app development.

Do you work on the bootloader or the hardware?

We work in software only. Where your part provides bootloader and storage support for updates we write to it, but the controller choice, memory layout capability, board and hardware route stay with your team. We do not design PCBs or certify anything.
