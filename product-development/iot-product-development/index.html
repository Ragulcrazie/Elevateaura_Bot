Common questions

IoT development, answered.

What does IoT product development cover?

The software of a connected product: firmware, the device-to-cloud communication, a telemetry pipeline that stores and processes data, and a dashboard to see and act on it.

Which cloud do you use?

Supabase or Azure, depending on scale and your preference. We are AZ-104 certified for Azure.

Can devices update over the air?

We build the app-side and cloud-side triggers for over-the-air updates, working with your firmware's update mechanism.

Do you build the hardware?

No. Software-only. We write the firmware, cloud and dashboard to your hardware team's specification.

Can it scale to many devices?

Yes. The telemetry pipeline and dashboard are built to handle a fleet, with per-device identity and alerts. See also device software.
