Common questions

Firmware development, answered.

What does firmware development include?

Peripheral drivers, the communication stack, core device logic, over-the-air update support and clean flashing, written to your controller.

Do you support over-the-air updates?

Yes. We build firmware with an OTA update path so devices can be updated in the field, with the app or cloud triggering it.

Which chips do you work with?

We work to your chosen microcontroller and toolchain, in C/C++ or MicroPython, rather than forcing a platform.

Do you design hardware?

No. Software-only. We write firmware to your hardware team's specification and do not design PCBs or manufacture.

Do we get maintainable code?

Yes. Firmware is delivered documented and ready to flash. See also embedded software.
