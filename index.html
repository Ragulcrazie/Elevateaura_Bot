We believe in rewarding consistent effort. Every week, the top 10 performers on the Global Leaderboard share a prize pool of ₹600. Winning amounts are credited to your Elevate Aura wallet.

We provide comprehensive daily practice for SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS), RRB (NTPC, Group D), Banking (PO, Clerk), and Police exams. All content is updated for the 2024-25 syllabus.

We value your phone's storage and battery. Elevate Aura is built directly into Telegram, allowing you to practice seamlessly without downloading heavy apps or dealing with slow interfaces.
