Questions

Preventive maintenance FAQ

What is preventive maintenance software?

It plans and tracks scheduled maintenance visits for installed equipment, generating PM visits automatically across a contract term and recording what was done on each asset.

Does it work for field technicians?

Yes. Technicians get dispatched to jobs and record work done and spares used on a mobile job card, with customer sign-off captured on site.

Can it track SLA compliance?

Yes. Response and resolution targets are tracked, and breaches are flagged before they affect a renewal.

Can I add AMC contracts, billing and inventory?
