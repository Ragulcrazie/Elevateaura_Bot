In one sentence

What is on this page

Three projects, each built and shipped end to end by the founder, Ragul Sekar, from Chennai. A 12-module operations platform for a medical-equipment distribution business. An e-commerce and distributor platform with 253 product and city landing pages for a medical robotics company. And Elevate Aura's own government-exam-preparation product, live on Google Play.

Nothing else appears here. There are no logos from work that was scoped but not shipped, and no figures that cannot be pointed at. What each page gives you instead is the modules that were built, the stack they were built on, what each part replaced, and how the work ran.

Two of the three became products. Aura Business grew out of the distribution platform, and Aura Learn grew out of the exam-prep app.

The projects

Three builds, three different shapes of problem.

Carna Medicare

Medical-equipment distribution, Chennai. A 12-module operations platform with a field-staff app, manager dashboard, GST billing, payment tracking and service tracking, on React Native, Supabase and WordPress, shipped in 30 days.

Medline Robotics

Medical robotics, Chennai. A storefront, distributor portal, quotations, distributor ordering and UTR payment verification, plus 253 product and city SEO pages across four product lines, on Supabase, JavaScript and Playwright.

Exam preparation app

Elevate Aura's own product. An Android app live on Google Play with Telegram practice bots and content automation, on Flutter, Python and Supabase. The origin of Aura Learn.
