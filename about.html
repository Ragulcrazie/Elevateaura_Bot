Aura Business isn't a generic CRM with a healthcare logo bolted on. It grew out of real operations platforms built for real medical-equipment companies.

Elevate Aura is a Chennai-based studio that builds digital operations platforms for founder-led medical-equipment and healthcare SMBs across India: the businesses still running on WhatsApp and Excel, ready to grow but held back by scattered tools.

Aura Business is the product version of that work. Every workflow a distributor lives on, leads, GST billing, AMC contracts, field service, inventory, procurement, tenders, finance, sits in one connected system, priced so a ₹1 to 10 crore business can actually afford it.

Why it's different

Industry-first, not generic. AMC and CMC contracts, preventive maintenance, spare-parts capture, installed-base registers, tender EMD tracking: the workflows generic CRM and accounting tools simply don't have for this trade.

The AMC leakage engine. The single biggest hidden loss for an equipment distributor is lapsed service contracts. Aura Business puts every contract on a renewal clock and shows the lost revenue in rupees, so you can stop it.

Builder, not agency. You deal directly with the person who builds and supports it, in person in Chennai, on WhatsApp everywhere else. No account-manager layers, no ticket queues.

Proven on real businesses

Carna Medicare

Medical-equipment distribution, Chennai. A 12-module operations platform, field app, manager dashboard, GST billing and service tracking, running live.

Medline Robotics

Medical robotics, Chennai. A full distributor and e-commerce platform with quotations, distributor ordering and payment verification.

The promise

Specific over vague. Numbers, not buzzwords. Software a founder can run their whole business on, set up for you, and supported by the person who built it.
