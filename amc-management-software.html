Questions

AMC software FAQ

What is AMC management software?

It tracks annual and comprehensive maintenance contracts, their value, coverage and expiry, schedules preventive maintenance, and flags renewals before they lapse so you do not lose recurring revenue.

How does it stop AMC revenue leaking?

Every contract sits on a renewal clock and the lapsing-revenue total is shown in rupees, so you chase renewals before the contract expires.

Does it handle CMC contracts and spare parts?

Yes. It tracks AMC and CMC separately, including parts coverage, and captures spares consumed on each service job.

Can I add billing, inventory and CRM too?

Yes. AMC is one of 12 suites. Switch on GST billing, inventory, field service, CRM and more as you need them. See all 87 modules.
