Questions on inventory

Inventory software, answered.

Does inventory update itself from sales and service?

Yes. Stock moves when an order is delivered or a spare is used on a service visit, so your figures match reality instead of a manual register.

Can it track batch and expiry?

Yes. Batch numbers and expiry dates are tracked, with alerts before items expire, which matters for consumables and reagents.

Do you support multiple warehouses and van stock?

Yes. Stock is tracked across warehouses and engineer vans, with transfers between them recorded.

Does it handle purchase orders and vendors?

Yes. Raise purchase orders, track vendors and supplier performance, and receive stock against POs.

Can I import my product list?

Yes. Import your catalogue from Excel with brand, HSN, list price, cost price and reorder levels.
