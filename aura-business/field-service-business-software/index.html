In one sentence

Software for a business whose product is the visit

Some companies sell equipment and service it as a courtesy. Others have no product line at all: the contract is the product, the engineer's time is the cost, and the spare is the margin. Software written for the first kind treats service as a support desk bolted to a sales system. This page is about the second kind.

Aura Business carries a service-led firm end to end. Contracts are set up with their coverage and billing schedule, calls arrive and are logged against the covered asset, an engineer is dispatched by skill and area, the job card records the work and the spares consumed, the SLA clock is visible throughout, and the service invoice is raised from the completed job.

Because contracts and assets sit in the same platform as stock and billing, the two questions a service owner cannot otherwise answer become answerable: which contracts are about to lapse, and which contracts cost more to serve than they bring in.

Why service firms struggle

The problems that only appear when service is the revenue.

A distributor can survive a messy service desk. A service company cannot.

Contracts expire in silence

Renewal dates live in a diary or a spreadsheet tab, and the customer only remembers the contract when something breaks.

Calls arrive everywhere

Phone to one person, WhatsApp to another, email to a shared inbox, so nobody knows the true open-call count.

The SLA is a promise nobody times

Response and resolution windows are written into the contract but measured, if at all, after the customer complains.

Spares vanish into vans

Parts are drawn for a job and never booked against it, so stock is wrong and the job looks more profitable than it was.

Invoicing lags the work

Visits done in the first week are billed in the following month because the paperwork has not come back.

No view of contract profitability

Every contract is assumed to be earning until one customer's call volume quietly eats a year of margin.

In practice

What changes when service runs on one system.

A call comes in at half past four

Workflow: The desk sees the asset, the contract, the SLA window and which engineer is nearest with the right skill, and dispatches without a round of phone calls.

Result: The commitment made to the customer is one the desk can keep.

A contract renewal is proposed before it lapses

Workflow: The renewal engine lists contracts ending next quarter with their call history attached, so the quote reflects how much work the customer actually generates.

Result: Renewals are negotiated from evidence rather than from last year's figure.

A heavy customer is priced properly

Workflow: Calls, engineer visits and spares are all booked against the contract, so the cost of serving it is visible next to what it earns.

Result: A loss-making contract is repriced or declined at renewal.

Billing catches up with the work

Workflow: Job cards close on the phone and chargeable jobs arrive at the billing desk the same day.

Result: Invoices go out while the visit is still fresh in the customer's mind.

Questions

Service business software, answered.

We never sold the equipment we maintain. Does that matter?

No. Assets are registered in the installed-base register with make, model and serial regardless of who supplied them, and contracts are written against those assets. Multi-brand coverage is a normal setup.

How is this different from the field service page for distributors?

The modules overlap, but here contracts and SLAs are the commercial centre rather than an after-sales duty. See field service software for the distributor view.

Can engineers work where there is no signal?

Yes. The mobile job card, photos and customer signature are captured offline in plant rooms and basements, and sync when the phone reconnects. See mobile job cards.

Do we get separate billing for contract and chargeable work?

Yes. Contract billing follows the schedule agreed in the contract, while chargeable calls and spares are invoiced with GST from the closed job card, so the two revenue lines stay distinct.

Can customers raise their own calls?

Yes. Customer ticket submission and the self-service portal let a customer log a call, see its status and view their contracts. See the customer portal.

How quickly can a service team be running on this?

The delivery framework is live in 30 working days where scope allows, with your contracts, assets and spares list migrated in during setup. Support is on WhatsApp, with in-person work in Chennai.
