Questions service teams ask

Field service software, answered.

How do you track field engineers?

Engineers check in by GPS at the customer site, log the job and capture photos and a signature. Managers see live location, visit status and productivity on the web dashboard.

Does the mobile app work offline?

Yes. The field app works offline in low-signal hospitals and basements, then syncs the visit, photos and spares used when the connection returns.

Can we track SLA and escalations?

Yes. Tickets carry priority and SLA timers, with escalation rules so breaches are flagged before they cost you a renewal. This ties into AMC contracts.

Are spare parts captured on the job?

Yes. Spares used on a visit update inventory and feed the service invoice automatically.

Can we prove a visit happened?

Yes. GPS check-in, timestamped photos and a customer signature give you proof of every service visit.
