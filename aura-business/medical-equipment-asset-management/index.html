Questions on assets

Asset management, answered.

Can I track every unit by serial number?

Yes. Each unit is registered by serial number with its customer, installation date, warranty and full service history, so a single lookup shows everything about that asset.

Is warranty linked to each asset?

Yes. Warranty and AMC coverage are linked to the asset, so when a service call comes in you instantly see whether it is covered.

Does it cover installation and acceptance sign-off?

Yes. Installation, commissioning and customer acceptance sign-off are recorded against the asset, which matters for warranty start and disputes.

Can I see an asset's full history?

Yes. An asset 360 view shows purchase, installation, every service visit, spares used, warranty and contract status in one place.

Does it handle transfers and replacement?

Yes. Asset transfers between sites, depreciation and replacement are all tracked through the lifecycle.
