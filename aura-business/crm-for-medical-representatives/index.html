In one sentence

The rep's view, not the manager's report

Most CRMs are bought by a manager who wants visibility and used, reluctantly, by a representative who gets nothing back. The rep types up the day in the evening, the manager reads a report in the morning, and within two months the typing stops.

The Aura Business mobile app is written the other way round. It opens on the day's accounts, each one carrying its own history: what they last bought, what quotation is open, what is outstanding, what sample is still with them, what the service team has been doing there. That is information a rep wants ten seconds before ringing the bell.

The manager's visibility is then a by-product. Attendance is marked in the app, visits are logged with GPS check-in, samples and follow-ups are recorded as they happen, and nobody writes a daily report after dinner.

Why reps abandon CRMs

The six reasons the app stops being opened.

Reps are not resisting technology. They are resisting unpaid data entry.

It only takes, never gives

The app asks for a call report and shows nothing useful before the meeting.

The evening report

A day of visits has to be written up after hours, so it is written from memory or not at all.

Too many fields

Logging one call takes eleven taps, so calls are logged in batches at the end of the week.

No signal, no app

Hospital basements and lab blocks kill the connection exactly where the work happens.

History lives elsewhere

Orders, invoices and service visits sit in the office system, so the rep walks in uninformed.

It feels like surveillance

When the only visible feature is tracking, the app becomes something to work around.

Questions

The rep's CRM, answered.

How long does logging a visit take?

A check-in and a short note, with a dated next action. The design intent is that a call is recorded before the rep leaves the premises, not written up at night, so the fields are kept to what matters.

Does it work in a hospital basement?

Yes. Visits, notes and samples are captured offline and sync when the phone reconnects, which is the difference between a CRM that gets used and one that does not.

Will the rep see other reps' accounts?

Role-based access usually gives a rep their own territory and book, while managers and the owner see across territories. The exact setup is agreed during implementation.

Is this the same app the service team uses?

Yes, with different screens by role. A service engineer sees job cards and dispatch, a rep sees accounts and visits, and both mark attendance in the same place. See the technician app.

Can a rep raise a quotation from the field?

Yes. The catalogue and the GST quotation builder are available, so a price can be issued from the site rather than requested from the office. See quotation software.

Is the app available on Android and iOS?

Yes. Elevate Aura builds mobile apps in React Native and Flutter for both, and the app carries your branding. Setup is remote with support on WhatsApp, and in-person work is in Chennai.
