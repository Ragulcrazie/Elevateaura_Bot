What a ticket is, and how it differs from a service call

A ticket is the customer's side of the conversation. It starts when somebody at the hospital or the dealership has a problem or a question, and it ends when they agree it has been dealt with. Some tickets become field service calls. Many do not: a consumable query, a missing document, a training request, a billing question.

Aura Business runs this as the Ticketing and Helpdesk suite, with Ticket Management, a status workflow, internal notes, SLA timers, customer ticket submission from the portal and CSAT on close. Keeping it separate from dispatch matters, because a support desk that treats every query as an engineer visit either sends engineers unnecessarily or ignores everything that is not a breakdown.

Why support feels chaotic

When every query arrives as a phone call, nothing is ever finished.

Support that lives on calls and personal WhatsApp has no queue, no owner and no record, so the same question is answered three times and the difficult one is answered never.

Queries only reach one person

The customer rings the engineer they happen to know, and the office never hears about the issue.

No status the customer can see

The only way to find out what is happening is to ring again, which is what they do.

Internal discussion in front of the customer

With everything in one WhatsApp group, the team cannot discuss a problem candidly.

Queries that are not breakdowns get lost

A document request or a training query has nowhere to sit, so it sits nowhere.

No idea what people complain about

Satisfaction is guessed

Questions

Helpdesk ticketing, answered.

Can our customers raise tickets themselves?

Yes. Customer ticket submission is part of the self-service portal, so a hospital's biomedical team can raise an issue with attachments at any hour and follow its status without ringing your office.

How is a ticket different from a service call?

A ticket is a customer request of any kind. A service call is an engineer visit. Tickets that need an engineer convert to a service call and go to dispatch. See service dispatch.

Can we control who sees internal notes?

Yes. Internal notes are for your team and are not shown in the customer portal view, while the status and the resolution are. Role-based access controls who can see and change what.

Do tickets have SLA timers?

Yes. Where a contract sets response times, the timer runs on the ticket and at-risk tickets are flagged. See SLA management.

What is captured in CSAT?

A short satisfaction response requested when the ticket closes, stored against the customer and the ticket so support quality can be reviewed by product line and by team member.

Can tickets come from WhatsApp?

Messages received through the WhatsApp Business integration can be logged as tickets so that support arriving by chat joins the same queue rather than sitting on one person's phone.
