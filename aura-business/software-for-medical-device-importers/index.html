What importing changes about distribution software

An importer buys in consignments, not in small top-up orders. Money leaves months before revenue arrives, a shipment exists as committed stock long before it can be sold, and the cost of a unit is the supplier invoice plus freight, duty, clearing and inland movement rather than the invoice alone. Software for medical device importers has to hold that cost build-up, hold the stock that is in transit, and still price a quote today from a number that is honest.

The other half is the channel. You hold authorisation from a principal for particular brands, product lines and territories, and you pass parts of it on when you appoint sub-dealers. Aura Business records the authorisations, gives each sub-dealer its own price list and order portal, and despatches interstate with the tax invoice, delivery challan and e-way bill raised together.

The importer's problems

Cost, timing and a channel you cannot see into.

These are not the problems of a local trader buying from a domestic principal. They come from distance, from consignment sizes and from selling through other people.

Cost known too late

Quotes go out on the supplier invoice price, and the clearing and freight costs arrive weeks later to reveal what the margin really was.

Stock that exists but is not here

A shipment has left the principal and is committed to two customers, but there is nowhere to record it, so sales is told there is nothing.

Authorisation boundaries blurred

A sub-dealer quotes outside its territory or outside the brands it holds, and the principal hears about it before you do.

Serial numbers lost on arrival

Units arrive with serials on a packing list, and by the time they reach a hospital nobody can say which serial went where.

Interstate paperwork rushed

Despatches to another state need the invoice, challan and e-way bill to agree, and they are assembled by hand at the last minute.

No read on sell-through

You know what you sold to sub-dealers. You do not know what they sold on, so the next consignment is ordered blind.

Questions

Importing and distributing, answered.

Can the system hold the full cost of a consignment, not just the supplier invoice?

Yes. Additional charges on a consignment are added at goods receipt so the received stock carries a cost that includes them. Your accountant decides which charges belong in stock cost, and the system records what you configure.

How is stock that has not arrived yet handled?

Keep an in-transit warehouse location. A consignment on a purchase order is visible there, so sales can see what is coming and roughly when, without it being confused with stock on the shelf.

Can each sub-dealer see only the products it is authorised for?

Yes. A distributor account carries its own authorised product list and price list, so the portal shows that dealer only what it may buy, at the prices agreed with it.

Does it raise e-way bills for interstate despatch?

Yes. The despatch produces the tax invoice, delivery challan and e-way bill from the same sales order, and e-invoice IRN and QR where you are required to generate them. See e-way bill software.

Can we track warranty claims back to the principal?

Each unit carries its serial from goods receipt onward, with its warranty record and its service history. A failure can therefore be evidenced with the job card and the serial when you raise it with the principal.

Do you support invoicing outside India?

Billing is GST-native and built for Indian requirements. For despatches or entities outside India, invoicing is configurable and the requirements should be confirmed on a call before scoping.
