In one sentence

What distributor management software has to cover in India

A distributor in India is judged on five things: whether the stock figure is right, whether the order reached the customer, whether the tax invoice is clean, whether the money came back, and whether the after-sales promise was kept. Distributor management software is the system that holds all five against the same customer and the same product, so nobody reconciles them by hand at the end of the month.

Aura Business is built for exactly that shape of business. Inventory with serial and batch detail, sales orders and GST tax invoices with e-invoice and e-way bill, payment tracking and receivables ageing, procurement from your suppliers, and a mobile app for the people who visit customers. Twelve suites, eighty-seven modules, one customer record underneath.

This page is deliberately trade-neutral. Whether you distribute medical equipment, industrial spares, electrical goods or building products, the operational spine is the same, and the trade-specific pages below go into the parts that differ.

The daily friction

Where a growing distribution business starts to leak.

Nothing here is a crisis on its own. Together they are why the owner is still reconciling figures on a Sunday.

Two stock figures, both wrong

The godown register and the billing file disagree, and the salesperson promises material that is already committed.

Orders that live in chat

A purchase order arrives as a photo, gets typed into a quotation, and gets typed again into the invoice.

Billing separated from operations

The invoice is raised in one place and the delivery, warranty and service record sits in another, so nobody can see a customer whole.

Collections chased from memory

Outstanding is a printout, and whoever remembers to call gets paid first.

The field team is unverifiable

Visits, attendance and service calls are reported by message, with no record that survives the week.

Service treated as a cost

Contracts and warranties expire quietly because nobody owns the renewal date.

In practice

Four ordinary days in a distribution office.

A customer asks for material that is reserved

Workflow: The salesperson opens the catalogue and sees committed and free stock per warehouse before promising a delivery date.

Result: Fewer apologies and fewer part despatches.

The accountant wants the month closed

Workflow: Invoices, receipts and credit notes are already in the system, and the Tally export hands the accounting package what it needs.

Result: The month closes from records rather than from memory.

A vehicle is stopped and asked for documents

Workflow: The e-way bill and e-invoice were generated with the tax invoice and are attached to the despatch.

Result: Paperwork travels with the goods, not behind them.

A unit fails eleven months after supply

Workflow: The serial number pulls up the customer, the invoice, the warranty status and the previous visits before an engineer is assigned.

Result: The call is answered with the history already known.

Questions

Distributor management software, answered.

Is this an ERP or a CRM?

It is an operations platform that covers both ends. The sales pipeline and customer record behave like a CRM, while stock, purchasing, billing and service behave like the operational half of an ERP. See the CRM versus ERP comparison.

Does it replace our accounting package?

No. Invoices, receipts and payment data are raised and tracked here, then exported for the accountant to post. The books stay where your chartered accountant works. See Tally export.

We are not in medical equipment. Does it still fit?

Yes. The spine of catalogue, stock, order, invoice, collection and service is trade-neutral. Field names, product attributes and document templates are configured for your trade during setup.

Can we switch on only part of the platform?

Plans are set by module tier, at fifteen modules, forty-seven modules, or all eighty-seven, with unlimited users on each. Most businesses begin with stock, billing and collections and switch on service later.

What happens to our existing Excel data?

Your product list, customers, opening stock and outstanding balances are migrated in during setup, and the dashboard, mobile app and invoices carry your own branding.

We have branches in several cities. Is that supported?

Yes. Multi-warehouse stock, role-based access and territory assignment let each branch see its own operation while the owner dashboard shows the whole business. Setup and support are remote, with in-person work in Chennai.
