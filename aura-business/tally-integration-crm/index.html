In one sentence

An export, not a replacement for your accounting

Almost every distributor in India has an accountant who works in Tally, and no software should ask them to stop. Aura Business does not try to become your books. It runs the operation, raises the GST documents that come out of that operation, and hands the resulting data to the accounting package through the Tally and accounting export module.

The split is deliberate. Quotations, sales orders, tax invoices, delivery challans, receipts, credit notes and the ageing of what customers owe belong with the operation that created them, because they are tied to stock, serial numbers, contracts and service visits. Ledgers, journals, the trial balance and statutory filing belong with your chartered accountant.

What the export removes is the keying that sits between the two. The invoice raised at despatch does not need to be typed again in the evening by somebody reading a printout.

The double-entry tax

What happens when the two systems never speak.

Every business running operations in one place and accounts in another pays this cost quietly, every month.

Every invoice typed twice

Once when it is raised for the customer and again when it is entered into the accounting package.

Figures that do not agree

A correction made in one system and not the other means the month closes with a difference nobody can explain.

Receipts recorded late

Payments collected in the field reach the accounts desk days later, so outstanding is always stale.

Return filing becomes a project

GST returns are compiled from two sources, each of which has to be checked against the other.

The accountant cannot see operations

Questions about a particular invoice mean a phone call to the despatch desk and a search through files.

Software that demands a switch

Platforms that insist on replacing accounting fail because the CA will not move, and the project stalls.

In practice

What the export changes at month end.

The accounts desk stops retyping

Workflow: Invoices raised through the month are exported for the period instead of being entered a second time from printouts.

Result: Evenings at the end of the month look like ordinary evenings.

A field collection reaches the books

Workflow: A cheque collected during a visit is recorded against the invoice on the spot and moves with the period's data.

Result: Outstanding reflects money actually received, not money reported.

Return preparation starts from one source

Workflow: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B preparation reads the same invoices, credit notes and e-invoice records the operation created.

Result: Reconciliation between two systems disappears from the filing routine.

A query about one invoice is answered fast

Workflow: The accountant asks about a document and the operations team opens it with its order, challan, serial numbers and payment history attached.

Result: The answer takes a minute rather than an afternoon.

Questions

Accounting export, answered.

Is this a live two-way integration?

It is an export of billing and payment data from the platform into the accounting package, not a replacement for accounting and not a substitute for your accountant's review. The exact format and frequency are agreed during setup.

Will we have to stop using Tally?

No. That is the point of the export. Your chartered accountant continues in the package they know, while the operation runs here and hands over the documents it produces.

What exactly is exported?

Billing and payment data for the period you select, meaning the tax invoices, credit notes and receipts the platform raised. Ledgers, journals and statutory statements remain the accountant's work.

Does it handle e-invoicing and e-way bills?

Yes. E-invoice with IRN and QR and e-way bill generation are part of the billing suite. See e-invoice software and e-way bills.

Can it prepare our GST returns?

There is a GST returns preparation view for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B built from the same documents. Filing and the treatment of any particular transaction remain matters for your accountant.

What about supplier bills and payables?

Purchase orders, goods receipt and the payables ledger sit in the procurement suite, so purchases are recorded against stock as well. See purchase orders and GRN.
