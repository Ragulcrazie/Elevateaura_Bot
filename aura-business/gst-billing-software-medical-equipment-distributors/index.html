What GST billing software for a medical equipment distributor must do

GST billing software for a medical equipment distributor has to hold the HSN code and tax rate on every catalogue item, apply CGST and SGST or IGST depending on where the customer is, number invoices without gaps, generate the e-invoice IRN and QR code where your turnover requires it, raise e-way bills for goods in transit, issue delivery challans and credit notes correctly, and hand clean data to the person filing GSTR-1 and 3B. Aura Business does all of that inside the Quotation, Orders and GST Billing suite and the Finance suite.

What makes it distributor billing rather than generic billing is where the invoice comes from. It is raised from a sales order that came from a quotation, or from a closed service job, so the lines, the customer's GSTIN and the delivery address are already right before anyone opens the invoice screen.

Where GST goes wrong

The mistakes your CA finds in March.

Most billing errors in a distributor are not tax knowledge. They are re-typing, wrong state codes and documents raised in the wrong order.

HSN typed per invoice

The same probe carries three different HSN codes across the year because each rep typed it from memory.

IGST on an intra-state sale

A hospital's billing address is in your state but the ship-to is not, and the tax split is wrong.

Gaps in the invoice series

A cancelled invoice was deleted rather than cancelled, and the sequence now has a hole to explain.

E-way bill after the truck left

The consignment moved before the e-way bill was generated because it lived in a separate portal login.

Returns without credit notes

A returned analyser was adjusted in the next invoice instead of through a credit note, and the return does not reconcile.

GSTR data rebuilt by hand

The accountant re-enters every invoice into the return because the billing export does not match.

Questions

GST billing for distributors, answered.

Does it generate e-invoices with IRN and QR?

Yes. The E-Invoice module generates the IRN and QR code for tax invoices where your business is required to e-invoice, and prints them on the invoice PDF. Registration with the e-invoice system is done by you during setup.

How is CGST, SGST or IGST decided?

By place of supply. The customer's GSTIN and delivery address on the order determine whether the invoice carries CGST and SGST for intra-state supply or IGST for interstate supply, and the split is applied per line from the HSN rate.

What does gapless numbering mean in practice?

Invoice numbers run in an unbroken series per financial year. An invoice that must be withdrawn is cancelled with a reason and stays in the series, rather than being deleted, so the audit trail has no holes.

Can we raise e-way bills from the invoice?

Yes. The e-way bill is generated from the invoice or delivery challan with transporter and vehicle details, so consignments moving between states or above the threshold have the document before dispatch.

Does it replace Tally?

It replaces the invoicing, receivables and GST document side. Your accountant can keep the books in Tally or any accounting package using the export, and the GSTR-1 and 3B prep gives them the return data. See billing software for the wider suite.

Are service and AMC invoices in the same series?

Service invoices for jobs and contract invoices for AMC or CMC are raised from the service and contracts modules with the same GST rules. Whether they share a series with product invoices is configured to suit your accountant. See service management software.
