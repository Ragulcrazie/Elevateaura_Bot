Questions on contracts

AMC and CMC software, answered.

What is the difference between AMC and CMC here?

AMC typically covers service visits and labour, while CMC also covers spare parts. The software handles both, plus warranty and extended warranty, on the same contract engine.

How does it stop renewal revenue leaking?

Every contract sits on a renewal clock. As expiry approaches, the lapsing revenue is surfaced in rupees with alerts and auto-quotations, so you chase the renewal before it is gone.

Does it schedule preventive maintenance?

Yes. PM schedules, checklists and calibration reminders are generated from each contract, and executed through field service.

Can it show contract profitability?

Yes. Costs of service visits and spares are tracked against contract value so you can see which AMCs make money and which do not.

Does it handle multi-site contracts?

Yes. A single contract can cover multiple sites and many assets, with consolidated billing and renewal tracking.
