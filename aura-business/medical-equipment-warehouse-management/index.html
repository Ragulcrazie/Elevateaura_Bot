In one sentence

What warehouse management adds to plain stock counting

Plain inventory tells you how many of an item you own. Warehouse management tells you where each one is, how it got there and who moved it. That distinction becomes urgent the moment you have a main godown, a branch, and engineers carrying parts in their vehicles, because the total on hand is then spread across locations that can each run out independently.

Aura Business handles this in the Inventory and Warehouse suite. Multi-Warehouse gives each location its own stock, Stock Movements and Ledger records every receipt, issue, transfer and adjustment with a reason and a user, and transfers move goods between locations as documented events rather than as an edited number.

Where stock goes missing

Stock does not usually disappear. It stops being findable.

A single stock figure across several locations is worse than no figure, because people trust it and then act on it.

One number for three godowns

The system says twelve are available; four are at the branch, six are in vans and only two are where the order is being picked.

Van stock is invisible

Engineers carry parts worth a serious amount and none of it appears anywhere until it is used or lost.

Transfers by phone call

Goods move between branches on a verbal request and neither side's records change.

Adjustments without reasons

Somebody corrects a quantity to match the shelf and there is no record of what was corrected or why.

Physical verification takes a week

Counting is done on paper, differences are argued about, and the book figure is overwritten rather than explained.

Reorder alerts fire on the wrong total

A location is empty while the company-wide figure looks healthy, so nothing is reordered until an order fails.

In practice

Four warehouse problems that stop repeating.

An order is picked from the right place

Workflow: The sales order shows availability per location, so the picker knows the units are at the branch and raises a transfer rather than promising a delivery date.

Result: No order confirmed against stock that is elsewhere.

Van stock is accounted for

Workflow: Parts issued to an engineer sit in their van location until a job card consumes them or a transfer returns them.

Result: Every part is somewhere, on the record.

A transfer cannot be denied

Workflow: Goods sent between branches leave one location on despatch and only enter the other on acknowledgement, with both entries in the ledger.

Result: In-transit stock is visible instead of disputed.

The annual count produces answers

Workflow: Counts are entered per location and differences are recorded as adjustments with reasons rather than silently overwritten.

Result: You learn where the shrinkage happens.

Questions

Warehouse management, answered.

How many warehouses can we set up?

The multi-warehouse module is not limited to a fixed number. Godowns, branch stores, the service spares store and each engineer's van can all be modelled as locations, and stock is held and reported against each.

Is engineer van stock really separate?

Yes. Parts issued to an engineer move into their location and stay there until a job card consumes them or they are transferred back, so the shelf figure and the road figure are never mixed.

Can we track serial numbers by location?

Yes. Serial and unit-level stock means a specific machine is known to be in a specific location, which also feeds the installed-base register once it is sold. See installed base.

What happens during physical verification?

Counts are entered per location and compared with the book figure. Differences are posted as adjustments with a reason and a user against them, so the ledger explains the correction rather than hiding it.

Do reorder alerts work per warehouse?

Yes, which is the point of them. A branch can be below its reorder level while the company total looks comfortable, and the alert fires for that location. See purchase orders and GRN.

How does stock leave for a customer?

On a delivery challan, which reduces the despatching location and can then be converted to a tax invoice. See delivery challan software.
