In one sentence

What medical distributor analytics should show an owner

Medical distributor analytics is the set of numbers an owner needs to run sales, service and money at the same time: the pipeline and what will close this month, what is outstanding and how old it is, which AMC and CMC contracts lapse soon and what they are worth, how many service calls are open and which are breaching SLA, and which product lines and territories actually make money. Aura Business shows these on the Owner and CEO Dashboard and lets you go deeper through the reports engine.

The difference from a stitched-together report is that every number on the dashboard is the live total of transactions already in the system: quotes, invoices, receipts, contracts and job cards. Nobody prepares the dashboard. It is simply the state of the business at the moment you open it.

In practice

Four decisions the dashboard makes easier.

Which customer to call about money

Workflow: Receivables ageing shows two hospital groups with invoices past ninety days; the owner calls those two, not the whole list.

Result: Collection effort goes where the risk is.

Which contracts to save this month

Workflow: The AMC at-risk tile shows contracts lapsing in thirty days with their rupee value; the owner reviews the top five with the service head.

Result: Renewal revenue is protected before it becomes leakage.

Which product line to push

Workflow: P&L by product line shows the high-turnover brand has the lowest margin after service cost, and a quieter line has the highest.

Result: Sales targets change to favour margin, not just volume.

Whether service is slipping

Workflow: Open calls, SLA breaches and repeat visits per engineer are on one view, reviewed weekly.

Result: Problems are seen before the hospital escalates them.

Questions

Distributor dashboard and analytics, answered.

What is on the owner dashboard by default?

Pipeline value by stage, collections and outstanding, AMC and CMC revenue at risk in rupees, open service calls with SLA status, and cashflow. Tiles are configured to your priorities during setup, and each drills down to the underlying records.

Can reports be sent to me automatically?

Reports from the reports engine can be delivered through the notifications hub and WhatsApp Business integration. The exact schedule and recipients are set up for your business during scoping, so confirm what you need on the call.

How is P&L by product line calculated?

Revenue from invoices is grouped by product line or territory and set against cost of goods from purchases and the cost of service closed against contracts, so a brand's true margin includes what it costs you to support it. See medical equipment management software.

What does receivables ageing show?

Outstanding invoices by customer in age buckets, with the contact and the last reminder sent, so collections calls go to the oldest and largest first. Payment reminders can go out on WhatsApp from the payment tracker.

What service analytics are included?

Open and closed calls by period, response and resolution against SLA, breaches, repeat calls per asset, spares consumed per job, contract profitability and CSAT collected on ticket close. See service management software.

Can I ask questions instead of building reports?

Yes. The AI assistant in the Analytics suite answers plain-language questions over your own data, such as which customers are past ninety days or which contracts lapse next month, and points to the records behind the answer.
