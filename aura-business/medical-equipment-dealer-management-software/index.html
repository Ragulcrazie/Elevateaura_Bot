What medical equipment dealer management software runs

Medical equipment dealer management software runs the two-sided business a dealer actually has: principals above, who authorise you for certain brands and territories and send price lists, and customers below, who may be hospitals buying directly or sub-dealers ordering in bulk. Aura Business handles both sides with brand and authorisation records, supplier price lists, a distributor order portal, UTR-based payment confirmation and an order tracking page.

A dealer carrying four brands has four price lists, four warranty policies, four sets of authorisation letters and four purchase relationships, and one sales team quoting across all of them. The point of the software is that the sales team, the tender desk and the accounts team all work from the same brand-aware catalogue.

The multi-brand problem

Every principal has its own rules, and your team has one head.

The dealership grew brand by brand. The tools stayed as one spreadsheet per brand and a WhatsApp group per sub-dealer.

In practice

A week at a four-brand dealership.

A price revision lands on Monday

Workflow: The principal's new price list is loaded as the supplier price list; quotes from that afternoon use the new cost and the margin rule.

Result: No quote goes out on last quarter's prices.

A sub-dealer orders at midnight

Workflow: The sub-dealer places an order on the portal from the list authorised for them, uploads the UTR the next morning, and the order moves to fulfilment.

Result: No phone call, no re-keying, no unmatched payment.

An authorisation question is settled

Workflow: A rep checks the brand record before quoting a district and sees the authorisation ends at the state border.

Result: No quote where you cannot supply.

The owner sees which brand pays

Workflow: P&L by product line shows one brand carries the volume while another carries the margin.

Result: The next principal negotiation is based on numbers.

Questions

Dealer management software, answered.

Can we hold different price lists for different sub-dealers?

Yes. Each distributor or sub-dealer account has its own agreed prices and the products it is authorised to buy, so the portal shows them only what applies to them.

How does UTR payment confirmation work?

The sub-dealer pays by bank transfer and enters the UTR reference against the order in the portal. Your accounts team confirms it against the bank credit and the order moves to fulfilment, with the receipt recorded against the account.

Do principals get any access?

Access is role-based, so you decide. Some dealers share nothing; others give a principal's regional manager a read-only view of installed base or sell-through for their brand. This is agreed at setup.

Does it handle stock by brand across warehouses?

Yes. Multi-warehouse inventory, serial-level stock and brand records mean you can see what you hold of each principal's products at each location. See inventory software.

Is there a real example of a distributor portal built by Elevate Aura?

Yes. Medline Robotics in Chennai runs a distributor portal with distributor ordering, quotations and UTR payment verification built by Elevate Aura. See the case study.

Can a hospital customer use the same portal?

Hospitals use the customer self-service portal, which shows their own orders, invoices, equipment and service tickets, while sub-dealers use the distributor order portal. Both share the branded login and docs vault. See distributor portal development.
