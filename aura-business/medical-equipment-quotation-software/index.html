What medical equipment quotation software does

Medical equipment quotation software produces a priced, GST-correct offer to a hospital from your product catalogue, tracks each revision the purchase committee asks for, and converts the accepted version into a sales order and a tax invoice. In Aura Business the GST Quotation Builder is the first module of the Quotation, Orders and GST Billing suite, and it reads the same catalogue, customer master and rate contracts as the rest of the platform.

Hospital buying is rarely one quote and one order. A committee asks for a revised configuration, the biomedical department wants a different accessory, purchase wants a proforma for advance payment, and a tender needs the same items in a prescribed format. The builder handles each of those as a version or a document type on the same enquiry, so the trail from first offer to final invoice is complete.

Why quotes go wrong

The quote is where a distributor first loses margin.

It is usually typed in a word processor, priced from memory, and revised by saving a new file. By the fourth revision nobody is sure which one the hospital accepted.

Prices from memory

The rep quotes last year's rate, or a rate agreed for a different hospital, and the discount is discovered at invoicing.

GST worked out by hand

HSN codes and CGST, SGST or IGST are added at the end, and a wrong tax line makes the committee send it back.

Versions in file names

Quote_final_v3_revised.docx is not a version history, and the accepted version cannot be proved.

Re-typed into the invoice

The accepted quote is keyed again into accounting software, and the two do not match.

Proforma and tender formats separate

A proforma for advance and a tender schedule are made from scratch, with different totals from the quote.

Follow-ups forgotten

A quote sent to a committee in April is never chased because it lives in someone's sent folder.

In practice

Four quoting situations, handled.

A purchase committee asks for three revisions

Workflow: The rep changes the configuration twice and the payment terms once; each is saved as a version on the same quote with a date.

Result: The accepted version is on record and the invoice matches it exactly.

A proforma is needed for advance

Workflow: The hospital's purchase department wants a proforma to release advance; it is generated from the accepted quote with the same totals.

Result: No second document typed by hand, no mismatch.

A quote crosses a state border

Workflow: A lab in another state is quoted; the builder applies IGST instead of CGST and SGST because of the customer's address.

Result: The tax lines are right the first time.

A tender needs the same items in its own format

Workflow: The lines from the quotation are reused for the tender schedule, and the tender is tracked with its EMD and deadline.

Result: One price list, two documents, one record.

Questions

Quotation software, answered.

Does the quotation carry GST correctly?

Yes. Each catalogue item carries its HSN code and tax rate, and the builder applies CGST and SGST for customers in your state or IGST for customers in another state, based on the customer address. The same lines flow to the tax invoice.

What happens when a hospital asks for a revised quote?

You edit the quote and it is saved as a new version. Earlier versions stay on the record with their dates, so when the committee accepts one you can convert exactly that version to an order.

Can I issue a proforma invoice before supply?

Yes. A proforma is generated from the accepted quotation for advance payment or import formalities, and the final tax invoice is raised separately with its own gapless number when goods are supplied.

How do tender quotes work?

The lines of a quotation can be reused in the tender format, and the tender itself is tracked in the Tenders suite with its EMD and submission deadline. See tender management software.

Can quotes come in from my website?

Yes. The quote-request inbound module captures enquiries from your website or portal straight into the pipeline, where a rep prices them in the builder.

Does it replace my accounting software?

It produces the GST invoice and tracks payments, and a Tally or accounting export is included so your accountant's books stay in step. See GST billing software.
