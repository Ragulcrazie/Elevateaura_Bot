In one sentence

What equipment warranty management software does

Equipment warranty management software records the warranty on every unit you have installed, by serial number and with a start date that matches what the manufacturer will honour, and uses that record to decide on every service call whether the visit and the parts are free to the customer, chargeable, or covered by a contract. In Aura Business this is the Warranty Tracking module, sitting on the installed-base register next to AMC and CMC contracts.

Warranty is the cover a dealer most often gives away twice: once to the hospital, when a visit outside warranty is not charged because nobody checked, and once to itself, when a part replaced under warranty is never claimed back from the principal. The module exists to stop both.

Where warranty costs you

Warranty is a promise with a date on it, and the date is usually wrong.

The invoice date is known. The installation date, the extended cover the customer bought, and the parts already replaced under claim are not.

Cover assumed from the invoice

The hospital insists the year runs from installation; the principal insists it runs from shipment; your engineer is on site with neither date.

Extended warranty in a drawer

The customer paid for two extra years and the certificate is in a file, so the call is billed and the argument follows.

Warranty, AMC and CMC confused

The same ventilator may be under principal warranty for parts and your AMC for labour, and nobody can say who covers what on this call.

Claims never raised

The engineer fits a board from your stock, the customer pays nothing, and the principal is never asked to replace it.

Expiry unseen

Warranty ends quietly; the first you hear is a breakdown call on an uncovered machine, and the AMC is now sold under pressure.

In practice

Where the warranty record settles the question.

A call on a machine with three kinds of cover

Workflow: A monitor is under principal warranty for parts, your AMC for labour, and a hospital-wide CMC for consumables; the ticket shows all three and the job card allocates each line accordingly.

Result: The customer is billed for nothing they already paid for, and you give away nothing you should charge.

A board replaced under warranty is claimed

Workflow: The failed board's serial, the replacement serial and the job card are logged; the claim to the principal is raised with that evidence and the credit tracked in payables.

Result: Your stock is replenished at the principal's cost, not yours.

Extended warranty is honoured without a search

Workflow: The customer bought two extra years; the asset shows the extended term and the call is closed as covered.

Result: No dispute and no digging through files.

Warranty expiry becomes an AMC

Workflow: Sixty analysers at a lab chain leave warranty in the next quarter; the list goes to the rep with contacts and the renewal engine tracks each quote.

Result: The AMC is sold before the first uncovered breakdown.

Questions

Warranty management, answered.

What is the difference between warranty, AMC and CMC on a service call?

Warranty is the manufacturer's or your own promise for a fixed term after sale, usually covering defects. An AMC is a paid annual contract, typically for scheduled visits and labour. A CMC is comprehensive and includes spares. The ticket shows which applies so the job card allocates each line correctly. See AMC vs CMC.

Can warranty start from the installation date?

Yes. Each asset's warranty can start from installation, customer acceptance or invoice date, whichever your principal honours, and the term can differ by brand and product line.

How are warranty claims to the principal handled?

Parts replaced under warranty are recorded on the job card with the failed and replacement serials, tied to the asset and to the GRN the part came from. That record is the evidence for your claim to the principal; the claim itself is raised with the principal in their process, and the credit received is tracked in payables.

Does extended warranty work like a contract?

Extended warranty sold by you is recorded on the asset with its own dates and terms, invoiced through GST billing, and treated as cover on service calls in the same way as a contract. See service contract management software.

How do expiry alerts reach the sales team?

The installed-base register produces lists of serials whose warranty ends in a chosen window, grouped by customer with contacts. These appear on the desk and the owner dashboard, and the renewal engine tracks the AMC or extended-warranty quote for each.

Can we track warranty on equipment we did not sell?

Yes. Assets you maintain but did not supply can be created in the register with whatever warranty information the customer provides, so the coverage check works on third-party equipment too.
