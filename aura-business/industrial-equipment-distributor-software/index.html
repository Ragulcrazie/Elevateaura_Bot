What changes when the customer is a factory

An industrial equipment distributor sells into plants, and a plant does not measure a fault in inconvenience. It measures it in stopped production. That single fact reorders the software requirement: the asset has to be findable by plant and by line, the call has to be timestamped and answered against a promise, the engineer has to arrive with the right part, and the whole thing has to be evidenced afterwards because the customer's maintenance head reports on it.

Around the breakdown sits ordinary distribution work: quotations to a purchase department, machines supplied and commissioned, annual contracts, planned maintenance scheduled into the plant's shutdown windows, and a spares business that is often larger than the machine business. Aura Business holds all of it on one platform with a mobile app for engineers who work in places with no network.

The factory reality

The call comes at six in the morning and the line is stopped.

Industrial customers are unforgiving about response and indifferent to excuses about paperwork.

Nobody knows which machine

The caller says a pump in the packing hall. Without a plant-wise register, identifying the machine is the first half hour.

The engineer arrives without the part

The fault could have been guessed from the service history, but the history is in an old job book.

Planned visits never planned

The plant has shutdown windows twice a year, and your preventive visits are not scheduled against them.

Site access wastes a morning

Gate entry, safety induction and work permits are arranged on arrival rather than before the visit.

Spares stock in the wrong place

A critical part sits in a warehouse eight hours away from the plant most likely to need it.

Multi-plant customers fragmented

One group with four plants is handled as four unrelated customers, so nobody sees the whole relationship.

In practice

Four moments on a factory account.

The machine is identified in a minute

Workflow: The caller names the plant and the line; the asset register returns the machine, its serial, its contract and its last three jobs.

Result: The engineer leaves knowing the likely fault.

Preventive work lands in the shutdown

Workflow: Schedules for that plant are grouped and planned into the shutdown window agreed with the maintenance head.

Result: Planned work without stopping production twice.

A critical spare sits near the plant

Workflow: Parts consumption history shows which items fail on that model, and stock is held at the warehouse nearest those plants.

Result: Hours saved on the response that matters most.

A group is seen as a group

Workflow: Four plants sit under one account with a multi-site contract, and reporting rolls up across them.

Result: A commercial conversation with the head office, based on the whole picture.

Questions

Industrial distribution and service, answered.

Can assets be organised by plant and production line?

Yes. Each asset carries its customer, site and location detail, so a group with several plants can be navigated from the group down to a specific machine on a specific line, with its contract and service history attached.

Does maintenance scheduling support running hours rather than dates?

Yes. Preventive maintenance can be scheduled on a calendar basis or on meter and usage readings, which suits machines whose service interval depends on hours run rather than months elapsed.

Will the engineer app work inside a plant with no signal?

Yes. The mobile job card works offline: the engineer records the fault, parts fitted, photographs and the customer signature, and the job syncs when the device regains a connection.

Can one contract cover several plants of the same customer?

Yes. Multi-site contracts hold assets across a group's locations under one agreement, while each site keeps its own visit schedule and service history. See service contract management.

Is this only for medical equipment businesses?

No. The platform is built for any trade that sells, installs and services, and the same modules serve industrial, electrical and building products distributors. The medical work is simply where most of the shipped experience is.

Can we see whether a service contract is worth renewing?

Contract profitability compares what a contract invoiced against the visits and parts consumed under it, so a contract that costs more to honour than it earns is visible before you quote the renewal.
