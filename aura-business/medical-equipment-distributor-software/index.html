Common questions

Distributor software, answered.

Can dealers place and track orders themselves?

Yes. A dealer and distributor portal lets partners see the catalogue, place orders and track status, which cuts the back-and-forth on WhatsApp and email.

Does it track tenders?

Yes. Tender opportunities are logged and followed, and a tender-alert feature helps you spot relevant government tenders so you do not miss submissions.

Can it manage brand authorizations?

Yes. Manufacturer authorizations and their expiry are tracked, so you know which brands you are authorized to sell and when to renew.

Does it handle a multi-brand catalogue?

Yes. Products across many brands sit in one catalogue with pricing, HSN and stock, feeding quotes, orders and the dealer portal.

Can it include an online store?

Yes. We have built storefronts and distributor portals with quote-to-order and payment, as we did for Medline Robotics.
