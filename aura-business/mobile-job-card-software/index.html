In one sentence

What an engineer fills in on the job card

The mobile job card is the record of a single visit, completed on the engineer's phone before they leave the site. It holds what the complaint was, what was actually done, any readings or checklist items the equipment requires, which spares came out of the van, photographs of the machine before and after, and the customer's signature confirming the work.

In Aura Business this is the Mobile Job Card module in the Field Service suite. The reason it matters commercially is simple: the spares recorded on it drop your stock, the labour and parts on it become a service invoice when the asset is out of cover, and the signature on it is what you produce when a customer disputes the visit months later.

Questions

Mobile job cards, answered.

What happens if the engineer has no internet on site?

The job card is completed offline on the device, including photographs and the signature, and syncs to the office when the phone reconnects. This is normal in hospital basements and shielded imaging rooms.

Does recording a spare part reduce stock?

Yes. Spares picked on the job card come out of the engineer's van stock when the job is closed and appear in the stock movements ledger, so consumption and reordering are based on real usage.

Can the job card include a checklist?

Yes. Preventive maintenance visits carry the checklist for that equipment, and calibration entries and readings are recorded against the serial number. See preventive maintenance.

Is the customer signature legally useful?

It is evidence of the visit and of the work accepted, captured with the time and the site. We do not offer legal advice on its evidentiary weight; confirm that with your own adviser if it matters to a contract.

Can the customer get a copy of the report?

Yes. A service report is generated from the completed card with the work done, spares and signature, and can be shared with the customer from the platform.

Which phones does the app run on?

The field app is built on React Native and runs on Android and iOS, with GPS and offline mode. See the service app for how it is built.
