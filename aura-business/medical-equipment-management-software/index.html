Does this replace all my separate tools?

Yes, that is the point. Sales, service, AMC, inventory, billing and staff management live in one platform instead of separate apps, spreadsheets and chats that do not talk to each other.

Can I see the whole business in one place?

Yes. A live owner dashboard shows pipeline, collections, AMC at risk, open service calls and field activity together, with role-based access.

Yes. Multi-branch operations, territory mapping and consolidated reporting are supported, with unlimited users on every plan.

Role-based access and permissions decide who can see and do what, with a full audit log of every action.
