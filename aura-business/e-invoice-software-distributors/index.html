What e-invoicing means for a distributor

E-invoicing does not mean sending an invoice by email. It means that the invoice you raise is reported to the government's invoice registration system, which returns an Invoice Reference Number, known as the IRN, and a QR code. Those are then printed on the invoice you give the customer. The invoice is still your invoice; it now carries a registration that anyone can verify.

Whether your business has to do this depends on rules that apply from a turnover threshold and that have changed more than once. We do not publish the figure, because the only useful answer is the current one for your business. Confirm with your accountant whether e-invoicing applies to you and from when. What Aura Business provides is the E-Invoice module in the Quotation, Orders and GST Billing suite, so that when it does apply, it is part of raising an invoice rather than a second job in another window.

Where e-invoicing hurts

The pain is rarely the rule. It is the second data entry.

Most distributors who struggle with e-invoicing are not confused about the requirement. They are re-keying the same invoice into a portal and hoping the two copies match.

Invoice typed twice

Once in your billing system and again into a portal, with every chance of a difference between them.

IRN not on the printed copy

The registration is obtained but the invoice given to the customer does not carry the IRN or QR code.

Rejections nobody understands

A registration fails on a formatting or master-data issue and the despatch waits while somebody investigates.

Cancellations handled badly

A registered invoice is deleted in the billing system, leaving the two records disagreeing.

E-way bill raised separately

The same consignment is entered again for the e-way bill with different details.

Return data does not tie up

The accountant reconciles registered invoices against your books by hand every month.

Questions

E-invoicing for distributors, answered.

What is the IRN?

The Invoice Reference Number is the identifier returned when an invoice is registered with the government's invoice registration system. It comes back with a QR code, and both are printed on the invoice you give the customer.

Does e-invoicing apply to our business?

It applies from a turnover threshold that has changed over time. We do not publish the figure because only the current rule for your turnover matters. Confirm with your accountant whether you are covered and from which date.

Is the e-invoice a different invoice?

No. It is the same tax invoice, registered. You raise it in the normal way from the order, and the registration adds the IRN and QR code to it. Numbering, GST calculation and the PDF are unchanged.

What happens if registration fails?

The invoice is flagged with the reason, which is usually a master-data issue such as a GSTIN or an HSN code. It is corrected and resubmitted, and the despatch is held rather than going out with an unregistered document.

Can a registered invoice be cancelled?

Cancellation is possible within the window the system allows; after that a credit note is the correct route. The platform keeps the invoice in the series either way. Ask your accountant which applies in your case.

Does it also do the e-way bill?

Yes, from the same record, so the consignment details are not entered twice. See e-way bill software and GST billing.
