Common questions

Healthcare CRM, answered.

Who is this healthcare CRM for?

It is for healthcare and medical businesses that sell to or service hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres, such as equipment distributors, device firms and diagnostics suppliers. It manages your business customers, not patient records.

Can it manage doctors and referral relationships?

Yes. Hospitals, doctors and dealers are all tracked with their contacts, history and follow-ups, so relationships are managed, not forgotten.

Does it track account health and credit?

Yes. Each account carries credit terms and a health status, so you see which relationships are strong and which are at risk.

Is it different from a hospital management system?

Yes. This CRM runs the business selling to healthcare. For hospitals and clinics managing patients, see our separate HIMS and AuraPACS product.

Can field and sales teams use it?

Yes. Sales and service staff work from a mobile app, and managers see the whole pipeline and account activity live.
