In one sentence

What a hospital sales CRM has to handle

A hospital sales CRM tracks a sale that has many people and few decisions: a doctor who wants the equipment, a biomedical engineer who evaluates it, a purchase officer who runs the process, a committee that approves it, and often a tender or rate contract that governs the price. Aura Business holds all of them on one hospital account, with the visits, demo loans, quotation versions and follow-ups that happen between the first conversation and the purchase order.

The sale can take a quarter or a year. What the CRM must do is make sure the account does not go quiet because a rep changed, a committee meeting was postponed, or a quote revision was sent from a personal email. Every touch is on the account, and every account has a next date.

Why hospital deals stall

The sale is not lost. It is forgotten.

Hospital purchasing is slow by design. A generic CRM built for quick B2B deals treats a nine-month cycle as a dead lead.

Contacts in the rep's phone

The consultant, the biomedical head and the purchase officer are known to one rep, and known to nobody when the rep moves on.

Committee dates missed

The purchase committee meets monthly; the quote arrives a day late and waits another month.

Demo units in limbo

An ultrasound sits in a department for evaluation and nobody has asked for a decision or the unit back.

Quote revisions untracked

Three revisions later the committee approves a version the rep cannot find.

Tender and direct sale confused

The same hospital buys some items by tender and others directly, and the pipeline does not distinguish.

Follow-ups by memory

'I'll call them next month' is the entire follow-up plan for a deal worth lakhs.

In practice

How the CRM keeps a slow deal alive.

A committee meeting is not missed

Workflow: The account records the committee's monthly date; the follow-up reminder fires a week before with the latest quote version attached.

Result: The proposal is on the table when the decision is made.

A demo turns into a decision

Workflow: The demo unit's return date passes; the rep is prompted to collect an evaluation outcome from the department head.

Result: Either an order or the unit back, within the month.

A rep leaves, the account stays

Workflow: Contacts, visit notes, quote versions and the next action are on the account; the new rep picks it up from the activity feed.

Result: No relationship walks out with the resignation.

A rate contract is used correctly

Workflow: A medical college has a rate contract for consumables; a new quote for those items pulls the contract price and flags the end date.

Result: The quote is compliant and the renewal is on the calendar.

Questions

Hospital sales CRM, answered.

Can I record doctors who influence a purchase but never sign a PO?

Yes. Contacts on a hospital account are recorded by role, so a consultant can be the clinical champion, the biomedical head the technical evaluator, and the purchase officer the commercial contact. Visits and notes are logged against any of them.

How are demo and sample loans tracked?

The sample tracking module issues a unit to a hospital department with a responsible rep and a return date, and prompts for an outcome when the date passes. Demo stock is a location in inventory, so the units are never lost.

Does it handle both direct sales and tenders to the same hospital?

Yes. A hospital account can have direct opportunities in the pipeline and tenders in the tender tracker at the same time, with rate contracts recorded where they govern price. See tender management software.

What does a rep do on the mobile app?

Check in by GPS at the hospital, log the visit and who was met, update the opportunity stage, issue or collect a demo unit, and set the next follow-up. Managers see the activity feed on the web dashboard.

How is this different from a healthcare CRM for hospitals themselves?

This CRM is for companies selling to hospitals. A hospital managing its own patients uses a different product; see healthcare CRM software for that distinction and HIMS for hospital operations.

What happens after the purchase order?

The sales order, delivery, installation and acceptance are handled in the same platform, the serial enters the installed base, and warranty and AMC opportunities are visible from day one. See installed base management software.
