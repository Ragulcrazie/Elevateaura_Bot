What an ageing view is for

Receivables ageing sorts every unpaid invoice by how long it has been outstanding and groups it into buckets, so a balance that is a week old is never confused with one that is five months old. The total owed is a number your accountant already has. The ageing is what tells you which part of it is still normal trade credit and which part is a problem.

Aura Business holds this in the Finance and Accounting suite, fed directly by the invoices raised in the billing suite and the receipts entered against them. Because invoices, receipts, customer credit terms and the WhatsApp reminder run through the same system, the ageing view is also a working list: you can open the customer, see every open invoice, and act on it.

Why collections slip

One number, no structure

Chasing by noise

Receipts not applied

Credit limits nobody checks

Follow-up leaves no trace

Disputes surface at ninety days

Questions

Receivables ageing, answered.

Can we set our own ageing buckets?

Yes. The bucket boundaries are configured to match how your business thinks about credit, because a distributor selling to government hospitals has a very different sense of normal from one selling to private clinics.

Does it replace our accounting software?

No. It manages invoicing, receipts, ageing and collections. Your accountant keeps the books, using the Tally or accounting export and the GST returns prep. See GST billing.

How are credit limits handled?

Each customer carries a credit limit and credit days. The credit days set the due date on their invoices, and an order that would take the customer past the limit is flagged for a decision before despatch.

Can one payment settle several invoices?

Yes. A receipt can be applied across multiple open invoices, wholly or partly, so a bulk transfer from a hospital leaves the ledger accurate rather than sitting as an unallocated credit.

Are AMC instalments included in the ageing?

Yes. Contract instalments, product invoices and service invoices are all invoices, so they age together and appear in the same customer balance. See AMC billing.

Can reminders go out automatically?

Yes. Outstanding reminders can be sent on a schedule over WhatsApp and stop when the invoice is settled. See WhatsApp payment reminders.
