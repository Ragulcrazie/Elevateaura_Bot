In one sentence

What lead management does before a quotation exists

Lead management is the part of the sale that happens before anyone raises a quotation. An enquiry arrives from somewhere, someone has to own it, qualify it, keep contacting it on a sensible rhythm, and either move it forward or close it. Aura Business handles that inside the CRM and Sales suite through Leads and Pipeline, Lead-Source and Conversion Analytics and the rep activity feed.

For medical equipment the cycle is long and multi-person, so the useful part is not a prettier list. It is that the enquiry keeps a source, an owner and a dated next action from the first call until it becomes a quotation, and that at the end of the quarter you can see which sources produced orders rather than which produced noise.

How leads are lost

Most enquiries are not lost to a competitor. They are lost to silence.

A hospital enquiry that is answered in two days and then never followed up looks exactly like a lead that was never captured at all.

Everything is equally urgent

In practice

What a managed pipeline changes.

A website enquiry is answered the same day

Workflow: A quote request from the site lands in the pipeline, routes to the rep for that territory and appears at the top of their list with the product asked about.

Result: The first call happens while the enquiry is still warm.

A capital lead survives a six-month cycle

Workflow: A CT enquiry is scored high, given a monthly cadence, and every visit, sample and contact is logged against it.

Result: The lead is still alive when the budget is finally released.

The exhibition is judged honestly

Workflow: Every badge scanned at the stall enters as a lead tagged to that event and is tracked to order or closure.

Result: You know whether to book the stall again.

A rep leaves without taking the pipeline

Workflow: Their open leads are reassigned with all contacts, notes and visit history intact.

Result: The handover takes an afternoon, not a quarter.

Questions

Lead management for equipment sellers, answered.

Can leads come in from our website automatically?

Yes. The Quote-Request Inbound module takes enquiries from your site into the pipeline with the product and message attached, so the rep sees what was asked rather than a bare name and number.

How does lead scoring work here?

Scoring is based on the qualification fields your team fills, such as equipment, budget stage, decision-maker reached and expected timeline. It orders the rep's list; it does not decide for them.

Does it track WhatsApp enquiries?

WhatsApp Business integration is part of the platform, and enquiries that arrive that way are recorded as leads with their source so the follow-up and the conversion reporting include them.

Can a manager see what reps did this week?

Yes. The activity feed, field visits with GPS check-in and the attendance module give a factual record of calls, visits and updates per rep. See field tracking.

What happens when a lead becomes an order?

It converts to a customer account, and the quotation, sales order, GST invoice and later the service contract all hang off the same record, so history is continuous. See quotation software.

Is this different from a general-purpose CRM?

The pipeline itself is similar. The difference is what sits behind it: catalogue, serial numbers, installed base, AMC contracts and GST billing, so the lead does not end at the sale. See the comparison.
