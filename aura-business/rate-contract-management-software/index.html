What a rate contract is, and why it needs its own record

A rate contract fixes the price of named items for a named buyer for a stated period. The hospital or government department does not place one large order; it draws supplies against the agreed rate whenever it needs them, sometimes for a year or more. Winning the contract is the hard part. Honouring it correctly is where suppliers lose money and credibility.

Aura Business keeps rate contracts in the tenders and government sales suite, alongside the tender tracker and the document vault they usually come from. The contract holds the buyer, the item list, the agreed rates, the validity dates and the supply drawn against it so far.

From there it does its real job quietly. When a quotation or an order is raised for that buyer, the contract rate is the price that applies, and the contract's end date is visible long before the day the buyer asks whether you will extend.

How rate contracts go wrong

The mistakes that only surface at audit.

A rate contract is a promise made months ago by someone who may no longer be handling the account.

Quotes above the agreed rate

A coordinator prices from the current list and the buyer's stores officer spots the difference.

The contract sits in a folder

The signed document is on somebody's drive, so the terms are known only to whoever negotiated them.

Nobody knows how much is left

Where a quantity ceiling exists, supply continues past it because nothing is counted.

Expiry noticed too late

The contract lapses and an order arrives that cannot be supplied at any agreed price.

Item codes drift

The contract names one model and the despatch sends a successor model, which the buyer refuses.

Renewal negotiated blind

Without a record of what was actually drawn, the renewal discussion is based on nothing.

What holding the contract properly prevents.

A supply order is matched to its contract

Renewal is raised three months early

Result: The conversation happens before a gap in supply forces one.

Workflow: The contract, its documents and every order drawn against it sit on the customer account.

Result: Continuity does not depend on the person who negotiated it.

Questions

Rate contracts, answered.

How is a rate contract different from an AMC?

An AMC is a service contract covering maintenance of equipment over a period. A rate contract fixes the supply price of goods for a buyer over a period. Both are held in the platform, but they answer different questions. See what AMC software does.

Does the quotation stop someone pricing above the contract?

The contract rate is what the quotation applies for those items and that buyer. Whether an override is permitted, and who may approve one, is set through role-based access during setup.

Can different buyers have different rates for the same item?

Yes. Rates are held per contract and therefore per buyer, so the same product can carry one rate for a government institution and another for a private hospital, each valid for its own period.

Is GST applied on top of the contract rate?

Billing is GST-native, so tax is applied to the contracted rate on the tax invoice with the correct CGST, SGST or IGST split. Confirm the treatment for your specific award terms with your accountant.

Where do rate contracts come from in the system?

Usually from a won tender. The tender tracker, document vault and outcome analytics sit in the same suite, so an award becomes a contract without re-entering the details. See tender management.

Can we see everything about to expire?

Yes. Contracts approaching the end of their validity are surfaced with their remaining period and supply history, through the notifications hub and the reporting views.
