The Hyderabad context

Hospital chains at home, customers across two states.

A Hyderabad distributor typically sells to the city's hospital chains and medical colleges while servicing equipment in district hospitals across Telangana and along the Andhra coast. The territory is wide and the service obligation follows the equipment wherever it went.

Hospital chains buy centrally and consume locally. One purchase committee at head office approves the equipment, one contract covers every branch, and the service call comes from whichever site the machine is in. Aura Business handles this with hospital accounts that hold departments and branches, multi-site contracts that attach serials at each branch, and a ticket that resolves to the right asset and agreement no matter which site raised it.

The two-state territory is a logistics and compliance problem as much as a sales one. Engineers based in Hyderabad cover calls hours away, spares travel with them, and an invoice to a hospital in Andhra Pradesh carries IGST while one to a Telangana hospital carries CGST and SGST. The Field Service suite dispatches by area and skill and gives engineers an offline job card for hospitals with poor signal; the GST Billing suite decides the tax split from the customer's place of supply and raises e-way bills for consignments crossing the border.

For Hyderabad's device manufacturers

The city's growing device manufacturing base has the manufacturer's version of the same problems: dealers in several states, demo units on loan, and an installed base that needs to be visible by dealer and territory. The CRM and Portals suites cover doctors and decision-makers, sample tracking, territory targets, a dealer order portal with UTR payments, and the installed-base register.

In practice

Hyderabad situations the platform handles.

One CMC, six hospital branches

Workflow: A chain's contract attaches forty serials across branches in Hyderabad and two district towns; a call from any branch shows the same cover and SLA.

Result: Every branch is served under the agreement, and none is billed twice.

A district hospital call is closed offline

Workflow: The engineer drives three hours, completes the job card and collects the signature without signal, and it syncs on the way back.

Result: The record exists the same day, not after the next office visit.

An Andhra invoice carries the right tax

Workflow: The customer's GSTIN and delivery address put the supply in Andhra Pradesh; the invoice applies IGST and the e-way bill is generated before dispatch.

Result: No correction, no held consignment.

A manufacturer sees its base by dealer

Workflow: Every installed serial carries the dealer and district; the reports engine groups installations and warranty status by both.

Result: Dealer reviews and service planning use the same list.

Questions

Aura Business in Hyderabad, answered.

Is there any in-person support in Hyderabad?

No. Elevate Aura works from Chennai and serves Hyderabad remotely: cloud deployment, screen-share onboarding and WhatsApp support. In-person work is offered in Chennai and in parts of Tamil Nadu reachable by travel.

Can one contract cover hospital branches in both states?

Yes. A multi-site contract attaches serials at any number of branches regardless of state, and the service ticket resolves to the right agreement from the asset. Billing for the contract follows the customer's GST registration and place of supply.

How do engineers work in hospitals with no mobile signal?

The mobile job card runs in offline mode. Fault, work done, spares, photos and the customer signature are captured on the phone and sync when it reconnects. See the technician app.

Does it handle IGST and e-way bills for Andhra Pradesh customers?

Yes. The tax split is decided by place of supply from the customer record, and e-way bills are raised from the invoice or challan for consignments that cross the state border. See GST billing software.

Can we start with a few modules and add more?

Yes. Aura Business plans are by module count, 15, 47 or all 87, with unlimited users on every plan. Many distributors start with CRM, billing and AMC and switch on field service, inventory and portals later.

Who supports us after go-live?

Ragul Sekar, the founder who builds and runs the platform, on WhatsApp and phone. There is no ticket queue between you and the person who can fix the issue.
