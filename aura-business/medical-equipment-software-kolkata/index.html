The Kolkata context

The goods leave quickly. They arrive days later.

Kolkata is the distribution point for eastern India and the gateway to the north-eastern states, so the distances between the godown and the customer are measured in days on the road rather than hours. Almost every operational weakness in this trade is made worse by that gap.

A consignment travelling to Assam, Tripura, Meghalaya or the hill districts spends a long time in transit, which means the paperwork it left with has to be right the first time. The invoice takes its tax split from the buyer's GSTIN and delivery address, so supply outside West Bengal carries IGST, the e-invoice IRN and QR are generated, and the e-way bill is raised from the same record with transporter and vehicle details. Order tracking gives the customer a page to watch instead of a phone number to ring.

The sub-dealer network is the second half of the business. In towns across Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand and the north-east, a local dealer holds the customer relationship, buys at its own price list and pays by bank transfer. The distributor portal gives each of them a branded login, the authorisation and territory they are entitled to, a place to upload payment references, and an order history that does not depend on a message thread. Receivables ageing then shows which dealers are genuinely overdue, with reminders sent over WhatsApp.

A service visit that has to succeed first time

When an engineer travels a long way to a district hospital, a second visit for a part he did not carry is expensive in a way it is not in a city. The call is logged against the serial with the asset's full service history, so the desk can see what failed last time and which spare it took, and the parts carried are booked against the job before departure. The mobile job card is completed on site with readings, photographs and a signature, works offline, and posts the spares used back to stock. Due maintenance in that region can be batched into the same trip.

Knowing where the money and the cover stand

A spread-out territory hides problems, so the owner view matters more than usual here. Receivables ageing by dealer, cover lapsing by region, open calls and SLA status, and profit by product line and territory sit on one dashboard, and the reports engine goes behind any of them.

In practice

Four eastern situations the platform is built for.

A consignment to the north-east leaves once, correctly

Workflow: The invoice applies IGST, the IRN is generated and the e-way bill is raised with transporter details, while the customer follows the order on a tracking page.

Result: Days on the road do not turn into days of phone calls.

An engineer packs for the right fault

Workflow: The call shows the asset's service history and previous spares, so the parts likely to be needed are booked to the job card before the journey starts.

Result: Fewer trips wasted on a part left behind.

A dealer in a distant town is chased on evidence

Workflow: Receivables ageing lists the dealer's invoices by age with the last reminder sent, and a WhatsApp reminder goes from the payment tracker.

Result: Collection effort follows the risk rather than the loudest caller.

Contracts in a whole region are reviewed together

Workflow: Cover expiring in one state is listed with its value and visit history, and the renewal calls and any due maintenance are planned as a single trip.

Result: Renewals and visits share the cost of travel.

Questions

Aura Business in Kolkata, answered.

Do you visit Kolkata to implement the system?

No. Elevate Aura works from Chennai and has no office, staff or local number in the east. Setup is on the cloud, training is over screen-share and support is on WhatsApp. Travel is possible where a project warrants it.

We supply to the north-eastern states. Is interstate documentation handled?

Yes. The tax split follows the buyer's GSTIN and delivery address, so supply outside West Bengal carries IGST, and the e-way bill is generated from the invoice or challan with transporter and vehicle details. See e-way bill software.

Can sub-dealers order and pay online?

Each dealer gets a branded portal login showing its authorised brands, territory and price list, can place orders and upload the bank payment reference for verification, and can track dispatch. See dealer management software.

How does it help when an engineer travels for a day to reach a site?

The call carries the asset's service history and past spares so the right parts can be booked to the job card before departure, due maintenance visits in the same region are batched into the trip, and the job card works offline on site.

Can we see outstanding by dealer and by state?

Yes. Receivables ageing groups outstanding invoices by customer in age buckets, and the reports engine groups sales, cover and profit by territory, so a distant dealer is visible rather than buried in a total. See receivables ageing software.

Do we pay for each branch user?

No. Users are unlimited on every plan, which differ only by module count, 15, 47 or all 87. Branch staff, sub-dealers and engineers all get logins with role-based access and an audit log behind them.
