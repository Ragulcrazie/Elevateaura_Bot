In one sentence

What technician tracking software is, and what the app does

Technician tracking software shows the office where each field engineer is, which job they are on, and what they did there, through a mobile app the engineer carries. In Aura Business the app is the field half of the platform: GPS check-in at the customer site, attendance marked with location and a selfie, a route for the day, and a mobile job card that captures fault, work done, spares, photos and the customer's signature.

Tracking on its own annoys engineers and helps nobody. The app earns its place because it removes the paperwork: the job card replaces the carbon copy, the photo replaces the WhatsApp forward, and the signature replaces the argument about whether the visit happened. The office gets the same data live, without a phone call.

Wrong skill on the job

A ventilator call goes to whoever is nearest, not to the engineer trained on that model.

In practice

What the app changes for the team.

A hospital asks when the engineer will arrive

Workflow: The desk sees the engineer checked in at the previous site forty minutes ago and the next job on their route is this hospital.

Result: An answer in seconds, without disturbing the engineer.

A job is completed in a room with no signal

Workflow: The engineer fills the job card, takes photos and collects the signature offline in the MRI console room; it syncs from the car park.

Result: No paper, no re-entry, no missing record.

The right engineer goes to the ventilator

Workflow: The dispatcher filters by skill and the call is assigned to the engineer trained on that model, even if another is closer.

Result: First-visit fixes go up and repeat visits go down.

Payroll takes attendance from the app

Workflow: Location-stamped attendance and leave feed the payroll inputs for the month.

Result: No reconciliation of chat messages against a register.

Questions

Technician tracking, answered.

Does it track engineers all day or only at check-in?

GPS check-in timestamps arrival at each customer site, and the HRMS suite includes GPS and field tracking for the office view. How continuous the tracking is can be set to suit your team and their consent, and is agreed during setup.

Will it work inside hospitals with no signal?

Yes. The job card, photos and signature are captured in offline mode and sync when the phone reconnects. Attendance and check-in also queue until a connection is available.

Is the app available on both Android and iOS?

Yes. Elevate Aura builds the mobile app in React Native or Flutter for Android and iOS, and it can be published under your own branding. See field service app development.

How does skill-based allocation work?

Each engineer's profile records the models and brands they are trained on. When the desk dispatches a call, it can filter and assign by skill as well as by area and schedule, so specialised equipment goes to the right person.

Can the same app be used by sales reps?

Yes. Field visits with GPS check-in, visit notes and the activity feed are part of the CRM suite, so sales and service staff use one app with different screens based on role.

What does the customer sign?

The engineer shows the completed job card on the phone and the customer signs on screen. The signed job card is stored on the asset's service history and can be shared with the customer as a PDF.
