The Delhi NCR context

Two capitals of the business: the tender desk and the border of three states.

Delhi NCR is where central and state purchasing bodies, teaching hospitals and import agents sit close together, and where a supplier's calendar is set by publication dates, pre-bid meetings and EMD cut-offs rather than by a sales pipeline alone.

A tender business fails quietly. A notice is published and nobody sees it, a document goes in without the authorisation letter, an EMD is not released after the award. Aura Business fetches government tenders every morning into the Tender Tracker, keeps the bid documents and authorisations in a vault against the tender, and sends reminders for EMD, submission and opening dates. Outcome and win-rate analytics then show which departments and product lines are worth the effort of bidding at all.

Rate-contract management is the part most dealers carry in a folder. Once an item is on contract, every subsequent supply order has to be priced and invoiced against that rate for the period agreed, and the quantities drawn have to be visible before the contract lapses. Holding the contract in the system means the quotation builder and the invoice both take the agreed rate, rather than a price a salesperson remembers.

Three states within an hour's drive

The second peculiarity of NCR is fiscal geography. An office in Delhi, a godown in Faridabad and a customer in Noida sit within a short drive of one another and are still three places of supply. Aura Business decides CGST and SGST or IGST from the buyer's GSTIN and delivery address, raises the e-invoice IRN and QR, and generates the e-way bill from the same record, so a consignment that crosses a state line on a city road still leaves with correct papers. Multi-warehouse stock keeps the Delhi and Haryana godowns separate rather than pooled.

After the award, the machine still has to be serviced

Supply orders from northern states send equipment to district hospitals and medical colleges far from NCR, and the contract to maintain them usually follows. The installed-base register records each serial with its institution, warranty start and contract, preventive maintenance schedules are raised per asset, and mobile job cards are filled on the engineer's phone with photographs and a customer signature, offline where the network is poor. SLA definitions and breach tracking matter more on government contracts than anywhere else, because the penalty is written into the agreement.

In practice

Four NCR situations the platform is built for.

A notice is seen the morning it appears

Workflow: The tender tracker lists new notices against your product lines, the desk marks the ones worth bidding, and deadline reminders go out for the pre-bid meeting, the EMD and the submission.

Result: Nothing is lost because somebody stopped checking the portal.

A rate contract prices itself

Workflow: The contract rate is held against the item and the buyer, so the quotation and the tax invoice both pick it up for the agreed period, with quantities drawn shown against the limit.

Result: Supply orders are billed at the rate you actually signed.

A godown in Faridabad ships to Noida

Workflow: The invoice takes the tax split from the buyer's GSTIN, the IRN is generated, and the e-way bill is raised with vehicle and transporter details before the consignment leaves.

Result: A short journey across a state line still carries full papers.

A district hospital raises a breakdown call

Workflow: The call is logged against the serial, cover is shown as warranty, contract or chargeable, an engineer is dispatched, and the job card records the spare fitted, which drops stock.

Result: Response time against the SLA is recorded, not argued about.

Questions

Aura Business in Delhi NCR, answered.

Do you have an office or engineers in Delhi?

No. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai and has no office, staff or local phone number in the NCR. Setup is remote by cloud deployment and screen-share, support is on WhatsApp, and travel is arranged only where a project warrants it.

How are government tenders picked up?

The tender tracker pulls government tender notices every morning and lists them for your team to shortlist. Documents and authorisations are stored against each tender, and reminders cover EMD, submission and opening dates. See tender management software.

Can it handle a rate contract with a government buyer?

Yes. The agreed rate is held against the item and the buyer for the contract period, quotations and invoices take that rate, and quantities drawn are visible against the limit so the contract can be reviewed before it lapses.

We supply to Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan. Is interstate billing handled?

Yes. The tax split follows the buyer's GSTIN and delivery address, so supply outside Delhi carries IGST, and the e-way bill is generated from the invoice or delivery challan with transporter details. See e-way bill software.

Can our engineers work where the mobile network is weak?

Yes. The field app holds the job card offline, including the fault notes, spares used, photographs and the customer signature, and syncs when the phone reconnects. See mobile job card software.

How many logins do we get for the tender desk and branches?

Unlimited users on every plan. Plans differ by module count, 15, 47 or all 87, and role-based access decides who can open tender documents, change rates or view the owner dashboard.
