Questions on billing

GST billing software, answered.

Is the billing GST compliant?

Yes. Quotations, tax invoices and Delivery Challans follow GST rules with CGST, SGST and IGST, HSN codes and gapless numbering that your accountant accepts.

Does it generate Delivery Challans and e-Way Bills?

Yes. Delivery Challans and the details needed for e-Way Bills are generated from the same order, so dispatch paperwork is one click, not re-typing.

How does it help me get paid faster?

An outstanding ageing view shows who to chase first, and automatic WhatsApp reminders follow up until the payment lands. See automation.

Can it handle credit notes and proforma invoices?

Yes. Proforma invoices, credit and debit notes and a customer ledger are all supported.

Does billing connect to sales and service?

Yes. An accepted quote becomes an order and a tax invoice without re-typing, and service jobs generate service invoices automatically.
