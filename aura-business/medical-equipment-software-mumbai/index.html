The Mumbai context

The goods land in Mumbai. The customers are everywhere else.

Mumbai is where a large share of imported medical equipment enters the country and where many national distributors are headquartered. The operational consequence is that most of what a Mumbai distributor sells leaves Maharashtra, through a dealer network that has to be priced, supplied, paid and supported state by state.

The dealer network is the first thing to put in order. Each dealer holds an authorisation for certain brands and territories, buys at its own price list, pays by bank transfer, and wants to know when the consignment will ship. Aura Business records brand and authorisation per dealer, loads dealer price lists, gives each dealer a branded portal to order and upload UTRs, and shows order status on a tracking page so the phone stops ringing.

Interstate billing is the second. A Mumbai distributor invoicing a dealer in Gujarat, Kerala or Assam applies IGST, and the consignment needs an e-way bill before it moves. The GST Billing suite decides the tax split from the dealer's place of supply, generates the e-invoice IRN and QR, raises the e-way bill from the invoice, and handles credit notes for returns and rate differences. GSTR-1 and 3B prep and a Tally export keep the accountant in step with a high invoice volume.

The equipment does not stop at the dealer

An importer's obligations follow the equipment: principal warranty to honour, claims to raise with the overseas manufacturer, and an installed base that is the pipeline for AMC and extended warranty. The installed-base register records every serial with the dealer, hospital and warranty start; warranty tracking marks parts replaced under claim; and the renewal engine lists expiring cover by dealer and city so the contract is sold in time.

In practice

Mumbai situations the platform is built for.

Forty dealers, one order desk

Workflow: Dealers across states order on the portal at their own price lists, upload UTRs, and watch dispatch on the tracking page; the desk confirms payments and releases orders.

Result: The order desk handles exceptions, not every order.

A consignment to Assam leaves with its papers

Workflow: The invoice applies IGST, the IRN is generated, and the e-way bill is raised from the same record with transporter details before pickup.

Result: No consignment held for a missing document.

A principal warranty claim has evidence

Workflow: A board replaced under warranty at a hospital in Pune is logged with failed and replacement serials on the job card, tied to the GRN it arrived on.

Result: The claim to the overseas manufacturer is filed with dates and serials.

The director sees the country on one screen

Workflow: P&L by product line and territory, receivables ageing by dealer, and AMC at risk by state on the owner dashboard.

Result: Decisions on dealers and brands from live numbers.

Questions

Aura Business in Mumbai, answered.

Do you visit Mumbai for implementation?

No. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai and serves Mumbai remotely, with cloud deployment, screen-share onboarding and WhatsApp support. In-person work is limited to Chennai and places in Tamil Nadu reachable by travel.

Can dealers in different states have different price lists and product access?

Yes. Each dealer account carries its authorised brands, territory and price list, and the portal shows only what applies. See dealer management software.

How are IGST and e-way bills handled for interstate supply?

The tax split is decided by the dealer's GSTIN and delivery address, so interstate supply carries IGST. The e-way bill is generated from the invoice or delivery challan with transporter and vehicle details before dispatch. See GST billing software.

Does it track warranty claims back to an overseas principal?

Parts replaced under warranty are recorded with failed and replacement serials, the job card and the GRN they came from, which is the evidence for your claim. The claim itself is raised with the principal in their process; the credit received is tracked in payables.

Can the installed base be viewed by dealer or state?

Yes. Every serial in the register carries its dealer, hospital and location, and the reports engine groups installations, warranty status and contracts by any of them. See installed base management software.

How many users can we have?

Unlimited users on every Aura Business plan. Plans are by module count, 15, 47 or all 87, so branches, dealers and office staff can all have logins with role-based access.
