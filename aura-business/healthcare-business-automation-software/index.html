Common questions

Business automation, answered.

What does the automation actually do?

It runs the repetitive work for you: WhatsApp payment and renewal reminders, auto-quotations for renewals, smart routing of service tickets, reorder alerts and scheduled reports, so your team spends time on customers, not admin.

Does it use WhatsApp?

Yes. Payment reminders, renewal nudges and updates go out over WhatsApp automatically, which fits how Indian SMBs and their customers already communicate. This powers billing collections.

What is renewal prediction?

The platform watches contract dates and surfaces renewals that are about to lapse, with the revenue at risk, so you act before the income is gone. See AMC software.

Is this AI hype or real workflow?

It is practical automation and smart routing built into the workflow, not hype. Rules and predictions handle the busywork; people still make the decisions.

Can automation be tailored to us?

Yes. Reminders, routing and workflows are configured to your process during setup, since the platform is built around how you work.
