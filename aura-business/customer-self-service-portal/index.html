In one sentence

What a customer sees when they log in

A customer self-service portal is a login for the people who buy from you. Instead of calling your office to ask where the order is, whether the invoice copy can be sent again, when the contract expires or how much is outstanding, they open the portal and read it for themselves.

In Aura Business the portal sits on the same records your team works in. An order status shown to the customer is the sales order your despatch team updated. A document in their vault is the invoice your billing desk raised. A service request they submit becomes a ticket in your helpdesk queue with the asset attached.

The point is not that customers enjoy portals. Most will still call sometimes. The point is that the routine questions, which are the majority, stop reaching a person who was doing something else.

Questions

The customer portal, answered.

Does the portal carry our branding or yours?

Yours. Branded login, your logo and colours on the portal, dashboard, mobile app and invoices are part of setup, so the customer experiences it as your system.

Can a customer see anything we would rather they did not?

No. Access is role-based and scoped to that customer's own records. Costs, margins, supplier prices and internal ticket notes are not exposed in the portal.

Is this the same as the distributor ordering portal?

They are related modules in the same suite. The self-service portal is for end customers checking their own account, while the distributor portal lets partners place B2B orders and submit payment UTR details.

What happens when a customer submits a service request?

It enters the helpdesk as a ticket with the asset and contract attached, follows the status workflow, and runs against the SLA timers defined for that customer. See helpdesk ticketing.

Will customers actually use it?

Institutional buyers with their own audit needs usually do, because the documents they chase are in one place. Expect calls to fall rather than stop, which is still a real saving for a small office.

Do our customers pay for logins?

Plans are priced by module tier with unlimited users, so portal access does not add a per-login charge. The exact scope is settled on a call.
