What a delivery challan is doing that an invoice is not

A delivery challan accompanies goods that are moving when the sale is not yet complete. The most common cases for a distributor are a demonstration unit going to a hospital for evaluation, goods despatched ahead of invoicing, material going out for job work or repair, and anything expected to come back. The challan records what physically left, from which location, for whom.

In Aura Business the Delivery Challan module sits between the sales order and the tax invoice. Raising it reduces stock from the despatching godown and creates a document the receiving party signs. When the sale is confirmed, the challan converts to a GST tax invoice carrying the same lines, so nothing is retyped and the goods movement and the sale remain separately documented.

Where despatch goes wrong

Goods leave the building faster than the paperwork does.

Most challan problems are the same problem: something physical happened and no document was created at the moment it happened.

Demo units that never come home

A monitor goes out for evaluation on a handwritten slip and nobody can say where it is eight months later.

Challan books with no link to stock

The printed book is filled in by hand and the system's stock figure never changes.

Retyping the invoice

When the customer confirms, the invoice is keyed again from the challan and the quantities do not match.

No proof of receipt

The hospital store says it never arrived and you have nothing signed to show otherwise.

Returnable goods treated as sold

Items sent for trial or repair are written off because nobody marked them returnable.

Challan and e-way bill out of step

The e-way bill is created from different details than the document actually travelling with the goods.

Questions

Delivery challans, answered.

When should we use a challan instead of an invoice?

Whenever goods move without a completed sale, such as demo units, goods sent for job work or repair, and despatches ahead of invoicing. Which situations legally require a challan for your business should be confirmed with your accountant.

Does raising a challan reduce stock?

Yes. The despatching godown or van is reduced when the challan is raised, and the movement appears in the stock ledger, so stock reflects what is physically in the building rather than what has been invoiced.

How does a challan become an invoice?

The challan is converted, and the tax invoice is created with the same lines, quantities, serials and batches, with GST applied by place of supply. The link between the two documents is kept.

Can we track demo equipment out on trial?

Yes. Goods sent on a returnable challan stay on an open list with their serial numbers until they are either invoiced or received back, so evaluation units do not disappear.

Is the e-way bill raised from the challan?

It can be raised from the challan or from the invoice, depending on which document the consignment travels against. See e-way bill software.

What proof of delivery do we get?

The person delivering captures a signature and a photograph on the field app against the challan, and the customer can also follow the despatch on the order tracking page. See dealer management.
