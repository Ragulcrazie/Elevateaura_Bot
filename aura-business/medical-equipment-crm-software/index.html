Questions distributors ask

Medical equipment CRM, answered.

What makes this different from a generic CRM?

A generic CRM tracks leads and stops. Aura Business follows the full medical-equipment cycle: enquiry, quotation, order, delivery, installation, AMC contract, service tickets and renewal, with GST billing and field tracking built in.

Can field staff use it on a mobile phone?

Yes. Sales and service staff work from a mobile app with GPS check-ins and visit logging, while managers see everything live on the web dashboard. See field service software.

Is it GST compliant for Indian billing?

Yes. Quotations, tax invoices and Delivery Challans are GST compliant with gapless numbering that your accountant accepts. See billing software.

Can we start small and add modules later?

Yes. The platform is modular. Start with the suites you need today and switch on AMC, inventory or others as you grow.

How long does it take to go live?

Aura Business is delivered on a 30-working-day framework where scope allows, configured to your workflow and branding.

Who builds and supports it?

It is built and supported by Ragul Sekar of Elevate Aura, a founder-led studio in Chennai, so you talk directly to the person who builds it.
