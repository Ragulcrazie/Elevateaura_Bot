In one sentence

What an automated reminder run actually does

A reminder run takes every invoice that is open and due, works out which customers need contacting today under the schedule you set, and sends each one a WhatsApp message with their own invoice number, amount and due date in it. When a receipt is entered against that invoice, the customer drops off the run without anybody having to remember to stop it.

In Aura Business this is the WhatsApp Business integration in the Analytics and Platform suite, driven by the Payment Tracker and the receivables ageing in the Finance suite. It matters because most overdue payments in a distribution business are not refusals. They are invoices sitting unactioned in somebody's approval queue, and a polite message with the reference in it is usually all that moves them.

Why chasing fails

Reminders are boring, repetitive, and the first thing to be skipped.

A person who has to send forty individual messages by hand will send fifteen, and will send them to whoever they remember.

Typed one message at a time

Each reminder is written by hand with the invoice number copied across from another screen.

Messages without a reference

A vague request for payment reaches an accounts department that handles hundreds of invoices and goes nowhere.

Reminders after the money arrived

A customer who paid last week gets chased again and the relationship takes the damage.

No rhythm at all

One reminder goes at ninety days, when nothing goes at seven, fourteen and thirty.

Sent from a personal number

The follow-up history lives on one employee's phone and leaves when they do.

Nobody knows what was sent

Two people chase the same balance and the customer hears two different things.

Questions

WhatsApp payment reminders, answered.

What does the reminder message say?

A short, plain message naming your business, the invoice number, the amount outstanding and the due date, with a request to confirm payment. The wording is set up for you during onboarding and is not written fresh each time.

Will a customer who has paid still be chased?

No. Reminders are driven by the open balance, so once the receipt is applied against the invoice the customer leaves the run. This is why applying receipts promptly matters more than the reminders themselves.

How often are reminders sent?

On the schedule you choose, typically a light first reminder shortly after the due date and firmer ones as the balance ages. Different schedules can be used for different kinds of customers.

Can we stop reminders for one customer?

Yes. A customer or a single invoice can be excluded, which is what you want when a dispute is open or a payment plan has been agreed, and the exclusion is recorded.

Does this need WhatsApp Business?

Messages are sent through the WhatsApp Business integration so they come from your business account rather than a personal number. The account setup is done with you during implementation.

Are AMC instalments reminded as well?

Yes. Any open invoice can enter the run, including contract instalments and service invoices. See receivables ageing for how the chase list is ordered.
