In one sentence

What territory management settles before the selling starts

Territory management answers one question in advance: for any given account, who is responsible. Split by geography, by segment, by product line or by a mixture of the three, and every hospital, lab or dealer belongs to exactly one rep who is measured on it.

Aura Business holds this in the territory and target management module. Accounts carry a territory, territories carry an owner and a target, and the reporting that follows, on pipeline, orders, collections and coverage, can be read territory by territory instead of as one company-wide figure.

For medical sales the segment split matters as much as the map. A government medical college, a corporate chain and a standalone nursing home in the same district buy in different ways, and it is reasonable for different reps to own them. The model allows that without leaving any account unowned.

Questions

Territory management, answered.

Can a territory be a segment rather than an area?

Yes. Territories can be drawn by geography, by customer segment such as government or corporate chains, or by product line, and an account is placed in the one that reflects who should own it.

Can two reps share an account?

The model is built around one owner per account so credit and follow-up are unambiguous. Where a specialist supports a sale, that involvement is recorded through visits and activity rather than by splitting ownership.

Do reps see other territories?

Role-based access decides that. The usual setup gives a rep their own accounts and gives managers and the owner a view across all territories on the dashboard.

How are targets tracked?

Targets are set against the territory for a period and read against orders and pipeline in the reporting views. What counts towards a target, order value or collection, is agreed during setup.

What happens to open leads when a territory is redrawn?

They move to the new owner with their contacts, notes, visits and samples intact, so a reassignment does not restart the conversation. See lead management.

Does this help with coverage as well as sales?

Yes. Territory grouping is what makes a call cycle possible, because accounts due for a visit can be listed per patch. See beat plan software.
