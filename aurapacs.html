Questions

Good to know

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. AuraPACS is hybrid. A node runs on your premises so your scanners and staff reach it over your local network even if the internet drops. A thin cloud layer adds remote viewing and encrypted backup.

Where are our images stored?

On your premises by default, with an encrypted backup copy in the cloud. Your data stays yours and is reachable only by the people you allow.

Can patients and referring doctors get reports?

Yes. AuraPACS generates a secure share link that you send over WhatsApp. They open the study and the report without any login at their end.

Is this a diagnostic device?

AuraPACS stores, displays and shares imaging. The clinical read and report are done by your own radiologist, exactly as today.

Who supports us?

The person who built it. Direct on WhatsApp, and in person in Chennai. No ticket queue, no account-manager layers.
