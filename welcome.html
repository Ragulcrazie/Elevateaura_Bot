Getting started with
Aura Business.

Everything you need to log in and start running your business on Aura Business. Keep this page handy, or print it for your team.

Your login details

Web address: app.elevateaura.co.in/login
Email: the email we set up for you
Temporary password: sent to you separately

First five minutes

  1. Open the login page

    Go to app.elevateaura.co.in/login in any browser, on your computer or phone. Nothing to install for the office team.

  2. Sign in

    Enter the email and temporary password we sent you. You will land on your dashboard.

  3. Look at your dashboard

    The left sidebar shows only the modules you signed up for. Your owner dashboard shows pipeline, collections, AMC and service at a glance.

  4. Add your team

    Every plan has unlimited users. Tell us the names and emails of your staff and we will create their logins with the right access for their role.

  5. Field staff: install the app

    Your field and service team install the Aura Business Android app and sign in with their own login to log visits, job cards and spares from the field.

A few things to know

Your data is yours. It is isolated to your business and only your team can see it. It is backed up in the cloud.
Add modules anytime. Want more of the 87 modules later? Just message us and we switch them on; your plan moves to the matching tier.
We migrate your data. Send us your existing Excel sheets of customers, products and open contracts and we load them in for you.
Support is direct. You deal with the person who built it, on WhatsApp, and in person in Chennai. No ticket queue.

Stuck on anything?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will sort it out, usually the same day.
