# Deliverable 17: Competitor gap analysis

Method: 18 live web searches and 10 page fetches on 20 September 2026 across the twelve
head queries below plus three international probes. The search tool is US-located, so
positions are a proxy for an India-based searcher. No domain-authority, traffic or volume
numbers are stated because none could be verified; treat every "top result" as approximate.

A useful baseline: `elevateaura.co.in/aura-business/medical-equipment-crm-software/` already
surfaced for "medical equipment distributor software India" and "medical equipment service
AMC software India". The site has a foothold on the niche Aura Business terms, which is why
Phase 1 reinforces that cluster rather than starting elsewhere.

## Cross-cutting patterns

1. Directories dominate PACS and distributor queries. That signals weak vendor pages. A rich
   product page can outrank them, and Elevate Aura should also be listed in Capterra India,
   SoftwareSuggest and TechnologyCounter with real information.
2. Vendor-written listicles dominate Indian SaaS terms and are thin. Honest comparison pages
   with real tables are a repeatable play; the `/compare/` hub is that play.
3. Price transparency is the biggest unmet intent across HMS, clinic software and PACS. This
   is answered without numbers: the owner has decided not to publish prices, so the pricing
   pages explain the model and what drives a quote, which is what the query really wants.
4. India-specific terms (ABDM, ABHA, NABH-style documentation, TPA, GST e-invoice, UPI,
   WhatsApp) are missing from ranking pages. They now appear on dedicated pages.
5. AMC is Indian vocabulary with an all-Indian SERP and no medical-equipment page. Built.
6. Hybrid on-premise plus cloud and WhatsApp sharing, AuraPACS's two named differentiators, are
   mentioned by no ranking PACS page. Both now have dedicated pages.
7. Free tools rank (free DICOM viewer, "free AMC software"). A public upload-and-view DICOM
   page is the strongest top-of-funnel asset available; it needs product work.
8. Small vendors do rank in embedded, UAE PACS and AMC. Services and PACS can win
   internationally; HMS and healthcare-app head terms should be attacked through long-tail,
   compliance and local pages, which is how the clusters are shaped.

## What was not adopted from the research, and why

- Pages with INR price tables: no verified price list beyond the 15-module tier. Owner task.
- "Top 10 software in India" listicles naming competitors: they rank, but they invite legal
  and accuracy risk and dilute the entity. Category comparisons were built instead.
- Regulatory claims (IEC 62304, HIPAA, Nabidh/Malaffi integration): not verified for the
  products; pages say "confirm locally" and describe documentation support only.
