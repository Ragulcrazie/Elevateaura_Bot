Asset management FAQ

What is biomedical asset management software?

It keeps a register of every installed machine with its serial number, warranty, calibration and full service history, so you know the status of each asset across its life.

Does it track warranty and calibration?

Yes. Warranty status and calibration schedules are tracked per asset, so you bill correctly and stay compliant.

How does it help with AMC?

The installed-base register is the foundation for AMC upsell and renewals, since every installed machine is a contract opportunity with a service record.
