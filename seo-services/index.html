What this service is

Elevate Aura plans, writes and builds the pages a healthcare or medical-equipment business needs in order to be found: a page for each product line, each application, each department or each city where there is real demand, with the technical work, the schema and the analytics that make those pages usable.

The work is done by the same studio that builds the websites, so an SEO recommendation does not go into a report and wait for a developer. It goes into the site. Medline Robotics, a medical robotics company in Chennai, has 253 product and city SEO landing pages across four product lines, built this way alongside their storefront.

No one can guarantee a ranking, and we do not offer one. What we commit to is the method, the pages actually shipped, and measurement you can read yourself in GA4 and Search Console.

Questions

Healthcare SEO services, answered.

Can you guarantee we will rank on page one?

No, and nobody honestly can. Search engines decide rankings and change how they decide them. What we can commit to is the pages built, the technical fixes made, the schema applied and the reporting, so you can judge the work on what was delivered.

Do you only do SEO for sites you built?

No. We work on existing WordPress, Next.js and other sites. Where the site cannot support the structure the pages need, we say so plainly and scope the fix before quoting any content work.

What do you actually deliver each month?

A named set of pages published, technical changes made, schema and internal links updated, and a report of what changed with GA4 and Search Console data. The deliverable is written into the scope before work starts.

How is this different from a traditional SEO retainer?

A retainer often ends with a list of recommendations for someone else to implement. Here the recommendation and the implementation are the same job, because the studio builds the site as well.

Do you write the content or do we?

We write it, using your specifications, product data, department lists and service details. You review before publication. Where a claim needs a fact we do not have, we ask rather than invent one.

Are you able to work with businesses in other cities?

Yes. The work is remote everywhere in India and abroad, with calls and WhatsApp for reviews. Face-to-face meetings happen in Chennai, and elsewhere in Tamil Nadu where travel is practical.
