What hospital SEO services cover

Hospital SEO is largely an inventory problem. You have thirty departments, a hundred consultants, a list of procedures and often more than one campus, and a website that represents all of it in about a dozen pages. The work is to give each department, each doctor, each major procedure and each campus a page that is genuinely worth landing on, and to keep that set maintained as the roster changes.

Alongside the pages sits the technical layer: index coverage across a large site, clean URLs by department, schema for the hospital, its departments and its doctors, and load times that survive a phone on mobile data. Then the measurement layer: GA4 events on appointment forms, call buttons and WhatsApp, so a department can be discussed in terms of appointment requests rather than sessions.

Two things we will not do. We will not promise a ranking, because nobody controls that. And we will not write, buy or arrange patient reviews. Reviews are the hospital's own to collect from real patients.

Why large hospital sites underperform

A hundred consultants and one Our Doctors page.

The problem is rarely the writing. It is that the site has far fewer pages than the hospital has things patients search for.

Departments as menu items

A department listed in a dropdown with two lines of text cannot answer the search that names it.

Doctors without profiles

A photo and a designation give a patient nothing to decide on and search engines nothing to index.

Roster changes never reflected

Consultants who left are still listed and new ones are missing, so the most searched names lead nowhere.

Appointments untracked

The form posts to an inbox with no event, so no one can attribute a single appointment to a department page.

In practice

Three hospital searches and what answers them.

A consultant searched by name

Workflow: A referred patient searches the consultant's name and reaches a profile with qualifications, department, OP days and a request form.

Result: An appointment request instead of a call to a switchboard.

A department with a waiting list

Workflow: The department page carries the conditions treated, the consultants, the facilities and the route to book, and its form is tagged to that department.

Result: The marketing head can see which departments the site is filling.

A second campus

Workflow: The newer unit gets its own page with its own consultants, timings, directions and emergency number.

Result: Local searches near that unit have something to reach.

Questions

Hospital SEO services, answered.

Can you guarantee our hospital ranks first for our city?

No. Rankings are decided by search engines and change without notice, and anyone promising a position is either guessing or planning something we would not do. We commit to the page set delivered, the technical work and honest reporting.

Will you arrange patient reviews or ratings for us?

No. Reviews must come from real patients through your own channels, and we will not write, buy or solicit them on your behalf. We can add clear links so a patient who wants to leave feedback can find where to do it.

We have over a hundred doctors. Is a page each realistic?

Yes, because it is a template filled from your roster data rather than a hundred design jobs. The harder part is keeping it current, so a routine for adding and retiring profiles is part of the work.

Can appointment requests go into our hospital system?

Where the hospital runs HIMS, a request from any page can land in the appointment queue and the patient can be confirmed on WhatsApp. Other systems are connected through their APIs where they expose one.

How do you report on a department?

Each department page's forms, call taps and WhatsApp links are tagged as GA4 events carrying the department name, and Search Console shows the queries that page reached. The accounts are yours, so you can check any figure we quote.

Do you work with hospitals outside Chennai?

Yes. The work is remote for hospitals anywhere in India, with calls and WhatsApp for reviews and approvals. In-person meetings are in Chennai, with travel elsewhere in Tamil Nadu by arrangement.
