What medical equipment lead generation covers

Most equipment businesses lose enquiries in two places. Before the enquiry, because there is no page for the product the buyer searched for. After it, because the enquiry arrived as a WhatsApp message on a sales person's phone and no one else ever saw it.

This service handles both ends. The pages come first: product, application and city pages that answer a real search. Then the capture: an enquiry form, a call button and a WhatsApp link on every page, each tagged so the page and query that produced it are recorded. Then the handover: the enquiry becomes a lead in Aura Business with its source, assigned to an owner, visible on the pipeline.

We do not promise a number of leads. Nobody can honestly forecast enquiry volume for a business before doing the work. What is committed is the pages, the tracking, the capture and reporting you can read yourself.

Questions

Medical equipment lead generation, answered.

How many leads will we get each month?

We will not quote a number. Enquiry volume depends on your range, your market, your prices and competition, and anyone promising a figure before the work is guessing. What we commit to is the pages, the capture and the reporting.

Do you buy contact lists?

No. Every lead comes from someone who contacted you through your own site, WhatsApp or phone. Bought lists damage sender reputation, annoy buyers and cannot be attributed to anything.

How does a WhatsApp enquiry become a CRM lead?

The WhatsApp link carries the product and page it came from in the prefilled message, and the tap is recorded as a GA4 event. The conversation is then logged as a lead in Aura Business with contact, product interest and source.

We already use a CRM. Can you work with it?

Yes, where it exposes an API for creating leads. Where there is no CRM, Aura Business provides leads, pipeline, quotations and reminders in one place.

Can you track calls as well as forms?

Taps on the call button are recorded as GA4 events with the page, which covers mobile visitors. Calls dialled manually from a hoarding or a card cannot be attributed to a page, and we say so rather than overclaiming.

Is this different from your SEO service?

It includes SEO and adds the capture and CRM side. If you only need pages and technical work, see medical equipment SEO. If enquiries are already arriving and being lost, this is the service.
