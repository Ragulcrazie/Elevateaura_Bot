Why local matters for a healthcare app

Some things about a hospital cannot be learned on a video call.

The workflow an app must fit is physical: where the queue forms, where the signal drops, where the engineer parks.

The real queue

Seeing how tokens are actually called at your OP counter shapes the patient app better than any requirements document.

Dead zones

Walking the basement imaging suite or the ICU shows exactly where offline mode must work.

The service bay

Watching engineers load spares at 8 am tells us what the job card must capture and what it must not ask.

Staff who will use it

A nurse or front-desk assistant tries a working build in person and tells us what is wrong, in Tamil or English.

Someone who turns up

When the app matters to the hospital, a developer who can be at your site the same day is a different kind of support.
