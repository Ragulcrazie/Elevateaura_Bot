What this service is

Elevate Aura is a founder-led software studio in Chennai that designs, builds and publishes mobile apps for healthcare and for the businesses that supply it. The apps run on Android and iOS from one codebase in React Native or Flutter, use a Supabase or Azure backend, and are released to the Play Store and App Store as part of the job.

The work is scoped around a real workflow rather than a feature list: a patient booking an appointment, a doctor clearing a consult queue, a service engineer closing a job card in a hospital basement with no signal. Where the app needs a system behind it, it connects to HIMS, AuraPACS or Aura Business, or to the backend you already run.

The pages below cover each type of app in detail. Every one of them ends in the same place: a call with the person who will actually write the code.

Questions

Healthcare mobile app development, answered.

Which platforms do you build for?

Android and iOS from a single codebase in React Native or Flutter. Release to Google Play and the App Store is part of the delivery, along with the developer account setup if you do not have one yet.

Do you build the backend as well as the app?

Yes. Most apps run on a Supabase backend with authentication, a Postgres database and realtime updates, or on Azure where that is preferred. If the app must sit on HIMS, AuraPACS or Aura Business, it connects to those directly.

Which apps have you actually shipped?

A React Native field-staff app for Carna Medicare, a medical-equipment distributor in Chennai, and Elevate Aura's own exam-prep Android app built in Flutter, live on Google Play. See the case studies.

How long does an app take?

The delivery framework is live in 30 working days where scope allows. Scope is fixed before work starts, and you review working builds during the project rather than waiting for a final handover.

Can the app work without internet?

Yes. Offline mode with local storage and background sync is a standard part of field and ward apps, so job cards, check-ins and forms are captured on site and uploaded when a connection returns.

Do you work outside Chennai?

Yes. Discovery and reviews are done in person in Chennai and remotely everywhere else, with support on WhatsApp. The app itself is deployed to the stores and the cloud, so location does not change the delivery.
