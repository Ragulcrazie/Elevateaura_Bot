What these comparisons are for

Most comparison pages exist to make one product win. These exist to help you work out which category of software your problem belongs to, which is the decision that actually costs money when it goes wrong. A centre that buys a viewer when it needed an archive, or a distributor that buys an ERP when it needed a service system, has bought a real product that solves somebody else's problem.

Every page follows the same shape: who each option is for, how each one works, a side-by-side table covering what it includes, deployment, cost model, who runs it and where it fails, and then a section on how to decide. Where an option we do not sell is the right answer, the page says so plainly.

Elevate Aura builds PACS, hospital software, an operations platform for distributors and a learning platform, so the bias is declared rather than hidden. The tables are written so you can check them against any vendor's demo.
