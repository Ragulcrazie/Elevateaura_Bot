Medical-equipment CRM FAQ

What makes a medical-equipment CRM different from a generic CRM?

It maps hospital accounts and decision-makers, tracks field visits with GPS, links quotations and orders, and connects to AMC, service and inventory, the workflows a medical-equipment distributor lives on.

Can my field reps use it on the phone?

Yes. Reps log visits with a GPS check-in and update leads from a mobile app, while managers see it live on the web dashboard.

Does it create GST quotations?

Yes. Build itemised, GST-correct quotes and convert an accepted quote into an order and a tax invoice in one click each.
