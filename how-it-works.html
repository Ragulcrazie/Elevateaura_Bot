Common questions

Good to know

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser, and your field staff use a mobile app. Nothing sits on your servers.

Can each of my staff have a login?

Yes, unlimited users on every plan. You're paying for the platform, not per person.

Is my data safe and private?

Your business's data is isolated to your account and only your team can see it. It's stored in the cloud, backed up, and reachable from anywhere.

Can I start small and add modules later?

Yes. Start with the modules you need today; switch on more whenever you like. Your price simply moves to the matching tier.

What about my existing invoices and customers in Excel?

We migrate them during setup, so you don't start from a blank screen.
