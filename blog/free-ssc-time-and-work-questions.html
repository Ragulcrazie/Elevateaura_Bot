Top Time and Work Questions for SSC Exams

English
Q1. A can do a piece of work in 10 days and B in 15 days. If they work together, in how many days will the work be completed?
Hindi
Q1. A एक कार्य को 10 दिनों में और B 15 दिनों में कर सकता है। यदि वे एक साथ काम करते हैं, तो कार्य कितने दिनों में पूरा हो जाएगा?
Solution:

Work done by A in 1 day = 1/10

Work done by B in 1 day = 1/15

Total work done by (A + B) in 1 day = (1/10 + 1/15)

= (3 + 2)/30 = 5/30 = 1/6

So, total days taken = 6 days

Correct Option: 6 days

English
Q2. A and B together can do a piece of work in 12 days. B alone can do it in 30 days. In how many days can A alone do it?
Hindi
Q2. A और B मिलकर एक कार्य को 12 दिनों में कर सकते हैं। B अकेला इसे 30 दिनों में कर सकता है। तो A अकेला इसे कितने दिनों में कर सकता है?
Solution:

Work done by (A + B) in 1 day = 1/12

Work done by B in 1 day = 1/30

Work done by A in 1 day = (1/12 - 1/30)

= (5 - 2)/60 = 3/60 = 1/20

So, A alone takes 20 days.

Correct Option: 20 days

English
Q3. A is twice as good a workman as B and together they finish a piece of work in 14 days. In how many days can A alone finish the work?
Hindi
Q3. A, B से दोगुनी कार्यकुशलता वाला एक कर्मचारी है और वे मिलकर एक कार्य को 14 दिनों में पूरा करते हैं। A अकेला उस कार्य को कितने दिनों में पूरा कर सकता है?
Solution:

Let efficiency of B = 1, then A = 2

Total work = Efficiency * Days = (2 + 1) * 14 = 3 * 14 = 42 units

Time taken by A = Total Work / Efficiency of A

= 42 / 2 = 21 days

Correct Option: 21 days

English
Q4. A does 4/5 of a piece of work in 20 days. He then calls in B and they finish the remaining work in 3 days. How long B alone would take to do the whole work?
Hindi
Q4. A एक कार्य का 4/5 भाग 20 दिनों में करता है। फिर वह B को बुलाता है और वे शेष कार्य को 3 दिनों में पूरा करते हैं। B अकेला पूरे कार्य को कितने समय में करेगा?
Solution:

A's 4/5 work = 20 days → Whole work = 20 * 5/4 = 25 days

Remaining work = 1 - 4/5 = 1/5

(A + B)'s 1/5 work = 3 days → Whole work = 3 * 5 = 15 days

B's 1 day work = 1/15 - 1/25 = (5 - 3)/75 = 2/75

B alone takes = 75/2 = 37.5 days

Correct Option: 37.5 days

English
Q5. 10 men can finish a piece of work in 10 days, whereas it takes 12 women to finish it in 10 days. If 15 men and 6 women undertake to complete the work, how many days will they take?
Hindi
Q5. 10 पुरुष एक कार्य को 10 दिनों में पूरा कर सकते हैं, जबकि 12 महिलाएं इसे 10 दिनों में पूरा कर सकती हैं। यदि 15 पुरुष और 6 महिलाएं कार्य पूरा करने का बीड़ा उठाती हैं, तो उन्हें कितने दिन लगेंगे?
Solution:

10M * 10 = 12W * 10 → 10M = 12W → 5M = 6W

Total work = 10M * 10 = 100M units

Efficiency: 15M + 6W = 15M + 5M = 20M

Time = 100M / 20M = 5 days

Correct Option: 5 days
