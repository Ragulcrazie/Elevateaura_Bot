Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

At Elevate Aura ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Telegram Bot and Website (elevateaura.co.in).

1. Information We Collect

  • Telegram Data: When you start our bot, we receive your public Telegram User ID, First Name, Last Name, and Username. We do not access your phone number unless you explicitly share it.
  • Usage Data: We track your quiz scores, time taken, subjects practiced, and interaction history to generate analytics and leaderboard rankings.
  • Payment Information: We do NOT store your credit card or bank details. All payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We only receive a transaction status (success/failure) and Order ID.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide personalized quiz content and "AI Coach" recommendations.
  • Maintain the public leaderboard (your First Name and Rank may be displayed).
  • Process subscription payments and verify premium status.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure fair competition in our "Weekly Grand Prix".

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information regarding visitors and users with our business partners and advertisers.

4. Smart Rivalry & Leaderboard

To ensure a competitive environment at all times, the leaderboard may include historical user data or simulated competitive profiles ("Smart Rivals") based on real performance patterns. Your data may be used anonymously to calibrate these systems.

5. Your Rights

You can request the deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting support via the bot.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Telegram Bot "Support" section.
