Field service FAQ

What is field service management software?

It coordinates field technicians end to end: logging service calls, dispatching the right engineer, capturing work and spares on a mobile job card, and getting customer sign-off on site.

Does it work on a phone in the field?

Yes. Technicians use a mobile app to record work and parts; managers see it live on the web dashboard.

Do spares used on a job update stock?

Yes. Spares captured on a job decrement inventory automatically and can be billed.
