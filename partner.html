What the role actually is

You are the trusted introduction. You don't build anything and you don't sell — you connect a founder to me, and I handle everything after. There are two ways the partnership pays, one of them recurring.

Aura Business (SaaS)

Recurring commission

Bring a distributor onto an Aura Business subscription and earn 20% of the first year, then a trailing share for as long as they stay. Genuine recurring income.

Custom builds & projects

One-time commission

Introduce a business that needs a custom platform, app, website or automation build, and earn 20% of the project, paid as the client pays.

Active partners

No cap on how many you introduce. The more you bring, the more you earn — with faster payouts and priority on your clients' timelines.

Straight answers

Do I have to sell anything?

No. You introduce. I sell and build. The introduction is the entire job.

What if I'm not technical?

Good — you don't need to be. You're connecting people, not writing code.

When do I get paid?

As the client pays. You're paid on collected revenue, so you and I are aligned — I pay when I'm paid.

What's this about recurring commission?

Aura Business is a subscription product. Bring a client onto it and you earn 20% of their first year, then a trailing share for as long as they stay subscribed.

How is an introduction counted as mine?

Register it before I've spoken to the client. First registered, within a 90-day window, wins — and the company must be net-new to Elevate Aura. It's all in the one-page agreement.

Is there any cost or commitment to join?

Zero. No fee, no minimum, no exclusivity — introduce to others too.

What about tax on my commission?

Commission is subject to standard Indian tax (TDS on commission, plus GST if you're registered). The partner agreement spells this out so there are no surprises.

Will this reflect badly on me if the work is poor?

Carna Medicare and Medline Robotics are live in Chennai today, supported directly by me. Your reputation is the reason I exist — I protect it harder than you do.

Ready to become a Business Development Partner?

One introduction is all it takes to start. I'll reply on WhatsApp, usually the same day.
