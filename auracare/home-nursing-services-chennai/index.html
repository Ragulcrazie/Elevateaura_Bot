Do you cover my area in Chennai?

Auracare covers Chengalpattu and the South Chennai belt along the GST Road, including Maraimalai Nagar, Singaperumal Koil, Guduvancheri and Mahindra City. Message your location on WhatsApp and we confirm quickly.

How fast can a nurse reach us?

For areas we cover, a visit can often be arranged the same day. Overnight and emergency requests are handled on priority.

Injections, IV, wound dressing, catheter and Ryles tube care, post-surgery recovery, elder and bedridden care, and overnight nursing.
