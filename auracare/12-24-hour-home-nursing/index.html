Common questions

Round-the-clock nursing, answered.

Do you offer 24 hour and overnight nursing?

Yes. Auracare arranges 12 hour, 24 hour and overnight nursing depending on the patient's needs, with emergency and overnight care available.

Who is round-the-clock nursing for?

Bedridden patients, people recovering from major surgery, critically ill or palliative patients, and anyone who needs monitoring and care through the night.

How is a 24 hour arrangement staffed?

Continuous cover is planned in shifts so the patient always has attention. We discuss the exact arrangement based on the condition and duration needed.

Can we start with just overnight care?

Yes. Many families begin with overnight care for the first risky nights after discharge, then adjust as recovery progresses.

Does this suit bedridden and chronic patients?

Yes. It pairs closely with chronic care and elderly home care.
