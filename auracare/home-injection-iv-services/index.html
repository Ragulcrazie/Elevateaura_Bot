Common questions

Home injections and IV, answered.

Do I need a prescription for injections at home?

Yes. Injections and IV medicines are given on a doctor's prescription. Keep the prescription and the medicine ready, and the nurse administers it correctly.

Can you set up an IV drip or antibiotic course at home?

Yes. For a prescribed IV course, the nurse places the cannula, runs the infusion, monitors the patient and cares for the line between doses.

Is home injection safe?

When done by a registered nurse with clean, single-use supplies and correct technique, yes. Auracare care is provided by an RN with ICU experience.

Can you give daily insulin for an elderly patient?

Yes. Insulin at the right dose, site and time, with blood sugar noted, is a common daily visit. It also pairs well with elderly home care.

Do you cover injections during post-hospital recovery?

Yes. Injectable medication is part of post-hospitalization home care.
