Common questions

Home healthcare, answered.

What is the difference between home nursing and home healthcare?

Home nursing is the clinical care a nurse provides. Home healthcare is the wider picture of caring for someone at home, built around that nursing, and including guidance on attenders, physiotherapy and equipment.

Do you provide everything yourself?

The nursing is provided directly by a registered nurse. For attenders, physiotherapy and equipment, we help you arrange and coordinate the right support rather than leaving you to figure it out alone.

Which areas do you serve?

Chengalpattu and the South Chennai belt, including Maraimalai Nagar, Singaperumal Koil, Guduvancheri and Mahindra City. See home nursing in Chennai for coverage details.

Can you plan care for a fully dependent patient?

Yes. For bedridden or seriously ill patients we build a plan around nursing, round-the-clock cover where needed, and coordination of any extra help.
