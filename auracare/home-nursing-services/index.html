Why families call us

A recovering parent at home, and no safe hands nearby.

Hospitals discharge patients earlier than families are ready for. The care does not stop at the hospital gate, it moves home, and most homes are not set up for it.

Daily procedures pile up

Injections, IV lines and dressings that need a trained hand, every single day, with no one qualified at home.

Repeated hospital trips

A weak, post-surgery or elderly patient dragged back to a clinic just for a dressing change or an injection.

Untrained attenders

A general attender cannot manage a catheter, a Ryles tube or a wound safely, and mistakes at home get serious fast.

Bedridden care is hard

Turning, hygiene, feeding tubes and bedsore prevention need skill and stamina the family often cannot sustain alone.

Nights are the worst

Emergencies and discomfort arrive after dark, exactly when clinics are shut and help feels furthest away.

No one to just ask

Families are left guessing about medication, wounds and warning signs with no clinical person to turn to.

Real situations

How a home visit usually plays out.

Post-surgery dressing

Problem: A parent discharged after surgery needs the wound dressed every second day.

At home: The nurse changes the dressing with clean technique and checks for infection signs.

Result: No painful clinic trips, and problems get spotted early.

Daily insulin for a senior

Problem: An elderly diabetic cannot self-inject and the family is unsure of dose and site.

At home: Insulin given correctly on schedule, with sugar levels noted.

Result: Stable control without a daily scramble.

Ryles tube feeding

Problem: A bedridden patient needs tube feeds and hygiene the family cannot manage.

At home: Safe feeding, tube care and repositioning to prevent bedsores.

Result: Comfortable, dignified care at home.

Overnight watch after discharge

Problem: The first nights home after hospital feel risky for the family.

At home: Overnight nursing to monitor vitals, medication and comfort.

Result: The family sleeps, knowing a nurse is watching.

Questions families ask

Home nursing, answered.

Who comes to my home for the nursing visit?

A registered nurse. Auracare is run by Janupriya Sekar, B.Sc Nursing, a Registered Nurse and Midwife with ICU experience (TNNMC Reg. No. 279663), not an untrained attender.

Which areas do you cover?

Chengalpattu and the surrounding South Chennai belt, including Maraimalai Nagar, Singaperumal Koil, Guduvancheri and Mahindra City. Message us with your location to confirm availability.

Can I book a single visit, or only a package?

Both. You can book a one-time visit for a single procedure such as an injection or a dressing change, or set up a recurring plan for ongoing care.

Do you provide overnight or 24-hour nursing?

Yes. Emergency and overnight nursing is available, and 12 or 24 hour arrangements can be planned for bedridden or post-surgery patients. See our 12 and 24 hour home nursing page.

What should we keep ready before the visit?

Any doctor's prescription or discharge summary, the medicines or supplies already prescribed, and a clean, well-lit space for the procedure. We will tell you if anything specific is needed.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp 95669 58725. Tell us the care needed and your location, and we confirm the visit, often the same day.
