The reality of caring for a parent

You love them. You also cannot be a nurse, a cook and an employee at once.

Ageing rarely comes as one clean problem. It arrives as medication, sugar, blood pressure, weak legs and long days, all at the same time.

Medication chaos

Multiple tablets, different times, easy to miss or double, with real consequences.

Falls and weakness

Chronic conditions drift

Diabetes and BP go unchecked between doctor visits until something spikes.

Bedridden strain

Turning, cleaning and feeding a bedridden parent exhausts the family.

Guilt and distance

Working children abroad or in the city cannot be there for daily care.

No one clinical to ask

Small worries about a wound or a dose have nowhere to go.

Questions families ask

Elderly home care, answered.

Is this a nurse or an attender?

A registered nurse. Auracare is run by Janupriya Sekar, B.Sc Nursing, RN and Midwife with ICU experience (TNNMC Reg. No. 279663), so clinical tasks like injections and monitoring are handled properly, not just companionship.

Can you manage my parent's diabetes and BP at home?

Yes. Insulin and medication on schedule, blood sugar and blood pressure monitoring, and clear notes on any changes to share with the doctor.

Do you help with bedridden seniors?

Yes. Turning to prevent bedsores, hygiene, feeding-tube and catheter care and mobility support are all part of elderly home care.

Can we start with a single visit?

Yes. Begin with one visit and move to a regular schedule if it helps. There is no lock-in.

Do you offer overnight care for seniors?

Yes. See 12 and 24 hour home nursing for round-the-clock arrangements.
