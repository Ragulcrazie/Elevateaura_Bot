What credit and corporate billing covers

Corporate billing is what happens when an employer, an institution or an empanelled organisation has agreed to pay for its people. The patient is treated and discharged without paying at the counter, the bill is raised against the company on credit, and the hospital then waits. HIMS records that from the moment the patient registers, so the front desk knows the payer before the first charge is posted rather than discovering it at discharge.

The part that matters most is what comes after. Bills raised on credit sit against the organisation, not against a pile of loose invoices, so accounts can see what each company owes, which bills make up that figure and what has been received. It is the same billing module that handles cash, insurance and TPA claims, so a hospital with all four kinds of payer is still looking at one set of books.

Questions

Corporate and credit billing, answered.

Is corporate billing different from TPA billing?

Yes. A TPA settles an insurance claim with pre-authorisation and claim documents. A corporate panel is a direct credit arrangement with an organisation. Both are handled in the same billing module. See TPA and insurance billing.

Can part of a bill go to the company and part to the patient?

Yes. The portion the organisation covers is billed on credit and the balance is collected from the patient on the same bill, so there is no second invoice and nothing to reconcile afterwards.

How do we see what a company owes us?

Outstanding is held per organisation, with the bills that make it up and the receipts recorded against them. That figure also appears alongside TPA and cash on the MIS dashboard.

Can we bill a package to a corporate panel?

Yes. A package can be billed to an organisation on credit, with the inclusions resolving the same way they do for a cash patient. See package billing.

Does it handle OP consultations on credit, not just admissions?

Yes. An empanelled employee coming to OP is registered against the organisation and the consultation is billed to the panel, which is often the bulk of corporate volume.

Is GST applied on corporate bills?

Billing in HIMS is GST-native, and tax is applied where your accountant says it applies using rules configured at setup. What the correct treatment is for a given corporate arrangement is a question for your auditor rather than for the software.
