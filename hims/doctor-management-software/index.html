Common questions

Doctor management, answered.

Does it manage doctor schedules?

Yes. Each doctor has their own roster and availability, and patients can only book open slots, so double-booking stops.

Can doctors write e-prescriptions?

Yes. Doctors write structured e-prescriptions tied to the patient record, which the pharmacy and the patient can see.

Does it track consultation share or payouts?

Yes. Consultation counts and revenue share per doctor are calculated automatically, so visiting-consultant payouts are clear.

Is it good for visiting consultants?

Yes. Visiting consultants get their own schedule, queue and share report without needing a full-time desk.

Does it connect to patient records?

Yes. Every consult, note and prescription is saved to the patient's unified record inside the HIMS.
