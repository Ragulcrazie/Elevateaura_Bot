Hospital software in Chennai, answered.

Do you provide on-site setup in Chennai?

Yes. Elevate Aura is based in Chennai, so setup, staff training and support can happen in person, not only over calls.

Who builds and supports the software?

It is founder-led. You work directly with the builder, Ragul Sekar, rather than a call-centre support queue.

Have you worked with Chennai healthcare businesses?

Yes. We deliver for Carna Medicare, a Chennai medical-equipment distributor, and Medline Robotics, a Chennai medical robotics firm.

What does the hospital software cover?

OP and IP registration, appointments, EMR, pharmacy, laboratory, inventory, GST and TPA billing, and AuraPACS imaging, in the one hospital system.

Is it only for large hospitals?

No. It scales from clinics and diagnostic centres to mid-size hospitals, and you switch on only the modules you need.
