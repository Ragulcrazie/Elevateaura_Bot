Common questions

Hospital billing, answered.

Does it handle cash, insurance and TPA together?

Yes. Cash billing, insurance and TPA claims run on one system, tied to the patient's treatment record, so the front office is not juggling separate books.

Is the billing GST compliant?

Yes. Invoices follow GST rules with correct tax and numbering, so your accounts and audits are clean.

How does it reduce claim rejections?

Because billing is tied to the clinical record, claim documents pull from the actual treatment, reducing mismatches that get claims rejected.

Does it support packages and deposits?

Yes. Surgical packages, advance deposits and running IP bills are supported, with a clear final bill at discharge. It is part of the full hospital system.

Can we see collections and outstanding?

Yes. Collections, outstanding and pending claims are visible on a dashboard, so nothing sits uncollected.
