Common questions

Hospital management system, answered.

What does the hospital management system cover?

The full patient journey: OP and IP registration, appointments and queue, doctor and nursing workflows, EMR, pharmacy, laboratory, inventory and insurance and GST billing, on one connected platform.

Does it include an EMR?

Yes. A structured EMR captures history, orders, notes and reports, designed to support NABH-style documentation, and links to the patient record across departments.

Can it handle insurance and TPA billing?

Yes. Insurance and TPA billing sits alongside GST-compliant cash billing, so the front office runs on one system. See hospital billing.

Does it work with imaging?

Yes. AuraPACS, our own browser-based PACS, integrates so radiology images and reports live alongside the patient record.

Is it ABHA and ABDM ready?

Yes. The platform is built to support ABHA linking and ABDM-aligned workflows for digital health records.

How long does it take to go live?

Configured to your hospital and live on a 30-working-day framework where scope allows, with founder-led delivery.
