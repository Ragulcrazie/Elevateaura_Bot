What a UHID is and why it comes first

A UHID is the unique hospital identification number a patient is given the first time they walk in. It is not a case number for that visit and not an admission number for that stay. It belongs to the person, and every visit, admission, prescription, laboratory order, imaging study and bill afterwards hangs off it. In HIMS the UHID is created at registration and never created again for the same person.

This is the least glamorous module in a hospital system and the one everything else depends on. If a patient can end up with three identification numbers, then the history is split three ways, the follow-up doctor sees a third of the story, and the outstanding on one of the three is invisible. Getting registration right is what makes a single patient record possible at all.

Questions

UHID and registration, answered.

What exactly is a UHID?

The unique hospital identification number issued to a patient at first contact. Visits, admissions, prescriptions, laboratory orders, imaging studies and bills all attach to it, which is what allows one continuous record instead of a file per visit.

How does the system stop duplicate records?

Registration begins with a search on phone number or name, so the counter is shown existing patients before a new UHID is created. Making the search quicker than retyping is what actually prevents duplicates at a busy desk.

Can OP and IP share the same number?

Yes. An admission is created against the existing UHID rather than as a separate identity, so the OP consultation that led to the admission and the ward notes afterwards sit on one record. See IPD software.

What if we already have thousands of patients on paper or in Excel?

Your existing patient data is migrated in during setup, so returning patients are found rather than re-registered. Records that were already duplicated in the old system are the ones to clean before migration.

Is the UHID related to ABHA?

They are different things. The UHID is your hospital's own identifier for the patient. HIMS is built to support ABHA linking and ABDM-aligned workflows alongside it. Read what ABHA and ABDM ready means.

Does the patient see their UHID?

Yes. It is given at registration and is how the patient is identified at every counter afterwards, including in the patient portal where their reports are visible.
