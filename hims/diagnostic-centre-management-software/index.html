In one sentence

What diagnostic centre management software does

Diagnostic centre management software has to do three things at once: register and bill patients at the front desk, run the laboratory from order to verified result, and handle imaging from the modality to the reported study. Most centres run these on three systems. HIMS runs them on one record, with the laboratory module (LIMS) and the AuraPACS imaging platform built in and GST billing shared by both.

The difference shows on a combined visit. A patient referred for a blood panel and an ultrasound is registered once, billed once, and their lab report and imaging report are both on the same record and both shareable to the patient and the referring doctor by WhatsApp link. For imaging-heavy centres, the AuraPACS diagnostic centre page covers the PACS side in depth.

The three-system problem

A diagnostic centre is a lab, a scan centre and a billing counter pretending to be one business.

Each side works. The seams between them are where the patient waits and the money leaks.

Registered twice, billed twice

The lab software and the scan register each have their own patient entry and their own receipt.

Reports collected from two rooms

The patient waits for a printed lab report at one counter and a film or CD at another.

Referrers chase by phone

The referring doctor calls to ask whether the report is ready because nothing is sent to them.

Results released unverified

Without a verification step, a result reaches the patient before a pathologist has looked at it.

Imaging in a separate world

The radiologist reports on a workstation that the front desk cannot see, so the report is typed again for the patient.

Questions

Diagnostic centre management software, answered.

Do we need HIMS and AuraPACS separately?

No. AuraPACS is built into HIMS, so the imaging side and the laboratory side share the front desk, the patient record and billing. A centre that only does imaging can start with AuraPACS alone; see diagnostic centre PACS.

Which modalities does the imaging side support?

AuraPACS receives DICOM from CT, MR, X-ray (CR and DR), ultrasound, mammography and more. Studies land automatically from the machine, and the modality worklist means the study is scheduled once and appears on the scanner.

How are reports shared with patients and referring doctors?

Lab and imaging reports sit on the patient record and can be sent by a secure WhatsApp link that opens without a login. The patient portal also shows reports.

Is the radiologist's read part of the software?

No. AuraPACS stores, displays and shares imaging; it is not a diagnostic device. Your radiologist reads the study in the browser viewer and finalises the structured report to PDF. See laboratory management for the pathology side.

Does it work if our internet is unreliable?

Yes. AuraPACS is hybrid: an on-premise node on your network receives and displays studies without internet, and a thin cloud layer handles remote viewing and encrypted offsite backup when the connection is available.

Is billing GST-compliant for a diagnostic centre?

Billing is GST-native, with one invoice covering lab and imaging services and tax applied where your accountant says it applies. Configuration is done during setup; this is a billing feature, not tax advice.
