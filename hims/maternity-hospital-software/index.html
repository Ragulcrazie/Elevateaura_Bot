In one sentence

What makes maternity different from general inpatient care

Maternity is the most predictable inpatient work a hospital does. The patient is known months before she is admitted, she is seen repeatedly during that time, the admission has an expected date, the stay has an expected length, and the charge is usually agreed as a package rather than counted item by item at the end. Hospital software for maternity is therefore less about emergency capacity and more about holding one long record and billing a stay that was priced before it started.

HIMS handles this with the same parts it uses everywhere: one UHID that carries every antenatal visit, appointments that book the next review before the patient leaves, bed and theatre scheduling for the admission, package billing for the stay, and a second patient record for the newborn linked to the mother's. The newborn is described here as a linked record, because that is what it is. There is no separate neonatal module on record, and anything beyond a linked record is scoped on the call.

Questions

Maternity hospital software, answered.

Does HIMS have an obstetrics module?

HIMS is a general hospital system configured for the way a maternity hospital works, which is long planned stays, months of antenatal visits on one record and package billing. It is not sold as a specialty product, and anything specialty-specific is scoped on the call rather than claimed here.

How is the baby recorded?

The newborn is registered as a patient with their own record, linked to the mother's admission, so notes and a discharge summary belong to the baby. This is a linked record rather than a neonatal module, and further requirements are discussed during scoping.

Can a delivery package be billed as one item?

Yes. The package sits on the admission and anything provided outside it is billed separately and visibly. See hospital package billing software for how packages and extras are handled.

Will antenatal visits stay on the same record for months?

Yes. Every visit attaches to one UHID, so a consultant opening the record sees the whole sequence of visits, investigations and prescriptions rather than the most recent one.

Does it handle insurance and TPA for a delivery?

Yes. Pre-authorisation is recorded at admission and claim documents come from the record at discharge. See TPA billing software.

Can patients book and see reports themselves?

Yes. The patient app and portal show upcoming appointments and reports, and reminders for reviews can go out on WhatsApp with templates agreed at setup.
