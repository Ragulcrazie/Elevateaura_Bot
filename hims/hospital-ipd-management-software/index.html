What hospital IPD software does in HIMS

Hospital IPD software handles everything that happens between admission and discharge: allocating a bed, recording doctor orders and ward rounds, keeping nursing charts, issuing drugs and investigations against the admission, running the IP bill as charges accrue, coordinating TPA pre-authorisation and producing the discharge summary. In HIMS the inpatient workflow is a continuation of the same record that OP or casualty created.

The difference from an OP visit is time and money. An admission runs for days, involves several shifts of staff and accumulates charges from pharmacy, lab, OT and the ward. HIMS keeps that running total visible so an interim bill can be printed for the family or the TPA at any point, and the final bill at discharge is what the record says, not what someone reconstructed.

Where admissions leak

Inpatient care is where documentation, nursing and money all meet.

The longer the stay, the more places a charge or an order can fall through.

Bed given, register not updated

The ward knows which bed is taken; the front desk does not, so a new admission is promised a bed that is already occupied.

Orders lost between shifts

The evening consultant's verbal order is not on the chart when the night nurse comes on duty.

Charges missed on the final bill

Dressings, injections and a second scan were done but never reached billing, so the hospital absorbs them.

TPA pre-auth on paper

Pre-authorisation is chased by phone and fax, and the status is known only to one person.

Discharge summary written last

The summary is typed on the day of discharge from a file, delaying the patient and missing details.

In practice

What changes on the ward.

A planned surgical admission

Workflow: Admitted with a package and a deposit, bed allocated, OT slot booked against the admission, consumables and implants charged as used.

Result: The package bill and the extras are visible before discharge.

A night-shift handover

Workflow: The evening orders are on the record; the night nurse charts vitals and medication against them.

Result: Nothing depends on a verbal handover.

A family asking for the bill on day three

Workflow: The ward prints an interim statement showing room, pharmacy, lab and procedures to date.

Result: No surprise at discharge.

A TPA case

Workflow: Pre-authorisation raised at admission, enhancement requested as the stay extends, claim documents pulled from the same record.

Result: The claim matches the treatment.

Questions

Hospital IPD software, answered.

Can an OP visit be converted to an admission?

Yes. An admission can be raised from an OP consultation, from the casualty triage board or directly at the front desk. In each case it continues on the same UHID, so history, orders and previous bills are already attached.

Does the bed board update automatically?

When a bed is allocated at admission, transferred or released at discharge, the ward and bed occupancy view updates for everyone, so the front desk and the ward see the same free beds. See bed management software.

Can we print an interim bill mid-stay?

Yes. The IP bill runs as charges accrue from pharmacy, laboratory, OT and the ward, and an interim statement can be printed at any point for the family or the TPA.

How is TPA pre-authorisation handled?

Pre-authorisation is raised against the admission with the payer recorded, and claim documents are prepared from the treatment record at discharge. See TPA and insurance billing software.

Where does the discharge summary come from?

It is generated from what the record already holds: diagnosis, procedures, investigations, medication and follow-up advice. The consultant reviews and signs it rather than typing it from a file.

Does IPD include the ICU and OT?

Yes. ICU beds with live vitals per bed and operation theatre scheduling are both linked to the admission, so their charges and notes land on the same IP record.
