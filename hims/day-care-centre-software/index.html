Why day care does not fit OP or IP neatly

Day care sits awkwardly between the two workflows most hospital systems are built around. It is more than an outpatient consultation, because the patient occupies a place, receives a procedure or an infusion and is observed. It is less than an inpatient stay, because there is no overnight and the same chair or bed may take two or three patients before the unit closes. Treated as OP it loses the record; treated as IP it clogs the ward view.

HIMS handles it as a short admission. The patient is admitted under their existing UHID, given a place in the unit, treated and discharged the same day, with the clinical record, the drugs and consumables used and the observations all on that episode. Billing then works the way day care is actually sold, as a package covering the procedure rather than a list of departmental charges the patient never agreed to.

Four things a day care unit stops improvising.

Two patients on one chair

Workflow: The morning session is discharged, the place shows free and the afternoon patient is admitted to it.

Questions

Day care software, answered.

Is a day care episode an OP visit or an admission?

It is handled as a short admission, which is what keeps the clinical record, the consumables and the observations attached to the episode. The patient is admitted and discharged on the same day rather than being recorded as a consultation.

Do you have a dialysis module?

No. HIMS does not carry specialty-specific modules, and it would be dishonest to claim one. What it does have is the generic short-stay flow described here: scheduled sessions, a place in the unit, the record, consumables and package billing. Tell us how your unit runs and we will say plainly what fits.

Can we see which chairs or beds are free?

Yes. Places in the day care unit are tracked the way ward beds are, so the unit can see what is occupied and what has freed up as sessions finish. See bed management.

How are repeat patients handled?

Every session is an episode under the same UHID, so a patient attending regularly builds one continuous history rather than a series of unrelated entries. See UHID registration.

Can the session be billed as a package?

Yes. The package is defined with its inclusions and billed as one item, with anything outside it shown separately. Read package billing software for how inclusions are set up.

Can we run day care alongside regular wards?

Yes. Day care is part of the same platform as the wards, the theatre and OP, so a hospital with a day care wing runs both on one patient record rather than two systems.
