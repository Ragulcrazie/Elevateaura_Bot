In one sentence

Why a physiotherapy clinic does not fit ordinary clinic software

Almost every clinic system is built around an episode: the patient arrives, is seen, is billed and goes home. Physiotherapy does not work that way. The patient is assessed once and then attends ten or twelve times on a repeating slot, often paying for the whole course at the start. The unit of work is the course, and the questions that matter are how many sessions are left, whether the patient is turning up, and what was done at each one.

HIMS is configured for that. Appointments are booked as a repeating series on a therapist's roster instead of one at a time. Each session carries its own note and its attendance against the same patient record. Billing uses package billing, so the course is charged once and sessions are recorded against it rather than generating a fresh bill each afternoon. How the remaining balance of sessions is presented at the desk is agreed during scoping.

The course problem

A diary and a receipt book cannot tell you how many sessions are left.

The clinic sells a course, then manages it one afternoon at a time.

Sessions counted on a card

The patient carries a card with ticks on it, and the clinic has no count of its own.

Money taken before the work

A course is paid for up front, so the takings and the treatment delivered fall in different months with nothing linking them.

The same slot, twelve times

Booking a repeating appointment means writing the same name in the diary twelve times and correcting it every time something moves.

Drop-outs discovered late

A patient stops attending after session four and nobody notices until the card turns up.

Therapist time is the real constraint

Rooms and hands are limited, and the diary cannot show who is free when a patient wants to reschedule.

Questions

Physiotherapy clinic software, answered.

Is HIMS a physiotherapy product?

No. HIMS is a general hospital system configured for the way a physiotherapy clinic works, which is courses of sessions, repeating slots and packages paid in advance. It is not a specialty product, and any specialty-specific requirement is settled during scoping.

Can we bill a course up front and deliver it over weeks?

Yes. The course is raised as a package against the patient and billed once, with sessions recorded against it as they are delivered. How the remaining balance is shown at the desk is agreed during scoping.

Can we book the whole course in one go?

Yes. Sessions are booked as a repeating series on the therapist's roster, and an individual session can be moved without disturbing the rest of the course.

Does it record what was done in each session?

Yes. Each session carries a note and its attendance against the same patient record, so the whole course reads in sequence rather than as loose sheets.

Does it work for a rehabilitation unit inside a hospital?

Yes. It is the same platform, so a course of therapy sits on the record that already holds the admission and the surgery. See orthopaedic hospital software.

Can patients be reminded about sessions?

Yes. WhatsApp reminders can go out before a session with templates agreed at setup, and the patient app shows upcoming sessions. Missed sessions appear on the list for the desk to follow up.
