Common questions

Clinic management, answered.

Is this simpler than a full hospital system?

Yes. Clinic management is a lighter version focused on appointments, patient records, prescriptions and billing, without the inpatient and ward complexity a hospital needs.

Can patients book appointments online?

Yes. Patients can book slots online and receive WhatsApp reminders, which cuts no-shows and phone calls.

Does it do e-prescriptions?

Yes. Doctors write e-prescriptions from the patient record, which can be printed or shared with the patient.

Is billing GST compliant?

Yes. Consultation and procedure billing is GST-ready, with a clear record per patient.

Can a multi-doctor clinic use it?

Yes. Multiple doctors, rooms and specialities are supported, each with their own schedule and queue.
