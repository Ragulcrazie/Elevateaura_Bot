What WhatsApp reminders do for a hospital

A reminder is only useful if it comes from the appointment, not from a list somebody keeps separately. HIMS sends appointment confirmations, reminders before the visit and follow-up nudges over WhatsApp using the appointment book and the patient record as the source, so there is no export, no second contact list and nothing to keep in step.

The reason to use WhatsApp rather than a phone call is simply that it gets read and it does not need anyone at the desk to make it happen. The reason to use it rather than a mass broadcast is that each message relates to a real appointment for a real patient under their UHID. Fewer patients drift away after the first visit when the review that is due actually reaches them.

Why follow-ups are lost

Nobody decides to stop coming. They just are not reminded.

The visit that never happens leaves no trace, which is why most hospitals underestimate it.

Reminder calls that do not happen

Calling every patient before the clinic is somebody's job until the day gets busy, which is every day.

Review dates only the doctor knows

The consultant says come back in six weeks, it is written on a slip, and nothing in the hospital tracks it.

Contact lists kept separately

A marketing sheet of phone numbers drifts out of date from the day it is exported.

Empty slots discovered on the day

The clinic runs light and the reason is never recorded anywhere.

Broadcasts that annoy

Untargeted messages go to everyone, and patients stop reading the ones that matter.

Questions

WhatsApp reminders, answered.

Do reminders go from our own number?

Yes. Messages are sent from your hospital's WhatsApp Business number so the patient recognises who is writing. Setting that up with WhatsApp, including the message templates, is part of the project and is agreed with you before go-live.

Will this reduce no-shows?

Patients who are reminded turn up more reliably than patients who are not, which is the whole reason to do it. We do not publish a figure for it, because the honest answer depends on your specialty, your patients and how far they travel.

Where do the phone numbers come from?

From the patient record created at registration under the UHID, so there is no separate contact list to maintain. See UHID registration for how that record is built.

Can we send follow-up reminders, not just appointment reminders?

Yes. When the doctor sets a review date in the record, the patient can be nudged when it falls due, which is usually where more visits are lost than at the booked appointment.

Can patients reply?

Messages come from your own business number, so replies land with your front desk like any other WhatsApp conversation. How you staff that is worth deciding before you switch reminders on.

Is this the same as sending reports on WhatsApp?

Related but separate. Imaging reports are shared through AuraPACS as a secure link that needs no login. See WhatsApp medical report sharing for that workflow.
