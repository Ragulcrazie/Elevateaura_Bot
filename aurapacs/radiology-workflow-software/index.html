Common questions

Radiology workflow, answered.

How does it improve report turnaround?

Every study has a visible status from registered to acquired to reported to verified, so bottlenecks are obvious and nothing waits unnoticed in a queue.

Can we see where each study is?

Yes. A live worklist shows the stage of every study, who it is with and how long it has been waiting.

Does it distribute reports automatically?

Yes. Once verified, the report and images are shared with the patient and referring doctor as a secure WhatsApp link.

Is verification and sign-off supported?

Yes. Reports move from draft to verified with sign-off, so only approved reports go out. It builds on RIS PACS.

Does it connect the whole department?

Yes. Front desk, technologists and radiologists share one workflow inside AuraPACS.
