Common questions

PACS in Chennai, answered.

Are you based in Chennai?

Yes. AuraPACS is built by Elevate Aura, a Chennai studio, so for Chennai clinics, centres and hospitals we can come on site to set up and support the system.

Do you set it up on our premises?

Yes. We install the on-premise node, connect your modalities, brand the system and train your team, in person where you are in Chennai.

How fast can we go live in Chennai?

About 30 working days, and being local means the on-site setup and any support visits are quicker to arrange.

Can we meet before deciding?

Yes. Being founder-led and in Chennai, you can talk directly to the person who builds and supports it, and see a live demo.

Do you support centres outside Chennai?

Yes. AuraPACS works anywhere over the cloud, and we handle setup remotely for locations outside Chennai. On-site visits are easiest within Chennai.
