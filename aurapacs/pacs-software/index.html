Common questions

PACS software, answered.

What is AuraPACS?

AuraPACS is a Picture Archiving and Communication System (PACS): software that stores medical images from your scanners, lets you view them in any web browser, and share reports with patients and referring doctors on WhatsApp.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. AuraPACS is hybrid. A node runs on your premises so your scanners and staff reach it over the local network even if the internet drops, with a thin cloud layer for remote viewing and encrypted backup. See cloud PACS.

Do I need to install special viewer software?

No. The viewer runs in a standard web browser, so any authorised computer can open studies without installing heavy DICOM software.

Which modalities does it support?

AuraPACS accepts DICOM from common modalities such as CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound and CR/DR, connected through a modality worklist.

How fast can we go live?

In about 30 working days. We set up the on-premise node, connect your modalities, brand it and train your team.

Can I try it before deciding?

Yes. There is a live demo you can open in your browser to see the viewer and workflow before you commit.
