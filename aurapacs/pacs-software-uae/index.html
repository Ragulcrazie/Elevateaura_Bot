In one sentence

What AuraPACS offers a UAE centre

AuraPACS is a hybrid PACS: an on-premise node on your clinic's network that receives, stores and serves studies, plus a cloud layer for remote reading and encrypted offsite backup. For the UAE it is deployed remotely. Your IT person installs the node with guidance from Elevate Aura, your CT, MR, X-ray or ultrasound is pointed at it, and reading happens in a browser.

Elevate Aura is a founder-led studio in Chennai, India. There is no UAE office, and this page does not claim one. What it offers is a product that runs on your own hardware, a builder you talk to directly on WhatsApp in English, and a time-zone overlap that makes same-day support practical.

How deployment works

Remote deployment to a UAE clinic, honestly described

Nothing on this page requires anyone to fly. It does require one capable IT person at your end.

The on-premise node

AuraPACS installs on a Windows machine on your network. Your IT person runs the installer, sets a static IP, opens the DICOM port and gives each modality the node's address and AE title, guided step by step over WhatsApp and screen share. A test send from each machine confirms studies land and open in the browser. See DICOM server software for what is configured.

The cloud layer

Once the node is live, an encrypted copy of each study syncs to the cloud layer. That is what enables a radiologist to read from home or a second site in a browser, and what produces the secure links you can send to referrers and patients.

Support and time zones

Support is on WhatsApp in English from the person who built the product. The UAE is a short offset from India, so a question sent during your working day is answered during ours.

Health-information exchange and data residency

Emirates in the UAE operate health-information exchange programmes, and licensed facilities may have obligations about connecting to them and about where patient data is stored. AuraPACS keeps images on your own node by default, with an encrypted offsite copy. Before deployment, confirm your integration, data-residency and health-record obligations with your licensing authority and advisers, and raise them on the scoping call so the deployment is planned around them.

Invoicing

Billing in AuraPACS is built for Indian GST. For a UAE centre, invoicing is configurable and the requirements are confirmed on a call rather than assumed.

In practice

Where a remotely deployed PACS fits in the UAE.

A polyclinic with X-ray and ultrasound

Workflow: The node is installed by the clinic's IT in an afternoon; both units send to it; doctors open images in the consulting rooms.

Result: Filmless viewing without a server room or a site visit.

An imaging centre with a part-time radiologist

Workflow: Studies sync to the cloud layer; the radiologist reads from home in a browser and signs reports on the study.

Result: Reporting continues on days the radiologist is not on site.

A centre evaluating before committing

Workflow: The team opens the live demo in a browser and runs the 200 KB Windows demo installer against sample studies.

Result: A decision made on the working product.

A centre with exchange obligations

Workflow: Integration and residency requirements are raised on the scoping call and confirmed with advisers before the node is installed.

Result: No surprises after go-live.

Questions

PACS software in the UAE, answered.

Do you have an office or partner in the UAE?

No. Elevate Aura is a founder-led studio in Chennai, India. UAE centres are served remotely: your IT installs the node with guidance, and support is on WhatsApp in English.

Where is our patient data stored?

On the on-premise node in your clinic by default, with an encrypted offsite copy through the cloud layer. Confirm your data-residency obligations with your advisers before deployment and raise them on the scoping call.

Does AuraPACS connect to the emirate's health-information exchange?

Exchange programmes and their requirements vary by emirate and facility licence. Confirm what applies to you with your licensing authority, and bring it to the scoping call so the deployment can be planned around it. Nothing on this page should be read as a claim of a specific integration.

What does our IT person need to do?

Install the node on a Windows machine, set a static IP, open the DICOM port and enter the node's address and AE title on each modality. Each step is guided remotely. See on-premise PACS.

Can our radiologist read from outside the clinic?

Yes. Synced studies open in a browser for authorised users, with role-based access and every view audited. See remote reading software.

Is AuraPACS a medical device?

AuraPACS stores, displays and shares imaging; the read is by your radiologist. Regulatory classification of imaging software differs by country, so confirm the position in the UAE with your advisers.
