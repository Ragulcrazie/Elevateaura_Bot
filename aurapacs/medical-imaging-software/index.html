Common questions

Medical imaging software, answered.

What does medical imaging software do?

It captures images from your scanners, stores them safely, lets authorised staff view them in a browser, and shares reports with patients and referring doctors. AuraPACS does all of this in one platform.

Which imaging types does it handle?

DICOM studies from CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound and CR/DR, connected through a modality worklist.

Can patients get their scans digitally?

Yes. Instead of a CD, patients and referring doctors receive a secure WhatsApp link to open the study and report.

Is it different from PACS?

Medical imaging software is the broader term. AuraPACS is the PACS that delivers it, with viewer, worklist, reporting and sharing built in.

How long to set up?

About 30 working days to connect modalities, brand it and train your team.
