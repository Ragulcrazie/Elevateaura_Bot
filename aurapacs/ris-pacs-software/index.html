Common questions

RIS PACS, answered.

What is the difference between RIS and PACS?

PACS stores and displays the images. RIS manages the workflow around them: registration, scheduling, the worklist, reporting and billing. AuraPACS brings both together so they share one record.

Does the report link to the images?

Yes. Structured reports are created against the study, so the report and the images stay together and are shared together.

Can it handle billing?

Yes. GST-ready billing is part of the platform, tied to the study and the patient, so imaging and the invoice are not separate systems.

Does it schedule appointments?

Yes. Registration and scheduling feed the modality worklist, so the patient's details flow to the scanner without re-typing.

Is this suitable for a diagnostic centre?

Yes. See diagnostic centre PACS for how it fits an imaging centre end to end.
