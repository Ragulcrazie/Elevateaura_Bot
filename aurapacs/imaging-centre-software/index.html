What imaging centre software should cover

Imaging centre software is the system that runs the day at a scan centre: booking the patient, sending the details to the scanner, receiving the study, reading and reporting it, billing the patient and getting the result to the referrer. AuraPACS covers that whole line in one product, with a browser interface and a role for the front desk, technician, radiologist and referrer.

Most centres run this on three or four disconnected things: a register or spreadsheet for bookings, the scanner console for images, a document for reports and a separate billing package. Every hand-off between them is a retyped name. Putting the steps in one system is what removes the retyping and the lost studies.

The disconnected centre

Four tools, four copies of the patient's name.

The problems at a scan centre are rarely about any single tool. They come from the gaps between them.

Bookings in a register

The day's appointments live in a book or a spreadsheet the technician cannot see from the scanner.

Retyped at the console

The technician enters the patient again on the modality, and the spelling drifts.

Images on the scanner only

Studies stay on the console until they are deleted for space or burned to a disc.

Reports in a document

Written in Word, printed, filed by date, unconnected to the images.

Billing somewhere else

A separate billing package or a manual invoice book, with no link to the study performed.

Referrers chase by phone

The referring physician calls the desk to ask whether the report is ready.

Questions

Imaging centre software, answered.

Is this a PACS or a RIS?

Both, in one product. AuraPACS has the archive and viewer of a PACS and the scheduling, worklist, reporting and billing of a RIS, so a small centre does not buy two systems. See RIS PACS software.

Does it handle GST invoices?

Yes. GST billing and invoicing are built in, so the invoice for a study is raised from the same system that holds the study.

What about the laboratory and pharmacy?

Those sit in HIMS, which includes AuraPACS imaging. A centre that runs pathology alongside imaging should look at diagnostic centre management software.

Which staff get logins?

Everyone who needs one: front desk, technician, radiologist and referrer roles are built in, each seeing only what its role allows, with every view audited.

Does the centre need internet all day?

No. Booking, worklist, receiving, viewing, reporting and billing run on the on-premise node over the local network. The cloud layer adds remote reading, WhatsApp links and encrypted backup when the connection is available.

How long does it take to go live?

Around 30 working days where scope allows: node installed, modalities connected, templates and branding set up, staff trained. In Chennai this can be done in person; elsewhere it is done remotely.
