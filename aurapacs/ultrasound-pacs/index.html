What an ultrasound PACS does

An ultrasound PACS stores the frames and cine loops the sonologist captures during a scan, files them against the patient, and keeps the report with them so the referring doctor sees both. AuraPACS receives ultrasound as DICOM from the machine onto a node in your centre, and shares the study and report by a secure WhatsApp link.

Ultrasound is the modality most often left out of a PACS. The images are small, the scan is quick and the report is what the referrer reads, so the frames stay on the machine until it runs out of space. That is a loss for follow-up scans and for the referring gynaecologist who wants to see what the report describes.

What goes wrong with ultrasound

The report goes out. The images stay on the machine and then disappear.

An ultrasound machine's hard disk is not an archive, and a thermal print is not a record.

Frames deleted to make room

The machine fills up and the technician clears old patients. The images from the last pregnancy scan are gone.

Report without pictures

The referring gynaecologist gets a typed report and a thermal strip that fades, not the frames that back it up.

Cine loops never saved

Cardiac or fetal motion clips are looked at once on the machine and never stored.

No comparison across visits

Serial growth scans cannot be laid side by side because only the numbers were written down.

Patients photograph the screen

In the absence of a digital copy, the family takes a phone photo of the monitor.

Questions

Ultrasound PACS, answered.

Does our ultrasound machine send DICOM?

Most machines from the last several years do, either natively or with a DICOM option enabled. Once configured with the node's AE title and port it sends frames and loops at the end of each exam. The model is confirmed on a call.

Are cine loops stored as well as stills?

Yes. Whatever the machine sends as DICOM, including multi-frame loops, is received and filed under the study and can be played back in the browser viewer.

Can the referring gynaecologist see the images?

Yes. The secure WhatsApp link opens both the frames and the report PDF on a phone, with no login. Every view is audited. See WhatsApp report sharing.

Do we get obstetric report templates?

Reports are written on structured templates per modality, and ultrasound can carry separate templates for obstetric, abdominal, pelvic and other exams. The finalised PDF carries your branding and sits on the study.

Can serial growth scans be compared?

Earlier scans are in the patient's searchable history on the node and open alongside the current one in the viewer, so the sonologist compares images and not only measurements.

Is AuraPACS a diagnostic device?

No. It stores, displays and shares the images. The scan and its interpretation are by your sonologist.
