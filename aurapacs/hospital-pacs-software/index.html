Common questions

Hospital PACS, answered.

Can one PACS serve every department?

Yes. Radiology, cardiology, the OT, wards and the OPD all read from one central archive, so a study is available wherever the patient is being treated.

Can doctors view images at the bedside?

Yes. Because the viewer is browser based, an authorised clinician can open a study on any ward computer or tablet without special software.

How is access controlled?

Role-based access decides who can view and report which studies, with an audit trail of access, which matters in a hospital setting.

Does it support remote and night reporting?

Yes. The cloud layer lets on-call and remote radiologists read securely from anywhere.

Will it connect our existing modalities?

Yes. It connects to CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound and other DICOM modalities through a modality worklist.
