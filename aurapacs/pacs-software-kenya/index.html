In one sentence

What AuraPACS offers a Kenyan centre

AuraPACS is a hybrid PACS: a node inside your facility that receives DICOM from your CT, MR, X-ray or ultrasound, stores the archive and serves a browser viewer over the local network, plus a cloud layer for remote reading, secure share links and an encrypted offsite copy. Nothing on the node needs the internet; everything in the cloud layer catches up when the line is available.

Elevate Aura is the founder-led Chennai studio that builds and supports it. Kenyan centres are served remotely: your IT person installs the node with guidance over WhatsApp and screen share, and support continues on WhatsApp in English with a comfortable working-hours overlap between Nairobi and Chennai. There is no Kenyan office or partner, and this page does not claim one.

Built for the conditions

Why the hybrid model suits Kenyan imaging units

Kenya's imaging is spread across private diagnostic centres in the cities, hospital imaging departments and county facilities that are gradually adding CT and digital X-ray. The constraints are similar across all of them.

Outages

Power and connectivity interruptions are a fact of life in many facilities. The node runs on a single Windows machine on your network; when power returns it restarts and the modalities resend or re-queue their studies. Reading and reporting happen on the local network, so a lost uplink does not stop the reporting queue. The cloud sync runs in the background. See hybrid PACS for what survives each failure.

Bandwidth

The browser viewer serves studies from the node in the building, not from a server abroad, so local reading does not depend on the line. A remote radiologist opens synced studies in a browser rather than downloading whole studies to a workstation, which suits modest connections.

Referrers and patients on WhatsApp

A secure link per study opens the images and the signed report in a phone browser with no login. It is sent from the centre's system, not a staff member's phone, and every view is audited. Patients travelling to a referral hospital in Nairobi carry a link instead of a film.

One radiologist, several sites

Facilities that share a radiologist across locations can run a node at each site, with both syncing to the cloud layer so the reader sees one pending list. Each site keeps its own archive on its own hardware.

Deployment, data rules and invoicing

Your IT person installs the node, sets a static IP, opens the DICOM port and enters the node's address and AE title on each modality, each step guided remotely; see DICOM server software. Kenya has its own data-protection and health-record rules; AuraPACS keeps images on your node by default, encrypts the offsite copy and audits access, and you should confirm data-residency and retention obligations with your advisers. Billing is built for Indian GST; for Kenya invoicing is configurable and confirmed on a call.

Questions

PACS software in Kenya, answered.

Do you have an office or partner in Kenya?

No. Elevate Aura is a founder-led studio in Chennai, India. Kenyan facilities are served remotely: your IT installs the node with guidance, and support is on WhatsApp in English.

Does it keep working when the internet drops?

Yes. Receiving, the worklist, viewing and reporting run on the node over the local network. Remote reading, share links and the offsite copy wait for the connection and catch up afterwards.

Can one radiologist read for two facilities?

Yes. Each facility runs its own node and both sync to the cloud layer, so the radiologist sees a single pending list in a browser. See remote reading software.

Where is our patient data stored?

On the on-premise node in your facility by default, with an encrypted offsite copy through the cloud layer. Confirm your data-residency and health-record obligations with your advisers and raise them on the scoping call.

What does our IT person need to do?

Install the node on a Windows machine, set a static IP, open the DICOM port and enter the node's address and AE title on each modality, each step guided remotely. See on-premise PACS.

Is AuraPACS a medical device?

AuraPACS stores, displays and shares imaging; the read is by your radiologist. Regulatory classification of imaging software differs by country, so confirm the position in Kenya with your advisers.
