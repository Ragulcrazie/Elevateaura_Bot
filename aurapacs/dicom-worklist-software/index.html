Common questions

DICOM worklist, answered.

What is a DICOM modality worklist?

A modality worklist (MWL) sends scheduled patient and study details to the imaging modality, so the technologist selects the patient from a list at the scanner instead of typing the details by hand.

Why does the worklist matter?

Typing patient details at the scanner causes spelling mistakes, wrong IDs and mismatched studies. A worklist removes that by pushing the correct details from registration to the modality.

Which modalities can connect?

Common modalities such as CT, MRI, X-ray (CR/DR) and ultrasound that support the DICOM Modality Worklist service can connect.

Does the study come back to PACS automatically?

Yes. After the scan, the study is sent to the AuraPACS archive matched to the same worklist entry, so it lands under the right patient.

Is the worklist part of the RIS?

Yes. Registration and scheduling in the RIS create the worklist entries, so one record runs from the front desk to the scanner to the report.
