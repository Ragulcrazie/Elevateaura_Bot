What a medical distributor portal is

A medical distributor portal is a private website where your dealers log in to do the things they currently do by phone: check the current price list, place an order at their dealer price, see where last week's order is, download brochures and certificates, and tell you the UTR of the payment they just made.

Elevate Aura builds distributor portals in two ways. As a custom portal on Next.js and Supabase, which is how the Medline Robotics distributor portal was built alongside their storefront. Or as the Customer and Distributor Portals suite inside Aura Business, which includes a distributor order portal, UTR payment, order tracking page, quote-request inbound and a branded login with a documents vault.

Either way the portal writes to the same sales orders, stock and receivables your office works from.

The dealer network problem

Managing forty dealers on WhatsApp is a full-time job for two people.

Every dealer question is the same five questions, asked at different times of the day.

What is the current price?

Price lists are sent as PDFs and dealers quote from last quarter's version.

Where is my order?

Order status is a phone call to the office, who call the warehouse, who call back.

Orders typed twice

A dealer's WhatsApp order is retyped into the billing system, with the wrong variant.

Which payment was that?

Bank credits arrive without a reference and accounts guesses which dealer paid for which order.

Send me the brochure again

Certificates, brochures and authorisation letters are re-sent daily from someone's phone.

In practice

What changes for the distributor's office.

Price revision day

Workflow: The new price list is published in the portal; every dealer sees it at login and the old PDF stops circulating.

Result: No dealer quotes a hospital from a stale list.

A dealer in another state

Workflow: The dealer orders at 10 pm, the order arrives as a sales order with IGST applied, and the dispatch status updates in the portal.

Result: No morning phone call, no retyping.

Month-end reconciliation

Workflow: Each dealer's payments carry a UTR against a specific invoice; accounts verifies the credits in one sitting.

Result: Outstanding per dealer is accurate without a spreadsheet.

Questions

Medical distributor portal development, answered.

Custom portal or the Aura Business portal?

If you already run Aura Business, the Customer and Distributor Portals suite switches on with your existing customers, prices and stock. If you need a portal with a public storefront and a specific design, we build it custom on Next.js and Supabase, as for Medline Robotics.

Can each dealer see different prices?

Yes. Price tiers are assigned per dealer account, and product authorisation can limit which brands or lines a dealer sees. Special pricing is handled through a quote request inside the portal.

How do dealers pay?

By bank transfer, entering the UTR against the invoice in the portal, which your accounts team verifies. A payment gateway can be added for smaller orders. Outstanding and ageing are visible to the dealer and to you.

Does the portal update stock?

When connected to Aura Business, a confirmed portal order becomes a sales order and fulfilment reduces stock at the warehouse level, with batch and expiry where relevant. See inventory software.

Can we onboard new dealers through it?

Yes. A dealer application form collects the GST number, documents and territory, and an approval step in the office activates the account with the right tier.

How long does it take?

Live in 30 working days where scope allows. Your dealer list and price lists are imported from Excel and your branding goes on the login and every page.
