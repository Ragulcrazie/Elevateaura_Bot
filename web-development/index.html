What this service is

Elevate Aura designs and builds the web side of a healthcare or medical-equipment business: the public website with a structured product catalogue and enquiry forms, the B2B storefront where hospitals and dealers order, the distributor portal with dealer pricing and order tracking, and the SEO pages that bring buyers to all three.

Sites are built in WordPress where content editing by your team matters most, or in Next.js on Supabase where the site is really an application: pricing tiers, quotations, order status, UTR payment verification. Medline Robotics, a medical robotics company in Chennai, runs a storefront, distributor portal and 253 product and city landing pages built this way.

Every page below is one part of that picture. Each ends with a call to the person who will build it.

Questions

Healthcare website and e-commerce development, answered.

WordPress or Next.js, which do you use?

Both. WordPress suits a content-led site your team edits every week. Next.js on Supabase suits a site that is really an application, with logins, dealer pricing, quotations and order status. The choice is made on the discovery call and explained in the scope.

What have you built on the web?

The Medline Robotics storefront, distributor portal, quotation and ordering flow, UTR payment verification and 253 product and city SEO pages. Carna Medicare's platform included a WordPress site. And this website, which the founder designed and built. See the case studies.

Can the website connect to our CRM or ERP?

Yes. Enquiries and quote requests can land in Aura Business as leads, and orders from a storefront or portal can create sales orders and reduce stock there. Other systems can be connected through their APIs.

Do you handle SEO as well?

Yes. Site structure, schema and GA4 are part of every build, and programmatic product-line and city pages are available as a separate service. See SEO services.

How long does a site take?

Live in 30 working days where scope allows. A catalogue site is quicker than a storefront with dealer pricing and payment verification, and the scope document states the timeline for yours.

Do you work outside Chennai?

Yes. Websites and stores are built and hosted remotely for customers anywhere in India and abroad, with reviews and support on WhatsApp. In-person meetings are in Chennai.
