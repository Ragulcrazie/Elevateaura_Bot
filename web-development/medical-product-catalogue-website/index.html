What a medical product catalogue website is

A medical product catalogue website turns your price list and brochure folder into a browsable structure: product lines at the top, categories under them, and a page for every individual product holding its specification table, images, applications, accessories, the brochure as a download, and an enquiry form that names the product it came from.

Because each product has a real page with real content, it can be found directly by someone searching for that model or that application, instead of being buried in a PDF that search engines cannot rank well. Where volume justifies it, the same structure carries programmatic pages per product and per city.

Elevate Aura built this structure for Medline Robotics, a medical robotics company in Chennai, alongside their storefront and distributor portal, with 253 product and city landing pages across four product lines.

In practice

What changes once every product has a page.

A buyer comparing two models

Workflow: Both models in the same line carry the same specification fields, so the buyer reads the two pages side by side and enquires from the one that fits.

Result: An enquiry that arrives with the model already decided.

The sales team on WhatsApp

Workflow: Instead of forwarding a 20 MB PDF, the rep sends a link to the product page with the current specification and brochure.

Result: One version of the truth, and a tracked visit.

A new product line added

Workflow: The line is created, its specification fields defined once, and the products imported from the price sheet.

Result: A complete set of live pages without a redesign.

Questions

Medical product catalogue websites, answered.

How many products can the catalogue hold?

There is no practical limit. The structure is built once per product line and products are imported from your Excel sheet, so a hundred products is data entry rather than a hundred design jobs.

Do we have to show prices?

No. Most medical catalogues show specifications and take an enquiry or quote request instead of a price. Where you do want prices, they can be shown publicly, or only to logged-in dealers at their own tier.

Can buyers still download the brochure?

Yes, per product, and you choose whether the download is open or asks for a name and number first. Each download is recorded as a GA4 event so you can see which products draw interest.

Can the catalogue become a store later?

Yes. The same product data supports a quote-to-order storefront with dealer pricing and payment verification. See medical equipment e-commerce development.

Who updates the catalogue afterwards?

Your team, through the admin. Adding a product means filling the defined fields for that line and uploading the brochure. Bulk changes can still be done by re-importing the sheet.

Does the catalogue connect to stock?

It can. With Aura Business the catalogue reads the same product master, so a product added in operations appears on the site and enquiries land as leads.
