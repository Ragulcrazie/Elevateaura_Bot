What medical equipment website development covers

Medical equipment website development is the design and build of a public website for a dealer, distributor or manufacturer, organised around the products: a catalogue split by product line and category, a page per product with specifications and brochure, and an enquiry or quote form and a WhatsApp button wherever a buyer might want to ask.

Elevate Aura builds these sites in WordPress when your team will add products and news themselves, or in Next.js on Supabase when the catalogue is large or the site will grow into a storefront or distributor portal later. Either way the page structure, schema and GA4 tracking are set up so product and city pages can rank.

Enquiries do not go to an inbox that nobody checks. They can land as leads in Aura Business, with the product and page recorded against each one.

Why equipment websites underperform

Most medical equipment websites are a brochure PDF with a menu.

Hospitals search by product and city. A site that only says who you are and lists brands is invisible to them.

No page per product

One page listing forty products means no product can be found in search and none has a specification to read.

Enquiries to a generic inbox

A contact form that emails info@ with no product name, so the sales team asks the buyer what they wanted.

No WhatsApp

Purchase officers and biomedical engineers ask on WhatsApp. A site without it forces a phone call during a shift.

Brochures locked in PDFs

Specifications exist only in a downloadable brochure, invisible to search and unreadable on a phone.

Nobody can update it

A new product line waits three weeks for the agency to add a page.

Questions

Medical equipment website development, answered.

Should we use WordPress or Next.js?

WordPress if your team will edit content often and the site is mainly a catalogue with enquiries. Next.js on Supabase if the catalogue is large, you want dealer logins and pricing later, or the site will generate hundreds of product and city pages. We recommend one on the call.

Can we show prices?

That is your decision. Many dealers show specifications and take quote requests, while a storefront or dealer portal shows prices behind a login. See medical equipment e-commerce development for the ordering version.

Where do enquiries go?

To your inbox and WhatsApp, and optionally into Aura Business as leads with the product and page recorded, so the sales pipeline starts from the website. See medical equipment CRM.

Will the site rank for our products?

The structure gives each product and product line its own page with schema and clean URLs, which is the foundation for ranking. We do not guarantee positions. Programmatic product and city pages are a separate service described under medical equipment SEO.

Can our team add products themselves?

Yes. In WordPress through the editor, in Next.js through a simple admin on Supabase. Training is part of the handover.

How long does it take?

Live in 30 working days where scope allows. A catalogue site with enquiry forms usually fits inside that; large catalogues are imported from your Excel or price list rather than typed.
