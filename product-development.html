Questions

Good to know

Do you design or make the hardware?

No. This is a software-only engagement. I write the firmware, device communication and companion app to your hardware team's specification. I don't design PCBs, manufacture hardware, or do mechanical or enclosure design, and I make that boundary clear before we scope anything.

Which languages and controllers do you work with?

Embedded firmware in C/C++ or MicroPython depending on your controller, covering the device's core logic, state handling and power management.

What communication protocols do you support?

Communication layers over BLE, Wi-Fi, serial / UART, Modbus or CAN as your hardware requires, so the device talks cleanly to a phone, gateway or cloud service.

Do we own the code?

Yes. Code is delivered ready to flash to your chip, with documentation your hardware team can maintain going forward.

Do you test on our hardware?

Yes. Testing and validation are done on your provided hardware before handover.
