Questions

Inventory software FAQ

Does it track batch and expiry?

Yes. Batches and expiry dates are tracked so you sell oldest first and avoid expired stock, important for consumables and reagents.

Does stock update automatically?

Yes. A goods receipt stocks items in, and spares used on a service job decrement stock, so figures match reality.

Can it handle multiple warehouses?

Yes. Hold stock across locations and see on-hand per warehouse, with reorder alerts at the level you set.

Can I add procurement, billing and service?

Yes. Inventory connects to procurement, billing and field service. See all 87 modules.
