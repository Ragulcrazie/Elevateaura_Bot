Questions

CMC software FAQ

What is the difference between AMC and CMC?

An AMC covers service visits; a CMC also includes spare parts. Aura Business tracks both separately so you see margin and parts cost correctly.

Does CMC software track parts coverage and cost?

Yes. Spares consumed under a CMC are captured per job and netted against the contract, so you see true CMC profitability.

Will it remind me before a CMC expires?

Yes. Every CMC sits on a renewal clock with alerts, and lapsing revenue is shown in rupees.

Can I add AMC, billing and inventory too?
