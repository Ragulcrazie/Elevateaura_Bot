Questions

Healthcare CRM FAQ

What is a healthcare CRM?

A CRM built for medical businesses: it maps hospital and clinic accounts and their decision-makers, tracks field visits, and links quotations and orders, rather than just storing contacts.

Can field reps use it?

Yes. Reps log visits with a GPS check-in and update leads from a mobile app, while managers watch the pipeline live.

Does it connect to billing and service?

Yes. A won deal flows into GST billing, AMC contracts and service without re-entering anything.
