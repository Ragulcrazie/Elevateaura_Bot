Questions

Good to know

How fast can we go live?

In 30 working days. We set up your workspace with your branding and your existing Excel data, then your team logs in. Nothing to install on your computers or servers.

Do I pay per user?

No. Every plan has unlimited users. You pay by how many modules you switch on: 15, 47 or all 87.

Is my data private and secure?

Your data is isolated to your business and only your team can see it. It is stored in the cloud, backed up, and reachable from anywhere.

Can I start small and add modules later?

Yes. Start with the modules you need today and switch on more anytime. Your price simply moves to the matching tier.

Who supports me?

The person who built it. Direct on WhatsApp, and in person in Chennai. No ticket queue, no account-manager layers.
