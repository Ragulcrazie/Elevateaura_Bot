Questions

Service software FAQ

What does service management software do?

It logs every customer issue as a ticket, moves it through a clear workflow, tracks SLA response and resolution times, and keeps a service history against each asset.

Can it track SLA breaches?

Yes. Every ticket runs on a response and resolution clock, and breaches are flagged before they affect a renewal.

Does it connect to AMC and inventory?

Yes. Tickets link to contracts and assets, and spares used on a job decrement stock automatically.

Is there a mobile app for technicians?

Yes. Field staff work from a mobile job card. See field service.
