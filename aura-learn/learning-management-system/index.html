Common questions

Learning management system, answered.

What comes with the LMS?

A learner mobile app and an instructor and admin dashboard: course library, video lessons, quizzes and mock tests, certificates, live classes, batches, analytics and payments, built around your programme.

Is there a mobile app for learners?

Yes. Learners get a mobile app with offline access to lessons. See learning app development.

How many learners can it handle?

It is built for unlimited learners, so you are never billed per student as your batches grow.

Can we run live classes and quizzes?

Yes. Live classes, timed quizzes and mock tests, a doubt forum and auto certificates are all part of the platform.

Can it collect payments?

Yes. GST-ready payments and batch enrolment are built in.

Is this proven?

Yes. The same LMS stack powers Elevate Aura's own government exam-prep app, live on Google Play with automated daily practice.
