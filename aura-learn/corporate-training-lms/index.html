In one sentence

What a corporate training LMS does here

A corporate training LMS puts your company's own training material in an app your employees carry, assigns it by batch, checks understanding with an assessment and shows the training head who has finished. Aura Learn is that platform, with a learner mobile app for staff and an admin dashboard for whoever runs training.

It is built around teams that are not in an office: sales reps on the road, service engineers at customer sites, branch staff in other cities. Lessons download to the phone and play offline, progress syncs when the signal returns, and a WhatsApp reminder follows anyone who has stalled. Batches can be cut by region, role or joining date, and the dashboard reports completion per batch.

The internal training problem

Training that happens once, in one room, for whoever was free that day.

A classroom session reaches the people who could attend. Everyone hired afterwards, and everyone posted elsewhere, learns from a colleague.

Field staff never attend

The people who most need product training are on the road when the session runs at head office.

New joiners learn by rumour

Onboarding depends on which senior happens to sit next to the recruit in their first week.

No record of completion

An attendance sheet says who sat in the room, not who understood the process or can be trusted with it.

Every region trains differently

Branch managers improvise their own version, so the same product is explained five ways.

In practice

What internal training looks like when it runs itself.

A new product reaches every rep in a week

Workflow: The product module is assigned to all sales batches with a deadline; reps watch it between visits and pass the quiz.

Result: The training head sees which region has finished and which has not.

Onboarding is the same for every recruit

Workflow: New joiners are added to a monthly batch that carries the standard onboarding course.

Result: Every recruit gets the same first week regardless of branch.

A process change is trained, not emailed

Workflow: A short lesson and a quiz replace the circular; compliance tracking lists who has passed.

Result: The change is confirmed understood, not just sent.

A remote branch trains offline

Workflow: Staff download the course on office Wi-Fi and complete it at home or on the road.

Result: Poor connectivity stops being an excuse.

Questions

Corporate training LMS, answered.

Can field staff use it without a laptop?

Yes. Employees use the learner mobile app. Lessons and documents download for offline viewing, progress syncs when the phone is back online, and quizzes are taken in the same app, so a rep or engineer never needs a desk.

How are employees grouped?

Into batches you define: by region, role, branch or joining month. A course is assigned to a batch with a deadline, and the dashboard reports completion per batch so you can see which team is behind.

Is there a limit on employees?

No. Aura Learn is built for unlimited learners, so enrolling every employee, including seasonal or contract staff, does not change what you pay. Scope is agreed on a call around the modules you need.

Can it certify staff on a process or product?

Yes. A course can end in an assessment with a pass mark and a certificate, with compliance tracking that lists who has and has not passed. See the certification training platform.

Does it handle live sessions?

Yes. Live classes and webinars run in the platform with recordings kept for the batch, so a session delivered to one region can be watched later by another, and a discussion forum carries questions afterwards.

What about training service engineers on equipment?

That is a common use, especially for medical-equipment and industrial teams that must certify engineers per model before dispatch. See healthcare training for engineer and clinical-staff certification, and training management software for assigning and tracking programmes.
