Common questions

Learning app development, answered.

Do you build for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build a single app that runs on both Android and iOS, with your branding, and publish it to the Google Play Store and App Store.

Can lessons work offline?

Yes. Learners can download lessons and study offline, which matters for students on the move or with limited data.

Does it support push notifications?

Yes. Push notifications, plus WhatsApp and Telegram nudges, bring learners back for daily practice and live classes.

Can the app take payments?

Yes. Course purchases and subscriptions can be handled in-app, with access granted automatically.

Have you actually shipped a learning app?

Yes. Elevate Aura built and published its own government exam-prep app, live on Google Play, using Flutter, Python and Supabase. It runs on the same Aura Learn platform.
