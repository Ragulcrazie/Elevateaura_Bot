Common questions

Custom LMS development, answered.

When do I need a custom LMS instead of a ready one?

When your model does not fit a template: unusual workflows, specific integrations, a unique assessment or pricing model, or a scale and branding that off-the-shelf tools cannot match. If a ready platform fits, see LMS software.

Can it integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. A custom build can connect to your CRM, payment gateway, video hosting, SSO and other systems, so the LMS fits into your stack.

Do we own the platform?

Yes. It is your platform with your branding, built around your model, not a rented seat on someone else's product.

Do you build the mobile apps too?

Yes. Web dashboard and Android and iOS apps are all part of a full custom build, published to the stores.

How does the project run?

Founder-led and end to end: a discovery call, a clear scope, focused build on a 30-day framework where scope allows, then launch and support.
