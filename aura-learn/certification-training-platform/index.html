What a certification training platform does here

A certification training platform delivers a course, checks that the learner has understood it, issues a certificate when they pass and keeps that certificate on the learner's record. Aura Learn does all four inside one product: the course and content library, auto-graded assessments, certificates on completion and compliance tracking for regulated training.

The difference from a plain course platform is the record. Each learner's profile shows which courses they completed, which assessments they passed, when the certificate was issued and, where a programme has a renewal period, when it falls due. A training head can answer an auditor or a customer from the dashboard rather than from a folder of scanned certificates.

The certification problem

The training happens. Proving it later is the hard part.

Certificates made in a document template, stored on a laptop and re-issued on request are not a record anyone can trust.

Certificates with no trail

A certificate exists as a file, with nothing behind it showing what was assessed or what score was achieved.

Expiry nobody tracks

Refresher training falls due and passes unnoticed until someone asks for a current certificate.

Learners across locations

Staff in other cities complete the course on paper, and the results reach head office weeks later, if at all.

Audit day panic

An audit or a customer asks who is certified on what, and the answer is a day of searching.

In practice

Where a certification platform changes the day.

A customer asks who is certified

Workflow: The compliance view is filtered by programme and exported, showing each person, their pass date and certificate.

Result: The answer goes back the same morning.

A refresher falls due

Workflow: The programme has a renewal period; learners nearing it are listed, assigned the refresher and reminded on WhatsApp.

Result: Certificates are renewed before they lapse, not after.

A paid certificate course

Workflow: A learner enrols and pays with a GST receipt, completes the lessons, passes the assessment and downloads the certificate from the app.

Result: The provider runs the course without a single manual step.

The same programme in every branch

Workflow: One course and one assessment are assigned to batches in different cities.

Result: A certificate means the same thing wherever it was earned.

Questions

Certification training platform, answered.

How is a certificate issued?

Automatically. When a learner completes the programme and passes the assessment at or above the pass mark, the certificate is generated with your branding, stored on the learner's record and made available to download in the app.

What does compliance tracking cover?

It shows, per programme, who is certified, who is due for a refresher and who has not yet passed. It suits regulated training where you must show who was trained on what and when. It is a record of your training, not a regulatory certification of the software itself.

Can a certificate expire and be renewed?

Yes. A programme can carry a renewal period. Learners approaching it are listed, assigned the refresher and reminded on WhatsApp, and the new pass replaces the old certificate on the record.

Can learners in the field complete the course?

Yes. Lessons and downloads are available offline in the learner app, so an engineer or a field trainee can study between visits, and progress syncs when the learner is back online. The assessment needs a connection to submit.

Can we sell certificate courses?

Yes. Enrolment can take a GST payment or a subscription, and the course, assessment and certificate then run without manual steps. Batches, live classes and reminders sit on the same platform. See course management software.

Is this also used for staff assessment without a course?

Yes. A standalone assessment can be assigned and certified without lessons attached, which suits an annual refresher or a skills check for existing staff. See the online assessment platform for that use.
