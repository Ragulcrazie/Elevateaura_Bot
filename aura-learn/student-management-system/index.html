Common questions

Student management, answered.

What does the student management system cover?

Admissions and enrolment, batch allocation, attendance, fee tracking with reminders, student profiles and communication with students and parents, all linked to the courses and exams they are taking.

Can it track fees and send reminders?

Yes. Fees, instalments and dues are tracked, with automatic WhatsApp reminders so collections do not depend on someone remembering.

Does it handle batches and attendance?

Yes. Students are grouped into batches, with attendance recorded for live and offline classes and visible to staff and parents.

Is it connected to the learning content?

Yes. A student's profile links to their courses, test scores and progress, so admin and academics are not separate systems.

Can parents get updates?

Yes. Attendance, fee and progress updates can be shared with parents over WhatsApp.
