Common questions

LMS software, answered.

Is this a rented template or our own software?

It is built around your institute with your branding and your workflow. You are not squeezed into a generic template, and there is no per-learner billing.

Can it carry our brand?

Yes. The learner app and dashboard carry your name, logo and colours, so students see your brand, not ours.

Does it work on web and mobile?

Yes. Learners get a mobile app, and instructors work from a web dashboard.

Can we migrate our existing content?

Yes. Existing videos, notes and question banks can be brought into the platform during setup.

How long does it take to launch?

Configured to your programme and live on a 30-working-day framework where scope allows. For fully bespoke builds, see custom LMS development.
