What coaching institute management software does here

Coaching institute management software keeps the admissions register, the batch timetable, the fee ledger and the teaching itself in one system instead of four. In Aura Learn the institute gets an admin dashboard on the web and the learners get a mobile app, and both read from the same batch list.

A learner is admitted into a batch, pays a fee that raises a GST receipt, sees the timetable and lessons in the app, sits mock tests that are graded on submission, and gets a WhatsApp reminder when a class, a test or an instalment is due. The centre head sees attendance at live classes, test scores and outstanding fees per batch without asking anyone.

The front-office problem

A coaching centre runs on a register, a fee book and five WhatsApp groups.

The teaching is often excellent. The administration around it is where students slip, fees go uncollected and the owner loses the picture.

Fees chased by memory

Instalments are noted in a diary, so the second and third payments depend on someone remembering to ask.

Timetable changes lost

A rescheduled class is announced in one group and missed by the students who muted it.

Mock tests marked by hand

OMR sheets are checked over the weekend and the scores reach students a week late, when the topic has moved on.

No view across batches

The owner cannot say which batch is attending, which is dropping off and which has the highest fee dues.

In practice

What changes at the counter and in the classroom.

The third instalment gets collected

Workflow: The fee plan has three dates; the app reminds the learner on WhatsApp before each one and the dashboard lists who has not paid.

Result: The office asks once, with a record, instead of chasing from memory.

A Sunday mock test is scored by Sunday evening

Workflow: The test is built from the question bank, released to the batch at a set time, and graded the moment each learner submits.

Result: Students review wrong answers the same day and faculty see weak topics before Monday's class.

A timetable change reaches everyone

Workflow: The batch timetable is edited once on the dashboard and every learner in that batch sees it in the app.

Result: No one turns up to a cancelled class.

Questions

Coaching institute software, answered.

Does it handle fees and instalments?

Yes. Each learner is placed on a fee plan with due dates, payments raise a GST receipt, and the dashboard shows outstanding fees per batch. Reminders for upcoming instalments go out on WhatsApp.

Can students take mock tests in the app?

Yes. Tests are built from your question bank, released to a batch at a set time, graded on submission and reviewed in the app with instant scores. See the mock test platform for details.

Do we pay per student?

No. Aura Learn is built for unlimited learners, so a growing batch, a new course or an extra branch does not change what you pay. Pricing is scoped on a call around the modules you switch on, not the number of students.

Does it work in areas with poor connectivity?

Yes. Lessons and material can be downloaded in the learner app and watched offline, with progress syncing when the learner is back online. Live classes and timed tests need a connection while they run.

Can we run live classes?

Yes. Live classes and webinars run inside the platform, the recordings are kept for the batch so absentees can catch up, and a discussion forum handles doubts between sessions without a separate chat group.

How long does it take to go live?

Aura Learn is delivered on a 30-working-day framework where scope allows. Your batches, fee plans and content are set up for you, with your branding on the app and dashboard, and support comes directly from the founder on WhatsApp.
