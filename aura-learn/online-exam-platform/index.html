Common questions

Online exam platform, answered.

Does it support timed, sectioned exams?

Yes. Papers can be timed overall or per section, with negative marking, question shuffling and a mix of question types, matching real competitive exam patterns.

Can students see results and ranking instantly?

Yes. Scores, solutions and an all-India style ranking are shown right after submission, which drives engagement.

Can we run a mock test series?

Yes. Schedule a full series of mock tests over weeks, with a question bank behind it. It fits neatly into an exam preparation platform.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Students take exams on the learner mobile app or the web, with progress saved even if the connection wobbles.

Can we import our question bank?

Yes. Existing questions can be imported and organised by subject, topic and difficulty. For lighter practice, see the quiz platform.
