What exams can it prepare for?

Any structured exam, such as government, police, banking, entrance and competitive exams. You bring the syllabus, question bank and lessons, and we shape the platform around them.

Does it include a mock test series?

Yes. A full mock series with timed, sectioned papers, negative marking, solutions and ranking runs on a schedule.

Can it push daily current affairs?

Yes. Daily current-affairs quizzes and lessons keep aspirants consistent, nudged over WhatsApp and Telegram.

Is there doubt solving?

Yes. A doubt forum and live doubt classes let students get answers, so they do not get stuck alone.

Is this proven for exam prep?

Yes. Elevate Aura's own gamified government exam-prep app runs on this platform and is live on Google Play with automated daily practice.
