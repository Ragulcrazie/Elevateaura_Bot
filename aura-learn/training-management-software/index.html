Common questions

Training management, answered.

Can I assign training to specific teams?

Yes. Assign programmes to individuals, roles or teams, with deadlines, so the right people get the right training automatically.

Can managers see who has completed it?

Yes. A manager dashboard shows completion and assessment results by person and team, so compliance is visible at a glance.

Does it issue certificates?

Yes. On passing an assessment, a certificate is generated automatically, which is useful for compliance and audits.

Can field staff train on mobile?

Yes. Field and service staff complete modules on their phones, with offline access. See learning app development.

Can we set recurring or mandatory training?

Yes. Mandatory and recurring training can be scheduled with reminders, so certifications are renewed on time.
